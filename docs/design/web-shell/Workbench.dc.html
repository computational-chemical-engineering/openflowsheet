EDUCATION · DEGREES OF FREEDOM
A well-posed steady-state model is square: every free variable is fixed by exactly one equation. The structural check pairs equations with variables (a matching). Here all 47 variables found a partner, but equations remain with nothing left to determine, so they either repeat information or contradict it. That is over-specification. Removing one specification on U-HEAT, such as a feed flow or temperature that is also implied elsewhere, restores a square system.
