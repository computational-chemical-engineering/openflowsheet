## 2. The evidence (all measured; anchors to read)

- **T04 spec** `docs/derivations/T04-globalization-spec.md` **§17 F9, F10, §15 Q8** (read them):
  fresh-flash tolerances (`τ_flow = 3.1e-8 mol/s`, `τ_E = 1.01e-3 W`) were argued from floors at
  deeply converged states, not from the row tolerance (`τ_eq = 9.3e-8 (mol/s)²`); a lifted split
  whose rows sit at `e` differs from the fresh flash by `B⁻¹e` (`B` the unit's lifted block at fixed
  `(n,T,P)`); worst case at the 355 K root 24.3 τ_flow on `S3.V`, 21.0 τ_E on the heater balance;
  `SYN-001-A02-355` from 358 K: rows ≤ 0.082 τ, independent split 1.092 τ_flow → `FAILED`,
  `false_success_detected` (false alarm); family scan: 15 of 179 correct roots would be `FAILED`,
  9 inside ADR 0007 D2.4's band; flash outlets (F10) 179 clear. **T04 Q8's recommended default:
  first-order propagation** — the independent-split tolerance of component `i` becomes
  `τ_flow + Σ_j |(B⁻¹)_{v_i,e_j}| τ_eq`, the fresh-flash balance's `τ_E + Σ_j |Σ_i Δh_i (B⁻¹)_{v_i,e_j}| τ_eq`,
  `B` the verifier's own lifted block at `x_final` (no solver quantity), recorded on the
  `CheckResult`; the trivial root still caught (0.304 mol/s against ~1e-6).
- **T05b, measured (`docs/T05_DECISIONS.md`, entries W6 and W7):** DZ-3 (B16) converges under v2 in
  one attempt (S4.T within 8.1e-7 K), Newton stops at row tolerance (largest row 0.2 τ), and the
  certificate is `FAILED` on `independent_split.U-PHF.S1.total` −9.1e-8 mol/s (2.9 τ) and
  `energy_balance.U-PHF`/envelope −1.33e-3 W (1.3 τ_E) — `xfail(strict=True)` at
  `tests/test_t05b_zero_flow.py`. NP-G's recorded solution-error bound 7.0e-8 > spec §13's `1e-8`
  (others ≤ 3.8e-10). T05b spec §13 assumed roundoff convergence.
- The T05b verifier already judges a *temperature-degenerate* split by the stored split, not a
  fresh flash (ADR 0012 D7) — read `verify/table.py`, `verify/saturation.py`, `verify/checks.py`
  (`:299`, `:654`), `verify/certificate.py`, `verify/regularity.py` for the current machinery.
