## Decision

- **D1 — Claim set.** `v0.3-gate-v1` covers `0.3.0`, `0.3.0bN` and `0.3.0rcN`. It claims V11–V20, W21–W26 and X31,
  seventeen gates, each judged at the candidate C. **W27 is not claimed.** It is printed
  `not claimed (v0.3-gate-v1)` and never read (spec §2.1). The reason is a measurement. At `a1b50ccf`, W27's version 1
  snapshot refuses the S11 build with `registry_snapshot: unsupported(provider 'iapws95-v1': no registered records and
  phases, W27-R63)`, so 0.2.0's campaign cannot be carried to any 0.3.0 candidate. Claiming W27 takes a new paid campaign
  under registration version 3 (R-808). That is Frank's alternative in the `Claim set` row.
- **D2 — Judgement.** ADR 0042 D2 applies, with this ADR in place of ADR 0042. `BLOCKED` blocks the tag. A `FAIL` is
  releasable only if its clause is in the D3 table below, with Frank's acceptance dated on or after the 0.3.0 verdict's
  `Judged` date. Evidence about another commit reaches C only through the RC job (spec §4). No acceptance from 0.2.0
  is carried.
- **D3 — Accepted failures for 0.3.0.** The table below is filled by Frank after the verdict. An empty table means none
  is accepted. X31 (c) is pre-registered as a `FAIL` unless S09's three-tool outcome reads "met with two references".
  The recorded outcome is "met where available, 3 of 4" (`docs/reviews/S09-verdicts.md` verdict 3), so X31 (c) needs
  its row (spec §3.2).
- **D4 — Script.** `scripts/v0_3_gate.py` holds one registry entry, `v0.3-gate-v1` (spec §2.2), and is what
  `release.yml` resolves for the 0.3 line. It reuses `scripts/v0_2_gate.py`'s engine. The two v0.2 entries and their
  test modules keep their behaviour byte for byte, except M07a.A10's two appended pairs (D5).
- **D5 — Pin and working form (R-563).** The cut commit K₃ declares `0.3.0` and pins the envelope as
  `v0.3-envelope-1`, `release: 0.3.0`. After the tag, `main` carries `0.4.0.dev0`, and the envelope returns to the
  existing working form `v0.2-envelope-dev`, `release: 0.2.0.dev0`. R-563 already ruled that form a fixed token, not a
  version label. `test_a10_…`'s loop gains exactly the two pairs `A10_PINS_0_3_0` (spec §5.2). This departs from
  D1-O2's version-free default. Under D1-O2, each version bump on `main` would add another A10 pair, so the recurrence
  it meant to stop would grow (spec §5.3). The alternative is Frank's (N-7).
- **D6 — ADR acceptance.** ADRs 0046–0052 become Accepted by the build lane at WO-4. Each needs its own condition shown
  by a merged `tested` manifest and a review record (spec §6). For S11 and E01, the G8 condition (the full gate and CI
  on both architectures) is shown by the merge records and again by C's RC steps 1 and 17. ADR 0043 is not required and
  stays Proposed. Its acceptance waits on L01-Q1 (W27 Amendment 3, not done), and nothing in 0.3.0 binds `pr-lib-v1`.
- **D7 — The tag is Frank's** (ADR 0021 D5). So are the dispatch and the PyPI approval.

## Alternatives rejected

- *Claim W27 by carrying 0.2.0's campaign.* The carry needs equal coverage rows at C_W and C. The version 1 snapshot
  cannot be built at the S11 build (measured above), and version 3 classifies a different route set (R-808). The carry
  is therefore impossible, not merely unlikely.
- *New gate ids for the property, binding and assessment packages.* That would re-plan plan §5's gates. The packages'
  evidence reaches the gate through items 8–9, because every v0.3 package has a `tested` manifest at an ancestor of C
  (spec §2.2 `packages`). It also reaches the gate through RC step 17 and the release notes. X31 is the plan's one v0.3
  gate.
- *Read X31 "met where available" as a `PASS` with a limitation.* S03 Q-A2-4 and the S09 verdict both leave the
  acceptance of an unmet reference clause to Frank. A D3 row is the existing, dated way to record that acceptance.
- *Add `v0.3-gate-v1` to `scripts/v0_2_gate.py`'s registry.* `release.yml` resolves the gate by its minor line
  (`scripts/v0_3_gate.py`). Adding the entry also moves M07.A30 (Table T-V) and A32 (two entries), which are registered
  facts of the released 0.2.0.
- *Carry the 0.2.0 journey (W25) to C.* `carry.journey_files` includes `src/openflowsheet/**`, which S01–S12 changed.
  The registered rule (M07 §4.6) therefore requires a re-run, which needs workstation compute but no money.

  NOTICE gains the Wilson and IPCC attribution blocks.
