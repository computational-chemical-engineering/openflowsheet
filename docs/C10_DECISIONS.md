# C10 — build decisions (IAPWS-95)

Append-only. One line per choice: `DECISION: <question> → <choice>. Alternative: <x>. Reversible by: <how>.`

Specifier, 2026-10-22 (design lane; first pass, stopped at the context cap before the spec was written; see `docs/briefs/C10-handoff.md`).

- DECISION: package id for the manifest → `S05` (`evidence/S05/`, checks `S05.Axx`; branch stays `wp/C10` until the build lane renames it, as L01→S04 under R-565). Alternative: keep `C10` (a C-item, so no manifest under R-565). Reversible by: renaming before the first manifest is written.
- DECISION: independent expectations → iapws.org is refused by the session proxy (CONNECT 403, 2026-10-22), so R6-95(2018) Tables 6–8 cannot be read. Registered values come from an mpmath 50-digit generator (`docs/derivations/scripts/s05_reference.py`) over coefficients transcribed from CoolProp 8.0.0 (`get_fluid_param_string('Water','JSON')`, 76 556 chars, SHA-256 `a10a0da6c55e623cccc1f56a70cb348c772d8fc1710a6a142b459abb0dec4586`). Each value is admitted only when CoolProp 8.0.0 and `iapws` 1.5.5 (PyPI; `iapws/iapws95.py` SHA-256 `89edb7c0e3b77533319d253819cdb4c1da50775d2e7b324249574607d6790520`) both agree with it. Comparison with the printed tables is an open *needs a fact* item. Alternative: wait for the release PDF. Reversible by: adding release-table assertions once the tables have been read.
- DECISION: generator layout → `s05_reference.py` (project venv, mpmath only, reads the committed coefficient JSON, `--check`) plus `s05_crosscheck.py` (runs only in a scratch venv with CoolProp 8.0.0 and iapws 1.5.5; writes `s05_crosscheck.json` with versions and hashes, and its own `--check`). `s05_reference.py` refuses to emit if the committed cross-check disagrees beyond its tolerances, so the claim that the implementations agree can be executed in the project venv. Alternative: one script that imports CoolProp, which would put CoolProp in the project venv (forbidden). Reversible by: merging the two scripts if the packages are ever vendored.
- DECISION: coefficient set → the R6-95 revised release (2016/2018). Its ideal-gas coefficients are n°1 = −8.3204464837497 and n°2 = 6.6832105275932, and both sources carry these values. R = 0.46151805 kJ/(kg·K) and M = 18.015268 g/mol, so the molar R is 8.314371357587 J/(mol·K): the IAPWS value, not CODATA's 8.31446261815324. Molar quantities are the specific values × M. Alternative: CODATA R, which departs from the formulation. Reversible by: a new provider id.
- DECISION: reference convention → a new `IAPWS95-ref-v1`, the formulation's own datum (u = 0 and s = 0 for the saturated liquid at the triple point, 273.16 K). It is not a formation datum and not in ADR 0011 D2's reaction-consistent set. Alternative: shift so that the ideal gas at 298.15 K has h = Δ_fH°. That was rejected because pure water has no reaction and ADR 0001 D5.2 already forbids mixing streams across providers. Reversible by: a later ADR adding the offset. FINDING (not written by C10): when the ADR is accepted, ADR 0001 D5's list and `interfaces-frozen.md` need a new line, as ADR 0026 did for `PR-C1-ref-v1`.
- DECISION: domain → T ∈ [273.16 K, 1273.15 K] and P ∈ [1.0e2 Pa, 1.0e8 Pa]. Leaving it gives `out_of_domain`. Ice is excluded because T ≥ T_t and P ≤ 100 MPa < 209.9 MPa (ice Ih/III). Alternative: the formulation's full range up to 1 GPa. Reversible by: widening the box, with new assertions.
- DECISION: phase rule →
  - T ≥ T_c: the single fluid is reported as VAPOR, and a LIQUID request is refused `phase_not_stable`.
  - T ≤ T_σ,max = 647.0 K: with s = ln(P/p_σ(T)), LIQUID is answered iff s ≥ −ε_σ and VAPOR iff s ≤ +ε_σ, with ε_σ = 1e-6. Inside the band both phases are answered, each on its own branch root, so a round trip through T_σ(P) works.
  - T ∈ (647.0 K, T_c): P ≥ p_c gives LIQUID and P < 22.0 MPa gives VAPOR.
  - The box T ∈ (647.0, 648.0) K × P ∈ [22.0, 22.2] MPa is refused `near_critical`.
  The generator must check that p_σ(647.0 K) > 22.0 MPa, that the critical point is inside the box, and that the band's metastable roots exist at 647.0 K. Alternative: label by density (LIQUID iff ρ > ρ_c), or answer metastable roots out to the spinodal (the pr_c1 L2 precedent). Reversible by: an amendment to the spec, since only the refusal set moves.
- DECISION: properties → per phase: `h`, `s`, `v`, `Z`, `cp`, `lnphi_H2O` (molar SI), with derivatives in T, P and `n_H2O`. The n-derivatives are exactly 0.0, because the properties are intensive for one component. Saturation: `Psat`(T) and `Tsat`(P), answered without a phase-stability check, and refused `no_saturation` outside [273.16 K, 647.0 K] (P outside [p_t, p_σ(647.0 K)]). `Psat` carries d/dT (Clausius–Clapeyron), and its d/dP is exactly 0.0. There is no `lnK`; that belongs to the binding package. Alternative: also `g`, `cv` and `w`. Reversible by: adding ids (additive).
- DECISION: flash → `TP` only. Outside the band it answers single-phase (vapour fraction 1.0 or 0.0; the other outlet is the zero stream). Inside the band it is refused `tp_flash_degenerate` (`unsupported`), because the split of a pure substance at fixed (T, P) is undetermined there. `near_critical` is refused. Alternative: refuse every flash, as pr-lib-v1 does. Reversible by: dropping `TP` from `flashes`.
- DECISION: density root → bracketed, safeguarded Newton. The vapour root lies in (0, ρ''(T)], extended by the band. The liquid root lies in [ρ'(T), 1250 kg/m³]. For T > 647.0 K the bracket is (0, 1250]. The generator asserts (∂p/∂ρ)_T > 0 on every bracket over a stated grid. Alternative: unbracketed Newton from an ideal-gas or IF97 seed. Reversible by: an amendment to the spec.
- DECISION: rights → IAPWS terms are `unresolved` (the site was refused). CoolProp is MIT, read from its dist metadata. `iapws` is GPL-3 and is used only as an executed comparison: nothing is copied or shipped. The coefficient file must not ship in a wheel until the IAPWS terms are read (L01-Q4 by analogy). Alternative: none. Reversible by: a rights row marked `vetted`.

Specifier, 2026-10-22, second pass (spec, generators, ADR 0045, register R-585…R-594).

- DECISION (amends "density root"): brackets for T ∈ (647.0 K, T_c) → bound both branches by the saturated densities at T (an internal Maxwell solve), as below 647.0 K; (0, 1250] only for T ≥ T_c. Reason: an isotherm below T_c crosses the two-phase dome, where (∂p/∂ρ)_T ≤ 0, so the first pass's "(0, 1250] for T > 647.0 K" cannot satisfy its own monotonicity claim. The generator now claims monotonicity on the band-extended branch brackets at 23 subcritical temperatures up to 647.05 K, and on (0, 1250] at 8 temperatures ≥ 647.1 K (spec §4.4, R-591). Alternative: split the brackets at 22.0/22.2 MPa. Reversible by: an amendment to the spec.
- DECISION: metastable band roots → found by Newton from the saturated density of that phase, and accepted only on that phase's side of ρ_c; otherwise `density_not_converged`. Alternative: compute the band-edge roots as bracket ends first. Reversible by: an amendment to the spec.
- DECISION: derivative method → one order-3 bivariate jet of φ (`phi_jet`, a tested module-level function); at δ = 1 the |δ−1|^{10/3} and |δ−1|^7 factors are zero jets. The generator differentiates numerically (mp.diff, and 110-digit central differences with h = 1e-45·x), so its expectations are independent of the jet. Alternative: hand-written Table-5-style partials (also permitted if they pass A03/A04). Reversible by: none needed; only the tests judge.
- DECISION: Tsat admission bound → 1e-7 relative, not 1e-9. iapws 1.5.5's Tsat inverse solve is loose (584.1471409 K against CoolProp's 584.1471470 K at 10 MPa), while its Psat(T) agrees to about 1e-14, so the curve itself is admitted tightly through Psat. Alternative: drop iapws for Tsat. Reversible by: tightening if iapws is replaced.
- DECISION: saturation-only requests → a request made only of `Psat` and `Tsat` skips the phase check, and its `phase_signature` echoes the request's phase. Mixed requests take the phase rule. Alternative: a separate saturation entry point, which would be an interface change. Reversible by: an amendment to the spec.

Build lane (`opus-engineer`), WO-S05-1, 2026-10-22.

- DECISION: Newton convergence when the 4ε relative step is unreachable → converged when the relative step is ≤ 4ε, **or** when, once a step has fallen below √ε = 2⁻²⁶, the next step does not shrink (the residuals are at their rounding floor; spec §3.5's second clause). Applied to the Maxwell, density and Tsat loops (`_converged`). Measured: with the literal rule Maxwell never converges at 300, 450, 647.0 or 647.05 K; its steps stall at 2e-16–1.6e-14 relative (273.16 K, 450 K) and 1e-11–5e-11 at 647.0 K, where the problem's conditioning grows; with this rule it converges in 5–7 iterations at every §5.3 temperature and at 647.05/647.09 K. Alternative: the literal rule (every T ≤ 647.0 K state `not_converged`), or a fixed floor tolerance per region. Reversible by: reverting the commit that introduces `_converged`.
- QUESTION (design lane, Q-floor): spec §3.5/§4.4 say "relative steps ≤ 4ε and the residuals at their floor". Is the √ε-onset stagnation test above an acceptable reading, or should the spec state a floor estimate (e.g. from Σ|terms|)? What would decide it: a ruling on the text; the measured stall levels are above.
- DECISION: which side gives p_σ → p(ρ'', T), the vapour's, not spec §3.5's p(ρ', T). Both equal p_σ at the root; the liquid's is ill-conditioned at low T (Z' ≈ 4.9e-6 at 273.16 K is 1 + δφʳ_δ with δφʳ_δ ≈ −1, and (ρ/p)(∂p/∂ρ) ≈ 3e6 amplifies the converged ρ''s 2e-15). Measured against ref `saturation.*.Psat`: liquid side 5.4e-8 (273.16 K), 1.9e-8 (275 K), 3e-13 (450 K), 7e-14 (647.0 K); vapour side ≤ 1.6e-14 at 273.16–625 K and 7.3e-14 at 647.0 K. A08's bound is 2e-9, so the literal text fails A08 at 273.16 and 275 K. Alternative: p(ρ', T) as written. Reversible by: reverting the commit that switches the side.
- QUESTION (design lane): amend spec §3.5's "p_σ = p(ρ', T)" to the vapour side (the measurement above)? What would decide it: a ruling; the numbers are above.
- DECISION: the value of Z (and the ln Z inside `lnphi_H2O`) at a solved (T, P) root → P/(ρ R_s T), not spec §3.1's 1 + δφʳ_δ; the partials of Z stay the jet's. The two are the same function on the branch at the root; the closed form is a difference of O(1) terms when Z ≪ 1. Measured with 1 + δφʳ_δ: |lnφ_L − lnφ_V| at P = Psat is 5.4e-8 (273.16 K) and 1.9e-8 (275 K) against A10's 1e-8, and Z at `L_ambient` is off ref by 8.9e-11 relative; with the quotient A10 passes at all five temperatures and every A05 Z is within 1e-11. The (T, ρ) path (`phi_states`, saturation slope) keeps 1 + δφʳ_δ. Alternative: the closed form as written (A10 fails at 273.16 and 275 K). Reversible by: reverting the commit that adds `root_pressure`.
- QUESTION (design lane): amend spec §3.1 to state that at a solved root Z is evaluated as P/(ρ R_s T) (with the measurement above)?
- DECISION: F-2's two T08 edits (`scanned_providers` gains `iapws95-v1`; `LIBRARY_ONLY_PROVIDERS["iapws95-v1"] = "S05"`) → made in WO-S05-1, not WO-S05-2, because the new module's `PROVIDER_ID` breaks T08.A20 the moment it exists (R-568). Consequence: `test_c3_*` (6 tests) fail on condition (e) only, "iapws95-v1: S05 has no U02 item in the harvest", until WO-S05-2 writes `evidence/S05/<commit>/manifest.json` with a U02 item whose note names `` `iapws95-v1` `` and library-only, and the envelope's harvest takes it (A25). Alternative: leave the module unregistered (A20 and C3 fail on condition (b) instead). Reversible by: reverting the two lines in `tests/test_t08_w2_support_envelope.py`.
- MEASURED (Q-6): A17's floor, max |f(x+h) − f(x−h) − 2h f′| / max(|f(x±h)|, s_f) with h = 1e-6·x, over the §5.2 states whose stencil lies inside the box. Away from NC: h 2.5e-13 (T) / 2.3e-14 (P); s 3.6e-14 / 1.4e-15; v 5.7e-15 / 4.6e-15; Z 2.6e-15 / 1.8e-16; cp 7.1e-14 / 5.8e-14; lnφ 6.3e-14 / 7.5e-15, all below 1e-11. At NC in T: cp 1.25e-9 (NC_vapour), 4.11e-10 (NC_liquid), v 3.6e-11, Z 1.2e-11, h 1.1e-11; in P ≤ 3.6e-12. The NC misses are truncation: they fall 8× per halving of h (cp NC_vapour 1.25e-9, 1.56e-10, 1.95e-11 at h/x = 1e-6, 5e-7, 2.5e-7), and A06 holds there at 1e-8.
- QUESTION (design lane, Q-6): A17 cannot be met as written at (a) NC in T (truncation above, 2 cases), (b) the four corner states, whose central step leaves the domain box (8 cases), and (c) 11 of the 16 band-probe (phase, axis) cases, whose step leaves the ε_σ band (a T step moves ln(P/p_σ) by ≈ 1e-5 ≫ ε_σ; a P step by exactly ε_σ). All 21 are strict xfails with their reasons in `tests/test_s05_iapws95.py`, nothing is dropped. Options: a smaller NC step (h/x = 2.5e-7 passes), one-sided stencils at the box edges, and ε_σ-scaled steps for band probes. What would decide it: a ruling on spec §6.3.
- MEASURED (Q-3): median `evaluate_phase` at `L_ambient` (all six properties, T/P/n derivatives, 20 calls) = 2.6 ms without a cache (the Maxwell solve is about 1.2 ms of it, 6 iterations at 450 K); 0.75 ms once T is cached. One φ jet costs about 0.1 ms in pure Python.
- DECISION: Q-3's default branch for > 1 ms → an exact-double-T `functools.lru_cache(maxsize=1024)` on `_saturation` (no quantization; a pure function of T and the coefficient file; T07 allowlist entry, pinned `cache` count 31 → 32). A fresh T still pays the solve. Alternative: no cache (2.6 ms per call), or a faster jet (vectorized terms). Reversible by: reverting the commit that adds `_SATURATION_CACHE_SIZE`.
