## 5. Alternatives considered and rejected

1. **B23 or (b1) `BLOCKED` because the harness might be manufacturing the `MID` reports.** Rejected.
   - Every defect B21 and Am2.2 register as blocking is zero.
   - The classes recompute from the final states against the spec's own roots with 0 mismatches.
   - The `MID` states lie within 6.5e-11 of the saddle and carry `VERIFIED` certificates.
   - The harness makes the product's revision-path calls (bind → plan → execute → verify). The design lane reviewed
     it before any result and found no must-fix.
   - Three machines agree class for class.
2. **(b1) `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` because B20's manifest clause is outstanding.** Rejected (§3.1). It is a (b2)
   clause, and the protection it gives cannot turn a FAIL into an artifact.
3. **Exempting `MID` starts that lie near the separatrix, or where Newton also reports `MID`.** Rejected. §A4.1
   registered the clause over all 441 starts because none lies within 4.898e-3 of the manifold. Dropping cases is
   forbidden (CLAUDE.md), and 144 of the 149 lie in the physical HIGH basin anyway.
4. **Treating PTC's `ptc_steps` exhaustion as `BLOCKED`.** Rejected. Am2.2 names `property_calls` only. The 200-step
   cap is the arm's registered constant, and the run changes no verdict.
5. **Judging on `ref-x86-64` alone.** Rejected by Q-A2 (each registered platform must pass). It is moot, because
   the platforms are identical.
6. **Reading (b1) as met because containment holds, or because `|S_ptc| > |S_newton|` (291 against 279).**
   Rejected. (b1) is a conjunction, and net counts are reported, not judged.
7. **Reading the saddle clause comparatively ("PTC reports `MID` no more often than Newton": 149 < 161).**
   Rejected. That is a criterion changed after the result (R-118; R-123's watch-for).
8. **Re-running with a capped Δτ, different SER constants, or a changed PTC core, and judging that instead.**
   Rejected as a way to reverse this verdict. It changes a policy hash or the method on T04's registered path, so it
   would be a new registration of a new method, not a re-judgement of PTC-R1.
9. **Waiting for the in-progress push CI before judging.** Rejected. The route-(a) evidence exists at the run commit
   itself (§3.6). The (b1) verdict is decided by the saddle clause, and route (a) does not move it.

## 6. What would reopen this verdict

A new explicit decision is needed in every case. None of the following deletes this result.

- **Evidence that the committed results are not the registered measurement.** Examples: `case.json` not recomputing
  at a run commit; a `case_sha256` mismatch; a solve-path code difference between `C_case` and the run commits that
  §1 missed. The outcome would be that B21–B23 become `BLOCKED` and PTC-R1 must be re-run. It would not be a PASS.
- **A defect in the PTC core or the SER controller, shown against an independent expectation.** An example is a
  failing T04 A19–A22 recount, or an SER update that differs from T04 §7.7 as registered. It must not come from
  searching for a change that moves these 149 starts. It would reopen only the question of whether this run
  measured the registered method, by an ADR 0023/T04 amendment. The consequence would be a new comparison, reported
  beside this one.
- **Nothing in the numbers themselves.** The criterion is exact set membership. The margin is 149 starts against 0
  allowed, the distances are 10⁷ inside the radius, and three machines agree.
- **For V14 (b) as a release clause:** only a new, preregistered case or method (v0.2) with its own verdict, or
  Frank's acceptance of the FAIL into ADR 0021's D3.

## 7. What this verdict does not establish

- **Not that residence-time PTC cannot qualify.** It measures T04 §7.7's registered constants (τ₀ = θ = 1 s,
  γ ∈ [0.2, 2], 200 steps, one Newton polish) under `T06-revision-v2`. It says nothing about another controller,
  another cap, or another class.
- **Not why PTC ends at the saddle.** The observations are *consistent with* §A3.6's named risk, and nothing more:
  - with growth capped at 2 per step from τ₀ = 1 s, Δτ can first exceed `2/s₊ = 8.317 s` at the fourth step, after
    which a step contracts toward the saddle;
  - the 149 runs converge in 8–11 steps.

  Δτ histories were not recorded, so the mechanism is a hypothesis.
  - The brief's follow-up (method or harness?): the harness's *classification* is ruled out as the source (§5.1).
  - What remains open is whether the reports come from the SER controller, from the product path's scaling, or from
    the polish.
  - The smallest measurement that would settle it is diagnostic only, not a criterion: per-step Δτ and distance to
    MID for the 149 runs. The hypothesis holds if Δτ passes 2/s₊ before each iterate enters the saddle's
    neighbourhood.
- **Not a statement about Newton.** Its 161 `MID` reports are not judged by any registered criterion.
- **Not validation.** SYN-001 and the kinetic law are synthetic (§D; L-CSTR-1). Certificates promise residual
  accuracy, not solution accuracy (ADR 0007 F3).
- **Not portability beyond the three machines named in §1.**
