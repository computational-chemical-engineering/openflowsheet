## 2. The verdict, in one paragraph

The three limitations are one fact: a PH-type outlet is determined by `(n, H, P)`, and its temperature is a poor coordinate — undefined at zero flow, degenerate for one component, ill-conditioned near purity (the two-phase band of 2 mol/s of B with a 1e-12 mol/s trace of A is 2.14e-11 K wide and carries 60 000 W). T05 parametrized everything by temperature: the kernel bisected on it, the contract classified by the provider's TP flash at it, the start re-flashed at it, and the verifier recomputed the split from it. T05b puts the vapour fraction and the enthalpy where the temperature was, wherever the temperature fails, and nowhere else: the kernel falls back from its temperature route to a **band route** in the vapour fraction when the temperature route's answer does not satisfy the closure's own rows (§5); under a second contract literal, a PH-type split is classified by its **PH closure** at its own enthalpy, which on SYN-001 never reports the far regime (§6); a lifted split with an exactly dormant feed runs a **`ZERO_FLOW` regime** that pins its lifted flows and replaces its temperature-free energy row by the dormancy label (§7); and the verifier judges a **temperature-degenerate** split — one whose bubble and dew points both lie within the registered temperature tolerance of its temperature — by the split the state carries, because its fresh flash cannot recompute it (§9). Every registered result is bitwise unchanged: every change is either behind the new literal, on a path no registered state reaches (degeneracy margins ≥ 14 K), or a bitwise identity on the registered path (the temperature route's answer and the closure's split are the same bits). A dormant **non-lifted** outlet whose temperature no row reads (the pump's, an exchanger side's, the K02 mixer's) takes the same swap of its energy row for a label row without any regime: the attempt signature carries an item `[<U>.<port>, ZERO_FLOW]` only while that outlet's inlet is exactly dormant, so no plan, no `signature_units` and no flowing signature changes, and the `t05` identity does not move (§7.6–§7.10). What remains is one band of near-pure EO certification that belongs to T04 F9 (§17).

## 4. The saturation band (derivation)

### 4.1 Definitions

For a flowing `n` at pressure `P` (total `N > 0`, fractions `z_i = n_i/N`, `K_i = exp(ln K_i(T, P))` from the provider), the **temperature form of Rachford–Rice** is

    g(T; β) = Σ_{i: n_i > 0} z_i (K_i(T) − 1) / (1 + β (K_i(T) − 1)),   β ∈ [0, 1].

`g(T; 0) = Σ z_i K_i − 1` is the bubble function and `g(T; 1) = 1 − Σ z_i / K_i` the dew function (T05 §4.4's classification tests, as equalities). `T(β)` is the root of `g(·; β)` on the domain; the **band** is `[T_b, T_d] = [T(0), T(1)]`. The **band split** at `β` and temperature `T` is

    D_i = 1 + β (K_i(T) − 1),   v_i = n_i β K_i / D_i,   l_i = n_i (1 − β) / D_i,

both nonnegative by construction (products and quotients of nonnegatives; no subtraction), with `v_i + l_i = n_i` in exact arithmetic and to rounding in doubles; an absent component has `v_i = l_i = 0` exactly. Its enthalpy is `H(β) = Σ v_i h_i^V(T(β), P) + Σ l_i h_i^L(T(β), P)`.

### 4.2 Monotonicity and the near-pure band

- `∂g/∂T = Σ z_i K_i (∂ ln K_i/∂T) / D_i² > 0`, because `∂ ln K_i/∂T = Δh_i/(R T²) > 0` on SYN-001's domain (T05 §4.2). So `T(β)` is unique when it exists, and bisection finds it.
- `∂g/∂β = −Σ z_i (K_i − 1)² / D_i² ≤ 0`, so `dT/dβ = −(∂g/∂β)/(∂g/∂T) ≥ 0`: the band's temperature rises with the vapour fraction.
- `dH/dβ ≥ Σ_i Δh_i ∂v_i/∂β|_T = Σ_i Δh_i n_i K_i / D_i² > 0`, since `T(β)` rising only adds sensible and `K`-driven terms of the same sign (T05 §4.2's argument). **`H(β)` is strictly increasing** with slope at least `min_i Δh_i · Σ_i n_i K_i / D_i²` — for 2 mol/s of B about 60 000 W per unit `β`, so one ulp of `β` near ½ moves `H` by ~7e-12 W, nine decades under `τ_E`. *Generator-checked* on 21-point `β` grids for all eight grid compositions.
- **The band's width, to first order in the traces.** With `k` the major component and `K_i` at `T_sat,k(P)`,

      T_b − T_sat,k ≈ −Σ_{i≠k} z_i (K_i − 1) / (∂ ln K_k/∂T),
      T_d − T_sat,k ≈ −Σ_{i≠k} z_i (1 − 1/K_i) / (∂ ln K_k/∂T),
      T_d − T_b    ≈  Σ_{i≠k} z_i (K_i − 1)² / K_i / (∂ ln K_k/∂T).

  **Sanity values** (B at `P_r`, `T_sat = 360 K` exactly, `∂ ln K_B/∂T = 0.027 840 8 K⁻¹`, `K_A = 2.840 644`, `K_C = 0.310 580`; trace `ε` in 2 mol/s of B, `z = ε/(2 + ε)`): light trace A — `T_b − 360 ≈ −33.06 ε`, `T_d − 360 ≈ −11.64 ε`, width `21.42 ε` K per mol/s; heavy trace C — `+12.38 ε`, `+39.87 ε`, width `27.48 ε`. At `ε = 1e-12`: width 2.142e-11 K (A) and 2.748e-11 K (C) — 380 and 480 doubles, while the latent jump is 60 000 W. *Generator-checked* against the 40-digit bisection: the width is linear in the trace to 1e-4 relative for `ε ≤ 1e-7`.
- **The pure limit.** One flowing component: `g(T; β) = (K_k − 1)/D_k` has the root `T_sat` for every `β`; the band is the point `T_sat` and `H(β)` is the lever rule, affine in `β`. T05 §4.4's saturation route is the band's closed form. At `ε = 1e-12` the 40-digit solution lies within 4e-11 K of 360 K and its vapour flow within 1e-11 mol/s of `2φ` (claim `pure_limit`).

### 4.3 The band solve

On `[T_b, T_d]`, `Ḣ_TP(T)` and `H(β)` describe the same closure: `H(β(T)) = Ḣ_TP(T)` where the TP flash is two-phase. So solving `H(β) = H*` in `β` and `Ḣ_TP(T) = H*` in `T` give the same answer in exact arithmetic. *Generator-checked:* at all 40 grid states the 40-digit `β`-bisection and T05's 40-digit `T`-bisection agree to 1e-25 in `T` and in every `v_i`. The parametrizations differ only in conditioning: `H` changes by `nΔh` across the band in `β` (width 1) and across 2e-11 K in `T`.

### 4.4 Temperature-degenerate streams (definition)

A flowing stream or split feed `(n, T, P)` is **temperature-degenerate** iff both `T_b(n, P)` and `T_d(n, P)` exist in the domain and

    δ(n, T, P) := max(|T − T_b|, |T − T_d|) ≤ τ_T = 1e-6 K.

`δ` is the **degeneracy distance**. It is defined by bisection (§5.2's rule, own implementation in each consumer), carries no new threshold — `τ_T` is the resolution a temperature is registered at (ADR 0001 D6) — and means: *within the temperature tolerance, every vapour fraction is consistent with this stream's temperature*. For one flowing component it is `|T − T_sat|`. For a near-pure feed it requires a band narrower than `τ_T` (any `T` inside) or `2τ_T` (`T` mid-band): with the widths of §4.2, a trace below about 4.7e-8 mol/s of A or 3.6e-8 mol/s of C in 2 mol/s of B. Every registered state is far from it: *generator-checked* minimum over C1–C3's streams and splits and SYN-001's nominal root **17.53 K** (SYN-001's flash split at 360 K), and over T05's five registered R-007 refusals **14.27 K**.

## 5. The PH kernel (normative; amends T05 §4.4; ADR 0011 D1, ADR 0012 D2)

### 5.1 Order

`ph_state(provider, n, P, H*)` keeps T05 §4.4 steps 1–4 and step 6, and the saturation route (step 2), unchanged. The new order is F1 of §3.3:

1. Step 1 (pressure) — unchanged.
2. **Saturation route** when exactly one component flows — unchanged, including its half-domain restriction when `H*` is outside the jump; its answer passes §5.3.
3. **Temperature route** (steps 3–4) — unchanged; its candidate is the bracket's better end with the provider's TP split there (step 6). If its candidate passes §5.3 → `ok`, route `bracket`.
4. **Band route** (§5.2) — only when the temperature route's candidate fails §5.3 and at least two components flow. If its answer passes §5.3 → `ok`, route `band`.
5. Otherwise → `not_converged`, `ph_ill_conditioned` (the message's later lines name the route that came closest and its row ratios; the first line is the registered code, T05 §3.5). A budget exhausted in any route → `not_converged`, `ph_not_converged`, as today.

The step-3 out-of-domain tests (`ph_outside_domain(below|above)`) still come first and end the call; the band route is never entered for a target outside `[Ḣ_TP(T_min), Ḣ_TP(T_max)]`.

### 5.2 The band route (normative algorithm)

- **`T(β)`.** If `g(T_min; β) > 0`, `T(β)` is **below** the domain; if `g(T_max; β) < 0`, **above**; else bisection on `[T_min, T_max]` with T05 §4.4 step 4's rule (stop at `g = 0` exactly or adjacent doubles; return the end with the smaller `|g|`, the lower on a tie), at most `MAX_EVALUATIONS = 200` evaluations of `g` per call, the two ends included; exhaustion → `ph_not_converged`.
- **`F(β) = H(β) − H*`** with the band split at `T(β)` (§4.1); `F = −∞` when `T(β)` is below the domain and `+∞` when above (the sign oracle: a band point outside the domain has less, or more, enthalpy than the domain's end, which step 3 already compared with `H*`).
- **Ends.** Evaluate `F(0)` and `F(1)`. If `F(0) > 0` or `F(1) < 0` the target is outside the band and the band route has no answer (→ step 5). If `F(0) = 0` or `F(1) = 0`, that end is the answer.
- **Bisection on `β`.** `m = ½(lo + hi)`; stop when `F(m) = 0` exactly, when no double lies strictly between `lo` and `hi`, or when `hi − lo ≤ 2⁻⁶⁰`; at most `MAX_BAND_EVALUATIONS = 64` evaluations of `F` (both ends included); exhaustion → `ph_not_converged`. Return the end (or `m` on an exact zero) with the smaller `|F|`, the lower on a tie; an end with infinite `F` is never returned (if it would be, → step 5).
- **The answer.** `T = T(β)`, the band split, `V = Σ v_i`, reported vapour fraction `V/N`, signature `TWO_PHASE` for `0 < β < 1`, `LIQUID` at `β = 0`, `VAPOR` at `β = 1`; `evaluations` counts the `F` evaluations.
- **Why `2⁻⁶⁰`.** Bisection toward `β = 0` reaches adjacent doubles only after ~1 000 halvings. At width `2⁻⁶⁰ = 8.7e-19` the spread of any flow across the bracket is `≤ n · 8.7e-19`, below the flow floor (`1e-14` mol/s) for every `n ≤ 1e4` mol/s, and of the enthalpy `≤ n · 35 000 · 8.7e-19`, below `τ_E` for `n ≤ 3e10` mol/s. For `β ≥ 2⁻⁷` adjacent doubles come first.
- **Cost.** At most 64 × (≤ 53 `lnK` calls + 2 enthalpy calls) ≈ 3 500 provider calls; the temperature route before it costs ≤ 200 flashes. The route runs only when the temperature route has failed. W0.6 measures it against the region's property budget (10 000 by default) for the contract's use in §6.

### 5.3 Acceptance, and what `ok` guarantees (replaces T05 §4.4 step 5 and "What `ok` guarantees")

**Acceptance (every route).** A candidate `(T, v, l)` for `(n, P, H*)` is accepted iff, with `K_i` and `h_i` from the provider at `(T, P)`, `V = Σ v_i`, `L = Σ l_i`:

    |v_i + l_i − n_i|        ≤ τ_flow = 3.1e-8 mol/s      for every i        (material)
    |v_i L − K_i l_i V|      ≤ τ_eq  = 9.3e-8 (mol/s)²    for every i        (equilibrium)
    |Σ v_i h_i^V + Σ l_i h_i^L − H*| ≤ τ_E = 1.01e-3 W                       (energy)

These are T05 §4.3's EO rows for a PH-type outlet at K04 §5.1–§5.2's registered tolerances; the kernel evaluates them itself and trusts no provider tolerance.

**What `ok` guarantees.** The returned state is a root of the PH closure's EO rows within K04's registered tolerances, reached by a route that bracketed it (in `T` on the temperature route, in `β` on the band route) and whose parameter is monotone (§4.2, T05 §4.2). Consequences, each executable:

- **Energy fixes the vapour flow.** `|V − V*| ≤ τ_E / min_i Δh_i = 4.04e-8 mol/s` (with `min Δh_i = 24 990 J/mol`), the traces' share being second order (they follow `β` through the equilibrium rows). The band route does far better: *emulated* floor over the 40 grid states `5.5e-16` mol/s.
- **Equilibrium fixes the temperature given the split.** Near purity `∂g/∂T ≈ 0.028 K⁻¹`, so the equilibrium rows at `τ_eq` pin `T` to the band; *emulated* `|T − T*| ≤ 3.9e-13 K` on the route the kernel takes, `5.7e-14 K` on the band route.
- **Each trace's split is right.** The vapour fraction of every flowing component, `q_i = v_i/n_i`, within `1.2e-8` (*emulated*, chosen route) of the 40-digit value, where the smallest trace `q` on the grid is 0.033.
- The previous criterion (energy alone) accepted any split carrying `H*`. The BIASED double (§12.2) shows why that is not enough: a flash whose vapour fraction is 1e-5 off meets the energy row to 9.5e-11 W at a temperature 2.2e-4 K wrong; its equilibrium rows are 84 `τ_eq` off, and §5.3 sends the kernel to the band route, which returns PHF-1's registered answer.

**What `ok` does not guarantee.** Accuracy beyond the provider's `K` and `h` (they are taken as exact); accuracy at flows so large that `τ_E` is below the double resolution of `H` (above ~1e8 mol/s, where the code is `ph_ill_conditioned`, correctly); anything about the provider class outside SYN-001's (monotone `H`, positive `Δh`) — the JUMP double (§12.2) shows the kernel refuses, typed, when monotonicity fails.

### 5.4 Inertness on registered cases

On every registered PH-type case (PHF-1…7, VLV-1…5, RX-* in duty mode, and the traversal calls inside C1–C3) the temperature or saturation route's candidate is exactly T05's answer, so §5.3 changes the result only if that candidate fails the rows. Its rows are at the roundoff floor (T05 A06 measured the rows at the twin's solutions ≤ `2e-10 W`, `≤ 1e-14 mol/s`), so it passes and the output is bitwise T05's. **W0.2** measures every registered call's three row ratios on the implementation; B02 asserts the outputs bitwise against the W0.1 baseline.

## 6. PH-type splits in the phase contract (normative; ADR 0012 D3–D5)

### 6.1 Closure type

A lifted split (R-046's descriptor) is **PH-type** when its unit's energy row fixes its temperature: `syn001.valve`; `syn001.ph_flash`; `syn001.conversion_reactor` with `energy_specification = duty`. Every other lifted split is **TP-type**: `syn001.tp_heater`, `syn001.tp_flash`, the reactor with `outlet_temperature`. The split registry declares the type per model, by configuration for the reactor, and for a PH-type split also: its energy row id (`<U>:VLV-energy`, `<U>:PHF-duty`, `<U>:RX-duty`), its split rows (`<U>:split:<c>` heater style; `<U>:PHF-mole:<c>` products style), and its label source (the unit's inlet stream temperature). `check_agreement` (R-046) extends to these ids: each must be a row the unit authored, the energy row must read the split's temperature, and the label source must be a column.

### 6.2 The kernel of a PH-type split (v2)

Under `"T05b-phase-contract-v2"`, wherever ADR 0005 D3–D4 or T02 §6.3 asks "the kernel" for a regime or a split — the regime of a flagged trial (T03 §4.3), the new regime and split of a closure conversion (T03 §4.7 (a) and (b)), the split of a restart opening (T03 §4.5) — a PH-type split at state `x` is answered as follows (F2):

1. If the split's feed is exactly dormant: `ZERO_FLOW` (§7).
2. Else `H_split(x) = Σ_i x[v_i] h_i^V(T, P) + Σ_i x[l_i] h_i^L(T, P)`, with `T`, `P` the split's temperature and pressure columns at `x` and `h` from the region's provider; call `ph_state(provider, n_feed(x), P, H_split(x))`.
3. If it is `ok`: the regime is its signature, and an opening takes its split (`v`, `l`, both totals) **and its temperature** (the split's temperature column).
4. If it is not `ok`: the provider's TP flash of the feed's `(n, T, P)`, as in v1 (a provider refusal is handled as in v1: `invalid_trial` at a trial, `EVALUATION_ERROR` at closure).

TP-type splits are answered by the provider's TP flash, as in v1. The screen's flag (`_admissible`, K03 §8.2's one-sided test at `admissibility_epsilon`) is unchanged: it decides whether to ask, the kernel decides the answer.

*(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S3 (2) with Q-S11 (a) — not confirmed, on the review's measurement.)* In step 3, "its temperature" is written to **every temperature column of the split's streams**: heater style, the outlet's `T`; products style, **both** products' `T` (the vapour product's, which the registry names and the label row reads, and the liquid product's, which the unit's copy row `PHF-T` equates to it). The implementation wrote the vapour product's only, on the premise that `PHF-T` closes the other in one step. The premise fails: at a restart the liquid product keeps the flagged trial's temperature (392.8 K at NP-2 and 393.75 K at NP-G from a liquid-form start, 33 K above the closure's), the opening is not a consistent two-phase state, and NP-G's attempt ended `BOUND_BLOCKED` at iteration 0; with both columns set it converged in one iteration (`docs/reviews/T05b-review.md` S2, P6, *measured*). The opening **sets** these columns to the closure's `T`; it projects nothing else, and no other opening value changes. What moves: the openings of registered products-style restarts — SC-3's attempt 1 and DZ-12's attempt 1 — and so their records in the `t05b` key, which is re-registered with this reason (B22, B31). Nothing that starts at its root moves (SC-2, SC-4, NP-1…NP-G from the traversal, every DZ case but DZ-12).

*(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S10; review S1; R-053 amended.)* **The screen's regime of a flagged PH-type trial is decided by the band's end enthalpies; the full closure is kept for openings.** Asking steps 2–4 at every flagged trial and again at the candidate costs a band route (≈ 3 100 provider calls) per ask on a near-pure feed: NP-1 from a liquid-form start spent 12 598 region-side calls, over the 10 000 the region meters (*measured*, review P5). At a flagged trial (not at an opening), after step 1:

- (s1) `H_split` as in step 2. If `H_split < Ḣ_TP(n, T_min, P)` or `H_split > Ḣ_TP(n, T_max, P)` — T05 §4.4 step 3's own test, same function — `ph_state` would refuse (`ph_outside_domain`), so the screen reports the TP flash's regime at the trial, as step 4 does.
- (s2) Else, with the band's ends `T_b = T(0)`, `T_d = T(1)` (§5.2's `T(β)`, sign oracle included; one flowing component: `T_sat`), `H_0 = Σ_i n_i h_i^L(T_b, P)` and `H_1 = Σ_i n_i h_i^V(T_d)` (`H_0 = −∞` when `T_b` is below the domain, `H_1 = +∞` when `T_d` is above): report `LIQUID` if `H_split < H_0 − τ_E`, `TWO_PHASE` if `H_0 + τ_E < H_split < H_1 − τ_E`, `VAPOR` if `H_split > H_1 + τ_E`.
- (s3) Otherwise — `H_split` within `τ_E` of `H_0` or `H_1`, or any evaluation of (s1)–(s2) not `ok` — ask steps 2–4 as before, and carry the answer.

*Why it is exact.* Every liquid state of `(n, P)` has `Ḣ ≤ H_0`, every two-phase equilibrium state `H_0 ≤ Ḣ ≤ H_1` (`H(β)` increases, §4.2) and every vapour state `Ḣ ≥ H_1`, and an `ok` answer of `ph_state` carries `H_split` to within `τ_E` (§5.3). So outside the two `τ_E` zones the phase region of any `ok` answer is fixed, and (s2) names it; floating-point error in `H_0`, `H_1` is ≤ 1e-10 W against the `τ_E = 1.01e-3 W` margin. Where `ph_state` would refuse inside the domain range (a provider outside the class's continuity, flows above about 1e8 mol/s; no registered region case), the screen's regime can differ from the opening's: the opening then takes the TP fallback's answer, records `fallback(<U>, tp)`, and its regime governs the opened signature — the one sanctioned difference between a screened and an opened regime. An `ok` answer at the opening whose regime differs from (s2)'s is a defect, and the existing guard stays. Cost of (s1)–(s2): two `T(β)` bisections, two TP flashes and two enthalpy sums, about 150 provider calls. **Carrying:** an opening at the state where the screen asked steps 2–4 reuses that answer (the same call on the same inputs) instead of asking again. Inert on R0: every screened regime equals what the full closure reports at every registered trial (SC-3's is KS-1), so trajectories, events and records are unchanged; only the property counts move, which are not R0 (R-015's `UNREPRODUCIBLE_COUNTS`).

### 6.3 Why the PH closure, and why it never reports the far regime (derivation)

- **It predicts the root's regime.** A PH-type outlet's energy row fixes its enthalpy, and the closure of `(n, H, P)` is unique (T05 §4.2 with the jump, §4.3 here). At a root, `H_split = H*` and the kernel returns the root itself. A single-phase attempt's Newton step solves the energy row in the frozen phase's form, so at a trial near its end `H_split ≈ H*` and the PH regime is the root's. The TP flash instead classifies the trial's temperature, which in the wrong form lies beyond the band by the latent heat's worth.
- **It never reports the far regime on SYN-001.** Leaving `LIQUID`, a liquid-form trial has `H_split = Ḣ^L(n, T, P)` with `T` in the domain, and `max_i h_i^L(T_max, P_max) = 14 010 J/mol < min_j h_j^V(T_min) = 23 000 J/mol` (*generator-checked*, `ph_regime_never_far_on_the_domain`), so `H_split < Ḣ^V(n, T_min) ≤ H_d` and the answer is `LIQUID` or `TWO_PHASE`, never `VAPOR`; symmetrically from `VAPOR`. ADR 0005 D4's adjacency rule therefore always has an adjacent candidate for a PH-type split. P3 cycled because the TP flash reported the far regime (`VAPOR` from `LIQUID`, then back).
- **One component is not a special case.** For pure B the PH closure takes its saturation route: at SC-3's first trial (`T = 365.08 K` in liquid form, `H_split = 13 016 W`) the TP flash says `VAPOR` and the PH closure says `TWO_PHASE` with `β = 1 016/60 000` at 360 K — the root (KS-1).

### 6.4 The start of a PH-type split (D4 (b), D5)

- **The traversal seeds it from the closure (D5; independent of the literal).** `initial_state` sets a heater-style PH-type split from the split its unit's causal closure returned (the kernel's `PHState.split`, carried from the traversal), not from a TP re-flash of the outlet. On the bracket route the kernel's split *is* the provider's TP flash at `(n, T*, P)` (T05 §4.4 step 6), the same function of the same inputs as the re-flash, so every registered start is bitwise unchanged (*measured* this pass: in C1–C3 the start's splits equal the kernel's bit for bit). A products-style split is its product streams, already the closure's.
- **Under v2 it is not re-projected (D4 (b)).** T02 §6.2's projection ("every lifted split of `x⁰` is the kernel's") applies to TP-type splits only. A PH-type split opens with its start split, in the regime of its branch (§8's rule, `V = L = 0` giving `ZERO_FLOW`). Its purpose — keeping a supplied trivial split off the start — is served by the closure itself: the traversal's split is the closure's answer, never a trivial one.
- **Under v1** the projection still applies to every split, as registered.
- *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S6, ratifying W7's start rules as implemented.)* **Under v2, §7.1's selection applies at the start as at every opening.** A lifted split whose feed is exactly dormant at `x⁰` opens in `ZERO_FLOW` whatever its closure type; its lifted flows are set to `+0.0` (a nonzero supplied split is overwritten) and the change is recorded in T02 §6.2's grammar, `projected(<S>, <branch>, ZERO_FLOW)`. A split with `V = L = 0` whose feed flows opens by §7.4's leaving rule (the TP flash of its feed's `(n, T, P)`), recorded as a T02 §6.2 projection whose branch word is `zero_flow` (v2 only). An opening whose regimes then disagree with its feeds' dormancy is a defect — `RuntimeError`, message prefix `defect:` — never a typed outcome: after these rules it is unreachable.

### 6.5 Records (v2)

- A restart opening whose PH-type split was answered by F2's fallback is recorded on its `attempt_opened`: `phase_update(<cause>; fallback(<unit>, <kernel>)[, …])`, one item per such unit in signature order, `<kernel>` ∈ {`tp`, `ph-band`}; `ph-band` when the PH closure answered through its band route; `tp` when the TP flash answered (the PH closure refused, or the split left `ZERO_FLOW`). No item when the PH closure answered by its primary route. The grammar carries no float (T03 §4.10 as amended).
- `ZERO_FLOW` joins the branch vocabulary of T03 §4.10's `inadmissible(<stream>, <branch>)`: `zero_flow` (a `ZERO_FLOW` branch whose feed flows at closure), beside `all_liquid` and `all_vapor`.
- The traversal's band routes are recorded as in F1 (`closure_route(<unit>, band)`), before attempt 0 and after T02 §6.2's projection records, in declaration order.

`SolvePolicy.phase_contract: Literal["T03-phase-contract-v1", "T05b-phase-contract-v2"]`, default v1; `schemas/solve-policy.schema.json` `phase_contract` from `const` to `enum` of the two. ## 7. The `ZERO_FLOW` regime, and the zero-flow form of a non-lifted outlet (normative, v2; ADR 0012 D4 (c), D12)

### 7.1 Selection

A lifted split is in `ZERO_FLOW` exactly when every flow of its feed is `+0.0` (ADR 0001 D3.1; after signed-zero normalization) — heater style: the outlet stream's `n`; products style: the inlet stream's `n`. No threshold.

### 7.2 The form (per split; square by construction)

| | pinned at `+0.0` (columns removed) | dropped rows | added row |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| heater style (valve, reactor, K02 heater) | `S.vap.<c>`, `S.liq.<c>`, `S.V`, `S.L` | `<U>:<EQ>-equilibrium:<c>`, `<U>:split:<c>`, `<U>:Vdef`, `<U>:Ldef` | — |
| products style (PH flash, K02 TP flash) | `S_V.n.<c>`, `S_L.n.<c>`, `S_V.N`, `S_L.N` | `<U>:<EQ>-equilibrium:<c>`, `<U>:<EQ>-mole:<c>`, `<U>:Ndef:vapor`, `<U>:Ndef:liquid` | — |
| **PH-type, in addition** | — | the unit's energy row | the **label row** `<U>:zero-flow-label`: `T_out − T_label` |

8 columns and 8 rows leave; a PH-type split swaps one more row for the label. Why each goes: at zero flow the equilibrium rows vanish identically (every term is bilinear in lifted flows), the total definitions become `0 − 0`, the split rows become `−n = 0` — a copy of the unit's own mole rows once the inlet is dormant — and a PH-type unit's energy row becomes a function of the dormant inlet's flows alone (its gradient `h_i` is a combination of the upstream rows that fix those flows) while its temperature has no row at all (T05 A15). For a TP-type unit the energy row still fixes the duty (`Q + Ḣ_in = 0`), and the temperature has its specification row, so nothing is swapped.

- `T_out` is the split's temperature column; `T_label` is the unit's inlet stream temperature — T05 §4.7 (a)'s label, which the causal evaluator returns (valve, PH flash, duty-mode reactor).
- The label row has kind `temperature` (tolerance `τ_T`, row scale K03 §4.2's temperature nominal). It is a row of the attempt's system and of the verifier's zero-flow form (§9.3) — **never of the compiled declaration**, so no `model_version`, structure hash or T01 report moves. Its derivative is exact (`+1` at `T_out`, `−1` at `T_label`); the attempt's `jacobian_pattern` includes its two entries.
- *Generator-checked* at DZ-1…DZ-5: the full (declaration) form has exactly the split's three equilibrium rows identically zero and rank `n − 3` (T05 A28's structure, re-derived); the zero-flow form is square with scaled `rcond₁` between 0.0131 (DZ-3) and 0.250 (DZ-1); the dormant temperature column is T05 A15's registered structure in the full form (no row for the valve and the duty-mode reactor, only `PHF-T` for the PH flash).

### 7.3 Lattice, screen and closure

- **Lattice.** `LIQUID — TWO_PHASE — VAPOR`, with `ZERO_FLOW` adjacent to each of the three. ADR 0005 D2's adjacency of signatures applies unchanged with this relation.
- **Screen.** For every lifted split — single-phase, `TWO_PHASE` or `ZERO_FLOW` — every trial checks that exact dormancy of the split's feed agrees with the regime being `ZERO_FLOW`. A disagreement flags the unit; its reported regime is `ZERO_FLOW` (dormant feed) or the kernel's (F2 for PH-type, the TP flash for TP-type, at the trial); the trial is phase-rejected (T03 §4.3), never altered. Cost: one comparison per split per trial.
- **Closure.** A `ZERO_FLOW` branch (§8) is admissible iff its feed is exactly dormant; otherwise `inadmissible(<stream>, zero_flow)` and a restart in the kernel's regime. A flowing branch cannot have a dormant feed at a root (the split rows). *(Amended, second pass, finding F6.)* Then the **swapped-row test** of §7.8 (iv) 2: a PH-type split's energy row, swapped out for the label row, must hold at the final state, or the region closes `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`, `zero_flow_conflict(<row id>)`. The valve's `VLV-energy` vanishes identically at dormancy; the PH flash's `PHF-duty` and the duty-mode reactor's `RX-duty` equal `Q = Q_spec` there, so they hold iff `Q_spec = 0` — the condition the causal evaluators already impose (`duty_into_dormant_stream`, T05 §4.7 (a)). Without the test, DZ-2C (§12.9) would close `CONVERGED` on a state that violates a declared row by 1 000 W.
- **Disappearance** (T03 §4.6) is unchanged: a dormant stream has no watched variable.

### 7.4 Leaving and entering `ZERO_FLOW`

- **Entering** (a flowing split's feed becomes exactly dormant): the opening pins the split's lifted flows at `+0.0` and leaves every other value as it is; the label row closes on the next Newton step.
- **Leaving** (the feed flows again): the zero-flow state carries no enthalpy, so the regime and split come from the TP flash at the feed's `(n, T, P)` for both closure types, recorded `fallback(<unit>, tp)` for a PH-type split (§6.5). The next attempt's own contract corrects the regime if the root lies elsewhere.
  *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S14 — re-review N-W1; code to the text, text made exact.)* **The flash's inputs, precisely:** the feed's `n` and `T` at the current state, and the split's **outlet** pressure (its own `P` column, which the unit's pressure rows tie to the feed's `P − ΔP`) — the pressure the unit flashes at; the text's "feed's `P`" was loose, and at `ΔP ≠ 0` would judge the regime at the inlet. For a PH-type split the answer's `T` (the feed's) is written to **every temperature column of the split's streams** (§6.2 step 3 as amended by Q-S11 (a)); a TP-type split flashes at its own specified `T`, as today. The implementation took the split's own `T` column (*measured*, re-review P9: 400 K in CH-UP, CH-DZ12 and CH-3 while the feed was at 352.005 K) — the zero-flow label of an earlier feed, i.e. the attempt history, which Q-S11 (a) already ruled must not decide an opening: a stale label far enough from the feed (a vapour feed judged at a liquid-range `T`) opens the wrong regime and costs a restart. It cost nothing on the registered cases (P14: still `VAPOR`); the change is for robustness, not a measured failure. Acceptance: B36.
  *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S15 (2) and (3) — the build lane's W10.4 decisions.)* **"The feed" is §7.1's.** Products style: the inlet stream, whose `T` is another stream's column, written as above. Heater style (valve, duty-mode reactor): the **outlet** stream, whose `T` is the split's own column. That flash is unchanged, and nothing is written. The ruling above does not reach it, because its hazard needs an opening that writes the feed but not the label. No opening writes a heater-style split's feed except that split's own answer: a stream has one producer, and the fixed point's resets write only dormancy-form outlets, which are not lifted. So such a split leaves only where a Newton step or the supplied start made its outlet flow. At every accepted iterate and every full-step trial, the linear label row holds its `T` to the label source to rounding. The one exception is a halved candidate taken from an attempt's first iterate. There both columns are trial values, and neither is history. *Rejected:* the label source's `T` for both styles. It gives the same value wherever it matters. By the build lane's statement, which was not measured here, it would move valve and reactor records that B36 (c) does not list. And it would make the heater-style leaving read a column of another unit. **The screen asks what the opening asks.** At a trial, a PH-type split in `ZERO_FLOW` whose feed flows reports the TP flash at the same inputs as the leaving answer: the feed's `n` and `T` (as above) and the split's `P`. The kernel at the candidate (§7.8 (ii) 5) and the screen are then one function at one state. The guard's one sanctioned difference (§6.2, Q-S10: `ph_state` refusing inside the domain range) does not cover a leaving answer: a leaving answer that differs from the screen's report is a defect. Before B36 the screen and the leaving answer used the same flash at the same inputs, so the exemption never actually acted there (re-review, item 6). B36 moved only the answer, and the exemption would then hide a real disagreement: the cause string would name one regime while the signature opened another. That is the form of the N4 defect that Q-S12 rejected. *Measured* with the change substituted: the full gate is unchanged (3129 passed, 1 xfailed), the `t05b` identity document is identical, and the B34 sweep is identical. Across the whole suite, under either screen, no leaving answer differs from the screen's report. Acceptance: B36 (d).
- *(Amended 2026-09-25, Q-S9.)* **Where entering and leaving are decided at an opening:** not only for the split the candidate names, but for every lifted split whose feed an opening writes — §7.8 (ii)'s fixed point, which applies both rules above to every split and every item until the opening agrees with exact dormancy.

### 7.5 Why the zero-flow form is regular (derivation)

In DZ-1 the zero-flow system is feed rows (`S1.n`, `S1.T`, `S1.P` fixed), `VLV-mole` (`S2.n = S1.n`), `VLV-P` (`S2.P`), and the label (`S2.T = S1.T`): a permutation of a unit lower-triangular matrix, determinant ±1 unscaled. The PH flash adds `PHF-T` (`S3.T = S2.T`), `PHF-pressure` and `PHF-Q` (`Q`), the reactor `RX-conversion` (`ξ`), `RX-mole` (`S2.n`), `RX-pressure`, `RX-spec`; each row introduces one new column. Hence regular at every dormant root whatever the label's value, which is what makes a moving label (DZ-3) solvable. The declaration's full form is singular there (three zero rows) — a property of the lifted formulation at zero flow, not of the problem.

### 7.6 Dormant non-lifted outlets: which, and the registry (amendment; Frank's answer to §18 Q2; ADR 0012 D12)

A **dormancy-form outlet** is an outlet of a unit with no lifted split whose temperature, at exact dormancy, is read by no row of the declared form (T05 §4.7 (a)'s singularity: `∂Ḣ/∂T = Σ n_i c_p = 0`). SYN-001's library has exactly these; a registry keyed by model id — and, for the exchanger, by its `specification` — declares them, beside R-046's split registry (`ref.dormancy_forms`):

| Model (configuration) | Outlet; item key | Trigger (every stream exactly dormant) | Swapped row | Label row | Label, `T_out − T_label` | Declared phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `syn001.liquid_pump` | `outlet`; `<U>.outlet` | `inlet` | `<U>:PUMP-energy` | `<U>:zero-flow-label` | `T(outlet) − T(inlet)` | `LIQUID` |
| `syn001.adiabatic_mixer` (K02) | `outlet`; `<U>.outlet` | every stream of `inlet` | `<U>:MIX-energy` | `<U>:zero-flow-label` | `T(outlet) − T(first stream of inlet)`, connection order | `LIQUID` |
| `syn001.heat_exchanger`, `specification ≠ hot_outlet_temperature` | `hot_outlet`; `<U>.hot_outlet` | `hot_inlet` | `<U>:HX-energy-hot` | `<U>:zero-flow-label:hot` | `T(hot_outlet) − T(hot_inlet)` | `hot_phase` |
| `syn001.heat_exchanger`, `specification ≠ cold_outlet_temperature` | `cold_outlet`; `<U>.cold_outlet` | `cold_inlet` | `<U>:HX-energy-cold` | `<U>:zero-flow-label:cold` | `T(cold_outlet) − T(cold_inlet)` | `cold_phase` |

- **The labels are the causal evaluators'.** T05 §9.2 (1) (the pump's inlet temperature), T05 §10.2 (1) (a dormant side's inlet temperature, or its specified outlet temperature, which is why a temperature-specified side has no form), K02's mixer (`connected[0]`, the first inlet in connection order, `InstanceView.ports`' R1 order). So a traversal start satisfies every label row exactly, as for §7.2.
- **Why these and no others (generator-checked, `ref.dormant_outlet_structure`).** At exact dormancy every other outlet temperature of SYN-001's library is read by a specification or copy row — feed `FEED-T`, heater `HEAT-T`, splitter `SPLIT-T`, separator `SEP-T`, outlet-temperature reactor `RX-spec`, a temperature-specified exchanger side `HX-spec` — or is a lifted PH-type split's (valve, PH flash, duty-mode reactor: §7.2's label). The K02 TP flash's product temperatures are its `FLASH-T:vapor/liquid` specification rows (T01 §4's registered single-column rows; not built by the twin). The exchanger is scanned at all three specifications × {hot, cold, both} dormant: a dormant side's outlet temperature is read by `HX-spec` exactly when the specification is that side's, and by nothing otherwise.
- **`check_agreement` (R-046) extends**: every registered swapped row is a row its unit authored that reads the outlet's temperature column, and every trigger and label port is wired; otherwise `ValueError` with message prefix `dormancy_form_disagrees(` — a defect, never a typed refusal (as for splits).
- A model outside both registries whose dormant outlet temperature has no row keeps the declared form; K04's regularity screen reports it (`RANK_DEFICIENT`, `UNVERIFIED`) — a typed result, never a silent success (§17).

### 7.7 The zero-flow form of a non-lifted outlet, and why it is regular (derivation)

**Selection.** Per dormancy-form outlet, exactly when every flow of every stream of its trigger port is `+0.0` (ADR 0001 D3.1, after signed-zero normalization). No threshold. The trigger is the inlet, not the outlet, because the inlet is what the causal evaluator tests and what upstream structure makes exactly zero (a zero feed, a splitter with `r ∈ {0, 1}`, a pinned product of a single-phase lifted split, a dormant chain); the outlet follows it through the unit's linear mole rows.

**The form.** Drop the swapped row, add the label row; pin nothing (the unit has no lifted flows; its outlet flows stay columns fixed by its mole rows). One row out, one in: square by construction. The label row is §7.2's in every respect — kind `temperature`, tolerance `τ_T`, temperature row scale, exact entries `+1` at `T_out` and `−1` at `T_label`, a row of the attempt's system and of the verifier's form and **never of the compiled declaration** — so no `model_version`, structure hash, T01 report, plan or `signature_units` moves, and the K02 mixer's compiled rows are untouched (R-038).

**Why the swapped row is the one to drop.** At exact dormancy of the trigger inlet and the outlet, the swapped row's `T_out` coefficient `−Σ_i n_out,i c_p` is zero, and its remaining gradient lies in the span of rows the attempt keeps:

- pump: `PUMP-energy`'s gradient is `(1/η)` times `PUMP-work`'s (both read `W`; `W` is their only non-flow entry at zero flow) plus enthalpy-weighted flow entries, which `PUMP-mole` and the rows fixing the inlet flows span;
- mixer: `MIX-energy` has flow entries only (its `T` and `P` entries carry a factor `n`), spanned by `MIX-mole` and the rows fixing the inlets;
- exchanger side `d`: `HX-energy-d` is `∓e_Q` plus flow entries; `e_Q` is spanned by `HX-spec` (duty) or by the other side's energy row.

So the declared form loses exactly one rank per dormancy-form outlet at a dormant state, and the zero-flow form is regular — *generator-checked* at DZ-6…DZ-10 and at the nine exchanger patterns (`ref.dormant_outlet_structure.exchanger_patterns`): the declared rank is `n − (number of forms)`, every dormant outlet temperature column of a form is empty, and the zero-flow form is square with full rank (scaled `rcond₁` from 0.0131 (DZ-10) to 0.250 (DZ-6, DZ-9)).

**Consistency of the swapped row (applies to §7.2's PH-type energy row as well).** At the form's root the swapped row equals: the pump's `W` — zero by `PUMP-work` (`ηW = 0` at zero flow), so always consistent; the mixer's `0` identically; an exchanger side's `∓Q` — zero iff the retained rows give `Q = 0` (a duty specification `Q_spec = 0`; a temperature specification on the dormant side, where the other energy row then fixes `Q = 0` if that side is dormant too or its outlet is at its inlet temperature); the valve's `0` identically; the PH flash's and duty-mode reactor's `Q = Q_spec`. Where it is nonzero the declaration has no root with that dormancy although the form has one — the causal evaluators refuse exactly these specifications (`duty_into_dormant_stream`, `specification_unsatisfiable_with_dormant_side`), and §7.8 (iv) 2 makes the EO path refuse them too.

### 7.8 How an attempt carries the form: signature items, screen, openings, closure (v2)

No split exists, so there is no split regime to extend. The form is nevertheless an attempt-level selection of equations, and ADR 0005 D2 requires that the equations an attempt runs are fixed at its opening and identified by its signature: the residual/Jacobian pairing identity includes the phase signature (`interfaces-frozen.md` §1), the cycle rule compares signatures, and every attempt rebuilds its problem from its signature (T03 §5.2). A regime-free switch evaluated per residual call would break all three. So the regime **machinery** is reused and the units are **not** made signature units:

- **(i) Signature.** The attempt signature is ADR 0005 D2's list (one regime per signature unit, in plan order) **followed by one item `[<U>.<port>, ZERO_FLOW]` per dormancy-form outlet whose zero-flow form the attempt runs**, in declaration order of units and, within the exchanger, hot before cold. An outlet in its declared form contributes no item. The plan's `signature_units` still lists the lifted units only. `AttemptContext.active_phases` and every `branch_provenance[].signature` carry the same list. The cycle rule and the attempt budget read the full list. For adjacency, an item's absence (the declared form) and its presence are one step apart, as `ZERO_FLOW` is adjacent to every flowing regime (§7.3). In a cause string the absent state is named by the outlet's declared phase: `phase_wall(<trigger>, U-PUMP.outlet:LIQUID->ZERO_FLOW)`, `phase_wall(<trigger>, U-HX.hot_outlet:ZERO_FLOW->VAPOR)`. No `fallback(…)` item, no kernel and no `branch_found` entry exist for a non-lifted outlet.
- **(ii) Attempt 0 and restart openings.** Attempt 0's items are the outlets whose trigger streams are exactly dormant at its opening state (after T02 §6.2's projection and §6.4's seeding). At a restart opening the lifted part of the signature is set as today (the candidate's regimes, pins, kernel splits); **then the items are recomputed from the opening state** — an upstream split the kernel sets can create or remove an item that the candidate's reported signature did not show (DZ-12). An outlet whose item changes has its outlet flows set to its mole balance at the opening state — pump and exchanger side `n_out,i := n_in,i`, mixer `n_out,i := Σ_k n_in(k),i` summed in connection order — which is exactly `+0.0` on entering; its temperature keeps its value (the label row closes it on entering, the energy row on leaving). **Why the reset on leaving:** the outlet flows are still zero at that opening, so the declared energy row's `T_out` coefficient `−Σ n_out c_p` is zero and the attempt's first Jacobian is singular — *generator-checked* at DZ-12's opening: rank 33 of 34 without the reset (the `S4.T` column empty), regular with it.
  *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S4 (5) and Q-S9 — the review's M1 and N1; R-065.)* **Every opening is made consistent with exact dormancy by one fixed point over lifted splits and items together.** The text above recomputed items only, once; the implementation iterated items and resets with each outlet reset at most once. Both miss what the review measured: an opening writes streams that are *feeds of other lifted splits* — a products-style split's products, a reset outlet — and nothing re-derived those splits' `ZERO_FLOW` membership, so two PH flashes in series (SC-4's topology) restarted from another duty's traversal opened `U-PHF2` in `ZERO_FLOW` with its feed flowing (or in `VAPOR` with it dormant) and the region raised an untyped `RuntimeError`; DZ-12 with a second PH flash on the exchanger's hot outlet did the same through the reset (`docs/reviews/T05b-review.md` M1, P1, P4, *measured*). The rule, under v2, at **every** opening — attempt 0 after T02 §6.2's projections and §6.4's seeding and start rules, and every restart after the candidate's regimes, pins and kernel answers:

  1. **Passes, in declaration order** (units in declaration order; within a unit its lifted split, then its dormancy-form outlets, an exchanger's hot before cold). Each pass: **(a)** every lifted split whose feed is exactly dormant and whose regime is not `ZERO_FLOW` enters it (§7.4: its lifted flows set to `+0.0`); every split in `ZERO_FLOW` whose feed flows leaves it (§7.4: regime and split from the provider's TP flash of the feed's `(n, T, P)` at the current state — *amended 2026-09-25, Q-S14:* the feed's `n` and `T` and the split's outlet `P`, the `T` written to every temperature column of the split's streams); **(b)** every dormancy-form outlet's item is read off the current state, and an outlet whose item differs from its **reference** — the previous attempt's item, or at attempt 0 the item read off the start as supplied, before any projection — has its flows set to its mole balance at the current state whenever they differ from it (so a reset made stale by a later write in the same opening is redone: review N1).
  2. **Termination.** The opening ends at the first pass that changes nothing; its signature (lifted regimes in plan order, then items) is read off that final state. With `m` lifted splits and dormancy-form outlets in the region, an acyclic propagation settles within `m` passes whatever the declaration order. If pass `m + 1` still changes something — reachable only through a recycle whose dormancy does not settle; none is known on SYN-001's library — the region closes **`ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING`** with message first line `opening_not_settled(<id>)`, `<id>` the first split's unit id or item key, in declaration order, that pass `m + 1` changed: a typed outcome, never an exception. The existing `RuntimeError` guard (`defect: <U> opens attempt …`) stays behind the fixed point and is unreachable.
  3. **Records; no new grammar.** At a restart, a regime the fixed point changed is carried by the opened signature; a PH-type split that leaves `ZERO_FLOW` adds `fallback(<U>, tp)` to its `attempt_opened` (§6.5's existing item); entering adds nothing (the zero-flow answer is the regime's definition). At attempt 0 each change is recorded as T02 §6.2's `projected(<S>, <branch>, <regime>)` (§6.4). An item change is recorded by the signature alone, as B20 (d) already states.
  4. **Inertness.** At the traversal start no projection changes a feed's dormancy (every registered start's splits already equal the kernel's, B07's premise) and every registered restart (SC-3, DZ-12) writes no feed of another lifted split, so on every registered record the fixed point changes nothing beyond what the text above already did: the `t05b`, `t05` and SYN-001 keys are unchanged by it (proved on its own commit, before Q-S11 (a) moves SC-3 and DZ-12).
  5. *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S12 — re-review M2, must-fix; the reviewer's option (A).)* **At a phase-rejected candidate every changed unit's kernel is asked at the candidate, before any answer is written.** T03 §4.5 opens a restart at "the candidate's full state, each changed unit's split from the kernel *there*". The implementation asked the changed units in turn and wrote each answer before asking the next, so a unit whose feed an earlier answer had rewritten (a products-style split's products are the next flash's feed) was asked at a state the screen never saw; the review N4 guard then compared a screen at one state with a kernel at another and raised an untyped `RuntimeError: defect: the kernel reports U-PHF2 VAPOR at the candidate the screen reported TWO_PHASE` — CH-UP's flowsheet with `U-PHF` declared first, 15 of the 104 restarts of the re-review's sweep P10, typed with `U-PHF2` first (*measured*, re-review P10, P11; present before W9). The rule, under both literals: (a) the candidate state is fixed; (b) every unit whose reported regime differs from the frozen one is asked at it, and each answer's regime is checked there against the reported one (N4's guard and its one sanctioned difference, §6.2, unchanged); (c) then every answer is written — each writes only its own products' or outlet's columns, so the writes are disjoint and their order is immaterial; (d) then items 1–4 settle dormancy. A downstream split's products then lag its rewritten feed by that feed's change; the lag sits on its linear mole rows and the next Newton step closes it, as every opening's does. *(Made exact 2026-09-25, Q-S15.)* The lag also sits on its energy row, through the feed's enthalpy. Its products' `T` too is the candidate's feed `T` (a leaving answer, §7.4) or the closure's answer at the candidate. That row is nonlinear, so the next step reduces its lag rather than closing it. The rule is unchanged: the candidate state is the one the screen judged, and the sweep measures the rule (B34 (a)). **Rejected:** (B), apply in order and skip the check for a unit whose closure inputs an earlier answer rewrote — it keeps the order dependence and lets a regime the screen never reported open the attempt, so the cause and the signature can disagree (the N4 defect in another form). *Measured with (A) substituted (design lane, P10's sweep):* no exception in 104 runs, and every pair ends with the same outcome, and for `CONVERGED` the same final regimes, in both declaration orders (80 `CONVERGED`, 12 `ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING`, 6 `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`, 4 `STAGNATION`, 2 `BOUND_BLOCKED`). Acceptance: B34.

  A single-pass or at-most-once reset would open the second of two chained forms with a stale item, and a lifted split downstream of a written stream in the wrong regime. Acceptance: B31.
- **(iii) Screen.** Every trial checks, per dormancy-form outlet, that exact dormancy of its trigger streams agrees with the item's presence. A disagreement flags the trial with the item toggled in its reported signature; the trial is phase-rejected (T03 §4.3), never altered. One comparison per outlet per trial.
  *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S4 (6); R-058 amended.)* **"Every trial" includes an attempt whose signature is empty** — a v2 region with at least one dormancy-form outlet, no lifted split and no active item. The implementation screened a trial only when the frozen signature was non-empty (`numerics/newton.py`, `signature and …`), so in such a region a trial that made a trigger exactly dormant was accepted, the declared form's next Jacobian had an empty `T_out` column (§7.7), and only the closure agreement of (iv) 1 — reached only if Newton still converged — could restart it: a lost root in the case this section exists for, and a departure from its letter. The screen runs at every trial of a v2 attempt in a region with a dormancy-form outlet, whatever its signature; under v1 nothing changes (no forms). It is inert on every registered record: every registered v2 attempt in such a region has a lifted split or an active item in its signature, so its trials are screened already (proved by the unchanged `t05b` key and B07).
- **(iv) Closure**, after T03 §4.7's checks and §7.3's, in this order, the first that fails deciding:
  1. *agreement:* an item whose trigger streams flow → `inadmissible(<outlet stream>, zero_flow)`; an outlet without an item whose trigger streams are exactly dormant → `inadmissible(<outlet stream>, dormant)`; each restarts with the item toggled. Both are unreachable after (ii) and (iii) and are specified so that no silent path exists. `dormant` joins `zero_flow`, `all_liquid` and `all_vapor` in T03 §4.10's branch vocabulary.
  2. *the swapped-row test:* every row a zero-flow form swapped out — non-lifted (§7.7) and lifted PH-type (§7.2) — is evaluated at the final state against its kind tolerance (K03 §5.2's per-row test, `KIND_TOLERANCE`). If one fails, the region closes **`SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`** (an existing outcome) with message `zero_flow_conflict(<row id>)` naming the first failing row in signature order; its value goes to the non-R0 `RegionAttempt.observations`; no certificate is issued. No restart is proposed: the swapped row is a function of values the attempt's retained rows fix (§7.7), so no state of this signature satisfies all rows, and another signature needs a lifted regime to change, which step 1 and T03 §4.7 have just found no reason for.
- **(v) Cores.** T04's homotopy and PTC cores run the attempt's rows as given. A label row is algebraic (ADR 0008 D3: no accumulation), so a PTC mass matrix has a zero row there; the swapped row's holdup leaves with the row.

## 8. `branch_found` (amends T03 §8.2; ADR 0012 D6)

K03 §8.2's branch found, per split, from the state: `V = L = 0` → `ZERO_FLOW`; `V = 0 < L` → `LIQUID`; `L = 0 < V` → `VAPOR`; else `TWO_PHASE`. The attempt signature, the closure's admissibility (§7.3) and the fingerprint read it. It applies under both literals, because a fingerprint describes the state (R-029). Because the branch rule applies under both literals, so does §7.3's closure rule for a `ZERO_FLOW` branch (admissible iff the feed is exactly dormant): under v1 a dormant split still runs its `TWO_PHASE` form and converges exactly as before, and only its `branch_found` and certificate change (B15). No registered fingerprint has a split with `V = L = 0` (W0.4 proves it on every committed record); the literal `T03-root-fingerprint-v1` is unchanged, and a T05-era test record of a dormant split (A28's) would compare `DISTINCT` with a new one — stated, not hidden. *(Amended, second pass, W0.4 measured.)* W0.4 found no fixture, benchmark reference or identity key with such a split, and one committed **evidence** record that has four: T05's manifest `evidence/T05/91ac0103…/manifest.json`, whose A28 value records the pre-T05b arm (`TWO_PHASE` for `V = L = 0`). That is the retired assertion's measured evidence, kept as it was (§16), not a registered expectation: nothing compares against it, and nothing beyond this note changes. A dormancy-form outlet of §7.6 has no `branch_found` entry: it is not a split, and its dormancy is in the state the fingerprint hashes.

## 9. The verifier (normative; amends K04 §4.4, §4.7, §7.1 for revision-built flowsheets; ADR 0012 D7)

SYN-001's legacy check set (`verify/checks.py`) is untouched. The revision table (`verify/table.py`, R-046) changes as follows; its K02 entries stay bitwise paired with the legacy set (W1.d) because nothing below triggers at a SYN-001 state (§4.4's margins).

### 9.1 Temperature-degenerate splits

For each lifted split whose feed flows, the verifier computes — by its own bisection on the provider's `lnK`, sharing no code with the solver (R-016) — `T_b`, `T_d` of the feed at the split's `(T, P)` and `δ` (§4.4). If `δ ≤ τ_T` the split is judged **degenerate**:

- **Enthalpies from the stored split.** Heater style: `Ḣ(S) = Σ_i S.vap.i · h_i^V(T, P) + Σ_i S.liq.i · h_i^L(T, P)`. Products style: `Ḣ(S_V) = Σ_i S_V.n.i · h_i^V(T_V, P_V)` and `Ḣ(S_L) = Σ_i S_L.n.i · h_i^L(T_L, P_L)`. Fresh `h` from the provider; one enthalpy map, so the producer's, every consumer's and the envelope's energy checks and every declared-port check read the same value (T05 §12.2). Each energy check that reads such an enthalpy appends to its `independence_qualification`: `enthalpy of <S> from the state's split: temperature-degenerate (ADR 0012 D7)`.
- **Admissibility.** `phase_admissibility.<U>.<S>.saturation`, value `δ`, kind temperature, tolerance `τ_T`, replaces the branch's `bubble`, `dew` or `closure` check: within `τ_T` every branch is admissible.
- **Independent split.** `independent_split.<U>.<S>` is reported `not_applicable` with reason `temperature_degenerate` (in place of `.total` and `.<c>`).

Otherwise K04 §4.4 and §4.7 apply unchanged, bit for bit.

### 9.2 Degenerate streams outside a split

A flowing stream that belongs to no lifted split and is itself temperature-degenerate is evaluated in its single declared phase (its `phase_capability`, `liquid` or `vapor`), with a qualification of the same form: *(ruled, second pass, confirming W4's text)* `enthalpy of <S> in its declared phase <PHASE>: temperature-degenerate (ADR 0012 D7)`, `<PHASE>` ∈ {`LIQUID`, `VAPOR`} — "from the state's split" would be false for a stream no split carries. With capability `vapor_liquid` its enthalpy is not determined by the state, and every energy or declared-port check reading it is `unsupported` with reason `temperature_degenerate_unlifted(<S>)` — a typed result, capping the verdict at `UNVERIFIED`. After T05's S1 ruling (a `vapor_liquid` connection only from a heater-style lifted outlet) this branch is unreachable; it is specified so that it cannot become a silent path.

### 9.3 Zero-flow roots

At a root whose `branch_found` has a `ZERO_FLOW` split:

- **Regularity** (K04 §7): the matrix is the scaled target Jacobian (after the alias eliminations, as today) with every `ZERO_FLOW` split reduced to its §7.2 form, the label row's two exact entries included. Its dimension is the reduced one.
- **Residual:** `residual.<U>:zero-flow-label`, value `T_out − T_label`, kind temperature, tolerance `τ_T`, for each PH-type `ZERO_FLOW` split, after the compiled rows (which all vanish at a dormant root).
- The split's admissibility and independent split stay `not_applicable` (`ZERO_FLOW`), as today.
- *(Ruled, second pass, answering W4's question.)* **Selection needs the feed dormant too.** A split is reduced here only when its branch found is `ZERO_FLOW` **and** every flow of its feed is exactly `+0.0`. A split with `V = L = 0` whose feed flows is judged as a flowing split — by §9.1 (`.saturation` when degenerate, as W4 implemented) or K04 §4.4 — with the declared matrix: such a state violates the split's own rows (`<U>:split:<c>` or the products' mole rows, by `n_c`) and carries no enthalpy for a flowing feed, so its residual and energy checks fail and the verdict is `FAILED` whichever matrix is used; the rule keeps the regularity record from describing a reduction the state does not support. `verify/zero_flow.py`'s `zero_flow_splits` adds the feed condition (W7b).

*(Amendment, second pass: dormant non-lifted outlets, §7.6–§7.10.)* At a root where the trigger streams of a dormancy-form outlet are all exactly `+0.0` — read from the state and the revision alone, under either literal:

- **Regularity:** the outlet is reduced to its §7.7 form in the same matrix — its swapped row removed, its label row (two exact entries) added. Its dimension is the reduced one.
- **Residual:** `residual.<U>:zero-flow-label` (pump, mixer) or `residual.<U>:zero-flow-label:<hot|cold>` (exchanger), value `T_out − T_label`, kind temperature, tolerance `τ_T`, after the compiled rows. The swapped row stays a checked compiled residual, which is how a certificate independently catches a zero-flow conflict (INJ-B4).
- **Exchanger terminal differences at a dormant side.** When either side's inlet stream is exactly dormant — whether or not that side has a form (DZ-8's temperature-specified hot side is dormant too) — `bounds_and_domain.<U>.hot_end` and `.cold_end` are `not_applicable`, reason `zero_flow`: each reads a dormant side's temperature, which is a label, not a point of a flowing temperature profile, and T05 §10.2 (1) does not judge them either. `bounds_and_domain.<U>.heat_flow` (`Q ≥ 0`) is still judged. DZ-7's dormant hot side is at 290 K, 10 K below the cold inlet, so a verifier that judged them would fail both (*generator-checked*).
  *(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S4 (2) and (4); R-058 amended.)* The reason is **`ZERO_FLOW`**, K04's existing reason for a check at a dormant stream — `zero_flow` above was a slip (no check reason is lower case, and one meaning should carry one word); the `t05b` key carries it at DZ-7 and DZ-8. And W0.11 (c) measured that the table already reported all three one-sided exchanger checks `not_applicable` (`ZERO_FLOW`) whenever either inlet was dormant (T05 §4.3's one-sided rule): `hot_end` and `cold_end` were never judged on a label, and what W7b changed is that `heat_flow` is now judged at a dormant side. The rule above stands as written, with that reason.
- **The verifier's own table (R-016).** `verify/zero_flow.py` keeps the verifier's own transcription of §7.6: per model id, each dormancy-form outlet's outlet port, trigger port, swapped-row family (`PUMP-energy`, `MIX-energy`, `HX-energy-hot`, `HX-energy-cold`) and label suffix; the exchanger's specification is read from which column the revision pins (`<U>.Q`, the hot or the cold outlet's temperature, as `_exchanger_specification` reads it). Selection reads the state and the revision; nothing is imported from the solver's registry.

The choice of matrix is made from the state alone, so it applies whichever literal solved the root: a v1 solve of DZ-1 (`CONVERGED` at iteration 0 in the `TWO_PHASE` form, T05 A28) now receives `VERIFIED`, because the state is a regular root of the equations that define it.

### 9.4 Why §9.1 is not a relaxation

The fresh flash recomputes a split from `(n, T, P)`. For a degenerate stream that recomputation does not exist at the registered resolution: every vapour fraction is consistent with `T` to within `τ_T`, and the provider's flash returns `LIQUID` or `VAPOR` (one component) or a vapour fraction its Rachford–Rice tolerance does not resolve (near purity; NP-1's fresh flash would miss the energy by 313 W and the vapour flow by 5.8e-3 mol/s, *emulated*). The two failure modes the fresh-flash checks exist for are still caught, at the same tolerances:

- **A wrong vapour fraction** fails the energy balance on the stored split: INJ-B1 (SC-1's root with the split all liquid, the TP kernel's answer) gives `energy_balance.U-VLV = +2 016 W` exactly, `2e6 τ_E`.
- **A temperature off saturation** leaves the window and falls back to the fresh flash: INJ-B2 (`T` 2e-6 K above `T_sat`, the lever-rule split kept) is judged by the fresh flash, which says `VAPOR`: `independent_split.U-VLV.S2.total = −1.9328 mol/s` and `energy_balance.U-VLV = −57 984.0004 W`, each `≥ 10³ τ`, although every compiled row passes (`VLV-energy` −4e-4 W, `VLV-equilibrium:B` −7.2e-9). INJ-B2p (5e-7 K above) stays inside and balances to −1e-4 W.

No tolerance moves, no category, kind or required check is added, and `check_policy_sha256` is unchanged (R-043).

### 9.5 Independence

The verifier's band bisection, degeneracy test, stored-split sums and zero-flow reduction are its own code on the state, the revision's declarations (split descriptors, as today) and a fresh provider. It calls no kernel, no row builder, no causal evaluator and no solver record (R-016).

## 10. R-007 for temperature-degenerate streams (normative; ADR 0012 D8)

In `tp_state.single_phase_admissible` (unit layer: K02's heater and mixer, T05's declared ports): a stream whose R-007 gap `|Ḣ_TP − Ḣ_φ| / (∂Ḣ_φ/∂T)` exceeds 1e-6 K is **admitted** if it is temperature-degenerate (§4.4, the unit layer's own band), and the reported gap is then `δ`. The verifier's declared-port check is unchanged in form: it reads §9's enthalpy map, which for a degenerate stream already carries its assigned phase (a split's) or its declared one (§9.2), so there the gap is measured against what the flowsheet says the stream is. Only the refusal path changes, so every admitted state is bitwise unchanged; every registered refusal is non-degenerate (T05's PHF-F3, PUMP-F1, PUMP-F2, SEP-F2, HX-F4: `δ ≥ 14.27 K`; R-007's 347.45 K state: `δ = 26.82 K`) and stays refused.

- **Why both phases, and why only in the unit layer.** At `T_sat` a pure stream's `(n, T, P)` cannot say whether it was produced liquid or vapour, so the causal layer, which receives only `(n, T, P)` (T05 §5.2 (3)), cannot tell and admits both. The certificate decides: its enthalpy map knows the stream's assigned phase (§9.1), so a saturated vapour product wired to a liquid-declared port fails `phase_admissibility.<U>.<port>` by `Δh/c_p ≈ 300 K`.
- **Registered states** (§12.7): R7-1 (pure B vapour at 360 K exactly, declared `VAPOR`) — TP gap 300 K, `δ = 0`, admitted with reported gap 0; R7-3 (5e-7 K above `T_sat`, declared `LIQUID`) — admitted, gap 5e-7 K; R7-2 (2e-6 K above) — refused, `inadmissible_phase(<port>, LIQUID)`; R7-4 — refused as registered. They are unit-layer states; the verifier's side of SC-4 is B11.
- *(Ratified 2026-09-25, Q-S6: W6's pre-screen, `saturation_band.outside_the_degeneracy_window`.)* The refusal path may first refuse at once when `g(T − 2τ_T; 0) > 0` (the bubble point lies more than `2τ_T` below `T`) or `g(T + 2τ_T; 1) < 0` (the dew point more than `2τ_T` above), by §4.2's monotonicity; every other answer — a provider refusal at `T ∓ 2τ_T` included — decides nothing and the band test runs as before. It is sound wherever `|g|` at `T ∓ 2τ_T` exceeds its own rounding whenever a computed `δ ≤ τ_T` is possible; on SYN-001 the build lane's argument puts that separation at about seven decades. *Measured:* decision-identical to the band test alone over 35 540 gate calls and a 126 420-call sweep (`docs/t05b-measurements.md`, W6); `tests/test_t05b_r007.py` keeps a sweep asserting it never refuses a stream the band test finds degenerate. The admitted path is untouched, so B19 and every registered refusal are unchanged.

## 17. What this specification does not establish

- *(Closed 2026-09-25 by ADR 0013 D1–D3, `docs/derivations/K04-F9-spec.md`; kept for the record.)* **Near-pure EO certification in T04 F9's band.** Between the degeneracy window (a band narrower than `τ_T` to `2τ_T`) and the fresh flash's own resolution, the certificate is judged by the fresh flash, which at such states is limited by the double resolution of `T` and by the provider's Rachford–Rice tolerance. - *(Added 2026-09-25, Q-S9 addendum.)* **A lifted flash whose root lies exactly on its dew or bubble point with the other product zero** — a `Q = 0`, `ΔP = 0` flash fed a saturated vapour or liquid, e.g. a knock-out drum after a flash (B31 (b)'s CH-UP, CH-DZ12, CH-3). Certifying it is §18 Q9. *(Corrected 2026-09-25, Q-S15 (1); measured.)* **The solve is unaffected only when its last attempt runs the flash single-phase.** Take a start whose restarts reach the flash in `TWO_PHASE`: B34 (a)'s `(Q2, Q_s, Q_t) = (0, Q_s, 30 kW)` for `Q_s` ∈ {90, 115, 125} kW, both orders. 6. **F6 — §7's first text had a false-success path** *(second pass)*: a zero-flow form whose swapped energy row does not vanish (a dormant PH flash or duty-mode reactor with `Q_spec ≠ 0`; a dormant exchanger side with a nonzero duty) converges, and nothing in §7.3 looked at the dropped row; the region would have closed `CONVERGED` and the certificate `FAILED`. §7.8 (iv) 2's swapped-row test closes it for lifted and non-lifted forms alike (DZ-2C, DZ-11).

8. **F8 — a dormant exchanger side would fail the second-law terminal checks on its label** *(second pass)*: the verifier judged `hot_end` and `cold_end` from whatever temperatures the state carries; a dormant side's is a label (290 K in DZ-7, 10 K below the cold inlet). §9.3 makes them `not_applicable` at a dormant side, as T05 §10.2 (1) already does causally. *(Premise corrected 2026-09-25, Q-S4 (2); W0.11 (c) measured.)* The verifier did not judge them: the table already reported `hot_end`, `cold_end` and `heat_flow` `not_applicable` (`ZERO_FLOW`) at any dormant inlet (T05 §4.3's one-sided rule), so no state had a label judged. The hazard was prospective, and §9.3 stands as its specification; what W7b changed is that `heat_flow` (`Q ≥ 0`) is now judged at a dormant side.
