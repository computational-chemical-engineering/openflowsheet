## 1. Scope, authority, and what is out of scope

**What K04 delivers.** A verifier that takes a solver's *final full state* and, independently of the solver, establishes whether the closed simulation problem is satisfied there; a `SolutionCertificate` that says so in one of four words and lists every check with its value, tolerance, scope and independence qualification; the [A08] regularity screen on the unregularized target Jacobian at that state; a `FailureBundle` for every solve that does not converge; and the registered injections that a correct verifier must reject.

**The six questions of the brief, and where each is answered.** (1) The independent checks and what each is independent of: §3–§4. (2) The [A08] matrix and recipe: §7. (3) The registered injections and what each must produce: §9. (4) The verdict vocabulary and partial-state labelling: §8. (5) The reproducibility classification and the declared numerical policy: ADR 0007 D1–D2. (6) What a certificate claims across a platform change and what K05's replay reports: ADR 0007 D3–D4.

**Design invariants.**

1. **The verifier never sees the solver.** Its input is a state vector and a revision; it evaluates the original 49 rows itself, assembles its own balances from the stream flows alone, flashes every stream itself, and factorizes the Jacobian itself. It reuses no factorization, no cache and no intermediate of K03. A certificate issued by a verifier that shares an intermediate with the solver is the injected-false-success failure of blueprint §8.2 in person, and the K03 review's Q3 is explicit about which intermediate would be the dangerous one: the lifted phase split, which the solver deliberately stopped watching (§4.7).
2. **Expectations are closed forms.** Every expected value in §6, §7.6 and §9 is from `benchmarks/k04/reference_values.yaml` or from `benchmarks/syn001/reference_values.yaml` (P01, 20 digits); the two were derived separately and agree to 4.8e-20. Where a number is a prototype observation it is labelled *measured* and used only to argue a tolerance.
3. **No accidental zeros, and every tolerance bites on both sides.** Every injection of §9 is registered with a twin state just below its threshold, so the assertion that the verifier fails one and passes the other cannot be satisfied by a verifier that fails everything or passes everything. The overall energy identity is registered at the trivial-root state *because it passes there* (§4.4, §9.2).
4. **Unsupported is a result.** A check the verifier cannot run reports `unsupported` with a reason, the verdict is then at most `UNVERIFIED`, and the certificate names the check (§8.3). No path returns silence as a pass.
5. **Every claim this document makes about its own numbers is executable** in `k04_reference.py --check` (§14.2).

**Out of scope** (brief §10, restated): replay bundle mechanics and the `RunManifest` field set (K05; ADR 0007 fixes what replay must *report*, not how); transactions, CLI, the v0.0 gate (K06); a validator for structural over-specification (no package before K06 delivers one; §10.3); generalizing the phase-attempt contract (T03) or rank diagnostics beyond this fixture (T06); a third platform; re-deriving the SYN-001 references; production code.

## 2. The verdict, in one paragraph

A `CONVERGED` K03 result carries a three-vector `t`. K04 requires the **full 47-vector `x_final`** (K03 §3.3's reconstruction, with its `full_state_sha256` — the K03 review's finding S2, which K04's implementation must close, §3.1). On that vector the verifier runs the check set of §4 under the registered check policy (§5): the original 49 rows re-evaluated without the solver's cache; the two alias certificates; component balances assembled from the stream flows alone; energy balances with every stream's enthalpy recomputed from its own `(n, T, P)` by a fresh flash, never from the lifted split; the fixed specifications; bounds and domain; phase admissibility and the independent split; a central-difference witness of the Jacobian; the [A08] regularity screen on the 47 × 47 scaled target Jacobian (§7), which includes the absolute-conditioning limit and records the first-order solution-error bound (§7.4). **`VERIFIED`** requires every required check to pass under the registered policy and the regularity status `NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED`; **`RELAXED`** is the same under an explicitly supplied policy that loosens any tolerance; **`UNVERIFIED`** retains passing checks but records a rank or conditioning limitation, a failed derivative witness or an unsupported required check; **`FAILED`** is any required check failing (§8). A certificate certifies residual accuracy at the registered tolerances; it reports what that buys in solution terms and does not promise it (§7.4). A non-`CONVERGED` solve receives no certificate and a `FailureBundle` (§10). Register entry: R-016.

## 3. The verifier's input, and what "independent" means here

### 3.1 Input contract

| Input | Source | Identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `x_final` (47 doubles, `variable_ids` order) | K03's reconstruction of the converged tear vector (K03 §3.3), handed over as an artifact by hash | `target_state_sha256 = EvaluationResult.state_sha256` of the verifier's own `residual(x_final)` call; must equal K03's `Checkpoint.full_state_sha256` when that is present |
| the `ProcessRevision` and its compiled problem | K01/K02, by `model_version` and `constants_sha256` | recorded; equality with the K03 `SolvePlan`'s is asserted (A02) |
| the check policy | the registered default (§5) or an explicitly supplied one | `check_policy_sha256` (ADR 0002 canonical JSON of the policy document) |
| the K03 `SolveResult`, plan and trace | for branch provenance and the attempt tree only | never read for a value the verifier computes itself |

**When `x_final` is absent** — which is K03's state today: `full_state_sha256` and `jacobian_identity` have no writer (K03 review S2; K03 manifest limitation) — the verifier does not reconstruct it from `t` through the traversal and proceed. It returns a certificate with `verification_status = UNVERIFIED`, every check `unsupported(reason = "final_state_absent")`, and the limitation named. The traversal is K02's code, not K03's, but a verifier that rebuilds the state it then judges has chosen the state; K04's implementation closes S2 by making K03 emit the vector and its hash at the converged checkpoint (A01), and the certificate cites that hash.

### 3.2 Independence, check by check

Blueprint §8.1 asks for "independent balance evaluators", and [A09] says what independence cannot mean for an energy check that uses the same property package. The table says, for each check, what it does not share with the thing it judges. "Shares the provider" means the SYN-001 correlations and reference data are common and the check is bookkeeping, not thermodynamic validation; the certificate records `provider_id`, `implementation_sha256`, `data_sha256` and `reference_convention` from `PropertyProvider.describe()` on every such check (A09).

| Check (§4) | Independent of K03 (solver) | Independent of K01 (compiler) | Independent of K02 (unit models) | Independent of the lifted split in `x` | Shares the provider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 residual | yes: fresh evaluation, no cache, no solver state | no: it *is* the compiled function | no | no: the rows contain the split | yes |
| 4.2 alias certificates | yes | no | no | n/a | no |
| 4.3 material balances | yes | yes: plain sums in the verifier | yes | yes: stream flows only | no |
| 4.4 energy balances | yes | yes | yes: assembled by the verifier | **yes: every enthalpy from a fresh flash of `(n, T, P)`** | yes (A09) |
| 4.5 specifications | yes | yes | yes | yes | no |
| 4.6 bounds and domain | yes | yes | yes | yes | domain limits only |
| 4.7 admissibility and split | yes | yes | yes | **yes: the split is recomputed and compared** | yes (the flash kernel) |
| 4.8 derivative witness | yes | partly: differences of K01's residual against K01's Jacobian | no | no | yes |
| 4.9 regularity | yes: a fresh factorization | no | no | no | yes |

Two facts about the provider path, *measured*: the provider's `h_liquid`/`h_vapor` and the P01 oracle's agree to 0.0 J/mol on a 21-point grid, and their K-values to 6.3e-16 relative. They are two transcriptions of one algebra. The verifier uses the **provider** (production code must not import `benchmarks/`); the test suite cross-checks the verifier's energy values against the **oracle** (A12), which is the code-independent second witness. Neither is a validation of the SYN-001 enthalpies, and the certificate says so (`independence_qualification` on every energy and phase check).

## 4. The check set

Every check produces a `CheckResult` (§12.2) with `id`, `value`, `tolerance`, `reference` (the `s_i` of blueprint §8.1's `|e_i| ≤ a_i + r_i s_i`), `result ∈ {pass, fail, unsupported, not_applicable}`, `scope` and, where applicable, `independence_qualification`. Rows and streams are named by id, never by position.

### 4.1 `residual` — the original 49 rows

At `x_final`, `F = CompiledProblem.residual(x_final, context)` under a **fresh context** (`phase_signature = None`, K03 §3.4) and a **fresh provider instance with no `ExactPropertyCache`** (blueprint §8.1: "reevaluate without approximate caches"; the exact cache is exact, but bypassing it costs nothing at this size and removes the question). For every row `i` of the 49, including the two alias-eliminated rows and the three tear rows: `|F_i| ≤ τ_kind(i)` (§5). One `CheckResult` per row; the check passes iff every row passes; the worst row is named. *Measured* floors at the five `x(t*)`: flow rows 3.6e-15 mol/s, temperature rows 0.0, pressure and energy rows 1.5e-10 (the high-recycle energy rows, magnitude 1.9e5 W), equilibrium rows 1.8e-14 (mol/s)². Registered tolerances sit 7, ∞, 7 and 7 orders above.

The pairing identity of ADR 0008 D2.4 is asserted between this `EvaluationResult` and the `JacobianResult` of §7 (equal `model_version`, `constants_sha256`, `state_sha256`, `phase_signature`); a mismatch is `unsupported(reason = "identity_mismatch")` on §7 and the verdict is at most `UNVERIFIED`.

### 4.2 `alias_certificates` — the eliminated rows are satisfied, not merely consistent

For each of K03 §7.2's two eliminated rows `e` with retained path rows `k` and signs `σ_k`: (i) `|F_e(x_final) − Σ_k σ_k F_k(x_final) − m_e| ≤ 1e-2 Pa` (the certificate identity holds at the final state; `m_e` from the `SolvePlan`), and (ii) `|F_e(x_final)| ≤ 1e-2 Pa` (the row itself is satisfied — K03 §7.3: eliminated is not discarded). *Measured* floor 1.5e-10 Pa. Registered: `U-FLASH:FLASH-P:inlet` and `U-SPLIT:SPLIT-P:recycle`, `m_e = 0` at every variant.

### 4.3 `material_balance` — sums of stream flows, nothing else

From the stream flows in `x_final` alone, by plain summation in the verifier (no compiled problem, no provider, no unit model): componentwise, for `i ∈ {A, B, C}`,

    mixer:     S1.n_i + S6.n_i − S2.n_i
    heater:    S2.n_i − S3.n_i
    flash:     S3.n_i − S4.n_i − S5.n_i
    splitter:  S5.n_i − S6.n_i − S7.n_i
    ratio:     S6.n_i − r · S5.n_i             (r read from the revision, not from the plan)
    envelope:  S1.n_i − S4.n_i − S7.n_i        (fresh feed = vapor product + purge, plan §3.2)
    totals:    S3.V − Σ S3.vap_i, S3.L − Σ S3.liq_i, S3.n_i − S3.vap_i − S3.liq_i, S4.N − Σ S4.n_i, S5.N − Σ S5.n_i

each `≤ τ_flow = 3.1e-8 mol/s`. *Measured* floor 3.6e-15. This is the check blueprint §8.1 means by "independent balance evaluators": the compiled `MIX-mole` rows compute the same sums, but through K01's expression graph and K02's row builders; a permuted component index, a dropped stream or a sign error in either would show here and not there. The *envelope* row is not a compiled row at all.

### 4.4 `energy_balance` — every enthalpy from a fresh flash of `(n, T, P)`

The verifier computes `Ḣ(S)` for every stream `S` from its `(n, T, P)` in `x_final` by calling the provider's `flash` at `(T, P)` on `n` and summing `Σ l_i h_i^L + Σ v_i h_i^V` over the phases the flash returns — **never from the lifted `S3.vap/liq` or from `S4`/`S5` as the flash's outlets**. A dormant stream (`Σ n = 0` exactly) has `Ḣ = 0` and no flash is evaluated (ADR 0001 D3.1). Then, with duties read from `x_final` (ADR 0001 D4.1: positive into the unit):

    mixer:     Ḣ(S1) + Ḣ(S6) − Ḣ(S2)
    heater:    Ḣ(S2) + Q_h − Ḣ(S3)
    flash:     Ḣ(S3) + Q_f − Ḣ(S4) − Ḣ(S5)
    splitter:  Ḣ(S5) − Ḣ(S6) − Ḣ(S7)
    envelope:  Q_h + Q_f − (Ḣ(S4) + Ḣ(S7) − Ḣ(S1))          (derivation §6)

each `≤ τ_energy = 1.01e-3 W`. *Measured* floor 9.4e-10 W (the Rachford–Rice root tolerance of the fresh flash at S3). **Why the fresh flash is the whole point:** at the trivial-root state of §9.2 the compiled `HEAT-duty` and `FLASH-duty` rows are exactly satisfied with both duties wrong by ±8237.85 W, because those rows read the lifted split; the fresh flash of S3 at (350 K, P) returns `β_h = 0.10136872342672775985` and the heater and flash rows here fail by exactly `∓8237.8503930694530451 W`. **And the envelope passes there** — the two errors cancel, `Q_h + Q_f` is invariant — which is why the envelope is registered at that state as a *documented blind spot* and never as the only energy check (invariant 3). [A09] qualification recorded on every row of this check.

### 4.5 `specification` — the revision's fixed values

For every fixed specification in the revision: `|x_final[id] − spec| ≤ τ_kind`: `S1.T = 300 K`, `S1.n = (1, 1, 1)`, `S3.T = 350 K`, `S4.T = S5.T = T_f`, `S1.P = P_spec`, and `r` (through §4.3's ratio rows). Tolerances: T 1e-6 K, P 1e-2 Pa, flow 3.1e-8 mol/s. *Measured*: exactly 0.0 at every variant (the traversal copies specifications, it does not solve for them). The temperature tolerance is the strictest in scaled terms (§5.3); §9.7 registers the state where it bites.

### 4.6 `bounds_and_domain`

`n_i ≥ 0` for every stream and lifted flow, exactly (after signed-zero normalization; `−0.0` is `+0.0`, ADR 0001 D1.5); every flowing stream's `(T, P)` inside the provider's declared domain (280–440 K, 50 000–200 000 Pa) — a dormant stream's `T`, `P` are labels and are not checked (D3.1); every stream's phase signature consistent with its declared capability. Exact.

### 4.7 `phase_admissibility` and `independent_split`

K03 §8.2's table, executed by the verifier at `x_final` for each lifted stream — S3 at (350 K, P) with its lifted `(vap, liq)`, and the flash unit's outlets (S4, S5) at `(T_f, P)` as the split of S3's flows:

| Branch found in `x_final` | Admissible iff | Registered value where it bites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `V = 0` | `Σ_i x_i K_i(T, P) ≤ 1 + 1e-12` | §9.2: S3 forced all-liquid at once-through, `Σ x K = 1.0703142190807741552` → **fail** |
| `L = 0` | `Σ_i y_i / K_i(T, P) ≤ 1 + 1e-12` | 420 K flash outlet, `Σ y/K = 0.31715802235115620433` → pass |
| both flowing | `v_i, l_i ≥ 0`, `|Σ_i (y_i − x_i)| ≤ 1e-10`, and the equilibrium rows of §4.1 | every 360 K flash outlet |

`ε_adm = 1e-12` as K03 §8.2 argues it. **`independent_split`:** the provider's `flash` of the stream's `(n, T, P)` is compared to the split in `x_final`: `|V_lifted − V_flash| ≤ τ_flow` and componentwise `|v_i,lifted − v_i,flash| ≤ τ_flow`. *Measured* at the true once-through state: 3.4e-14 mol/s; at the trivial-root state: `0.30410617028018327955 mol/s` (= `3 β_h`), 1e7 times the tolerance. This is the check the K03 review's Q3 asked for by name: the sink block's branch is somebody's responsibility, and it is this check's.

### 4.8 `derivative_witness` — central differences of the compiled function

The [A08] evidence stores "derivative accuracy". The compiled Jacobian is exact AD of closed forms (K01), and the certificate records its `pattern_provenance`; the runtime witness is a central difference of the *compiled 49-row function* (not of the traversal — plan §4.2 demotes that, and K03 measured why it fails at `t*`) at `x_final`, one column at a time, `h_j = 1e-5 · S_x,j`, compared to the AD Jacobian on the scaled entries `Ĵ = S_F⁻¹ J S_x`:

    max_ij |Ĵ_ij − FD_ij| ≤ 1e-7   over the declared pattern;   max off-pattern |FD_ij| ≤ 1e-7.

*Measured* (nominal / 420 K / high recycle): 2.8e-11 / 2.4e-11 / 2.8e-10 at δ = 1e-5; quadratic in δ down to 1e-5 and roundoff-dominated from 1e-6 (2.8e-9) — δ = 1e-5 is the registered step because it is the measured minimum; off-pattern differences are exactly 0.0 at every state (the compiled function is structurally exact). `1e-7` sits 350× above the worst floor and 1600× below the smallest nonzero scaled entry (1.6e-4, *measured*), so a dropped term, a wrong regime or a K at the wrong temperature is caught and roundoff is not. A failed witness makes the verdict `UNVERIFIED` with `derivative_limitation` (§8.2): the residual truths of §4.1–§4.7 stand; the Jacobian-based evidence of §7 does not.

## 5. Tolerances

### 5.1 The acceptance rule, by row kind

Blueprint §8.1's `|e_i| ≤ a_i + r_i s_i`, with the registered constants of ADR 0001 D6 (SYN-001 only; never applied to a real process), reported in full on every `CheckResult` (`value`, `tolerance = a + r s`, `reference = s`):

| Kind | `a` | `r` | `s` | `τ = a + r s` | `τ / S` (scaled) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `molar_flow` | 1e-9 mol/s | 1e-8 | 3 mol/s | 3.1e-8 mol/s | 1.0333e-8 |
| `heat_rate` | 1e-5 W | 1e-8 | 1e5 W | 1.01e-3 W | 1.01e-8 |
| `temperature` | 1e-6 K | 0 | — | 1e-6 K | 1e-8 |
| `pressure` | 1e-2 Pa | 0 | — | 1e-2 Pa | 1e-7 |
| `normalized_composition` | 1e-10 | 0 | — | 1e-10 | 1e-10 |
| `molar_flow_squared` (§5.2) | 3e-9 (mol/s)² | 1e-8 | 9 (mol/s)² | 9.3e-8 (mol/s)² | 1.0333e-8 |

The solver cannot move `s_i`: it is the registered scale, read from the registry, never an iterate (ADR 0001 D3.5).

### 5.2 The equilibrium rows — a registered tolerance for a lifted row kind

ADR 0001 D6 registers five physical kinds; the lifted equilibrium row `v_i L − K_i l_i V` (P02 §2.4, K02) has kind `molar_flow_squared` and D6 says nothing about it. K03 bounded it only through the inner-consistency check (`η ≤ 1e-10` scaled). The certificate needs a per-row rule, and the derivation is one line: dividing the row by the flow scale `S = 3 mol/s` gives a quantity of flow kind, so the rule is the flow rule at the flow scale — `a = 1e-9 × 3`, `r s = 1e-8 × 9`, `τ = 9.3e-8 (mol/s)²`, scaled `1.0333e-8`, identical to the flow rows' scaled tolerance. *Measured* floor 1.8e-14. This is a derived registration, not a new physical tolerance; §18 finding 3 notes that ADR 0001 D6 could carry it.

### 5.3 The scaled minimum

`τ̂_min = min_kind τ_kind / S_kind = 1e-8`, attained by the temperature rows (executable claim). It is the threshold of §7.4.

### 5.4 The check policy

The registered default policy `K04-check-policy-v1` is the table of §5.1 plus §7's thresholds and the required-check list of §8.1, serialized as a canonical JSON document (ADR 0002) whose SHA-256 is the certificate's `check_policy_sha256`. The verifier accepts a supplied policy; §8.2 says what a supplied policy does to the verdict.

*(Amended 2026-09-27: ADR 0013 Amendment 2; T07 design note, ruling round 5, M1.)* A supplied tolerance enters a certificate in two ways only.
- **As a comparison.** It is the right-hand side of each check's `|value| ≤ τ`.
- **As a route.** A decision that selects which checks exist, or at which state they are judged, reads ρ_k = max(τ_k(policy), τ_k(registered)). The routes are:
  - ADR 0013's projection guards and unresolved-split test;
  - ADR 0012 D7's test, which reads the registered τ_T.

So a policy that only tightens yields the registered certificate's checks and values, with stricter tolerances. It can turn a pass into a fail, never remove a check.

## 7. The regularity screen ([A08])

### 7.1 Which matrix

**The scaled target Jacobian `Ĵ = S_F⁻¹ J S_x` of the closed simulation problem after alias elimination: 47 rows (the 49 assembled rows minus the two eliminated pressure rows of K03 §7.2) by 47 columns (every variable), evaluated by the compiled problem at `x_final` under the fresh context of §4.1, with the recorded scales of the `SolvePlan`.** Not the 3 × 3 tear derivative, not the 44 × 44 inner block, not the rectangular 49 × 47.

Why. [A08]'s words are "the unregularized target Jacobian for the closed simulation problem after legitimate fixed-variable/alias elimination", and this is that matrix; it is the matrix an equation-oriented Newton would factorize (T02), so its nonsingularity is what "the root is locally unique" means for the flowsheet. In K03's partition it is block-triangular after elimination: `det Ĵ₄₇ = det Ĵ_φu · det(Ĵ_ρt − Ĵ_ρu Ĵ_φu⁻¹ Ĵ_φt)`, so it covers both the inner block and the tear derivative at once; a regular 3 × 3 with a singular inner block, or the reverse, would be masked by either smaller choice. The rectangular matrix is rejected because its two dependent rows are *known* aliases with certificates (§4.2); a rank test on it would report "deficient by 2" at every state and say nothing. The eliminated rows' satisfaction is §4.2's business.

*Measured* at the five `x(t*)`, scaled: `rcond₁` = 1.161e-3 (nominal), 1.851e-3 (once-through), 1.331e-4 (high recycle), 2.019e-3 (310 K), 7.705e-4 (420 K); the 44 × 44 inner block gives the same to three digits at four of five states, the 3 × 3 is between 1.2e-2 and 1.0. **Unscaled**, the same matrices give 9.0e-12 to 5.9e-11 — a threshold applied to the unscaled matrix would call every registered solution ill-conditioned, which is why [A08] says "recorded scales" and why K03 §4.3 forbids a silent unit scale.

### 7.2 The recipe and its thresholds

Exactly plan §6.3: `lu = splu(Ĵ)` with ADR 0004 D1's options (one code path; the factorization is *this* screen's, never a Newton's — §7.5); a `LinearOperator` whose `matvec` and `rmatvec` are `lu.solve(·)` and `lu.solve(·, trans="T")`; `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁` by `scipy.sparse.linalg.onenormest`; `rcond₁ = 1 / (‖Ĵ‖₁ · est ‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁)`. `onenormest` returns a lower estimate of the norm, so `rcond₁` is an *upper* estimate; *measured* ratio estimate/exact = 1.000 at every registered state and fixture (n ≤ 64). Recorded: dimension, `‖Ĵ‖₁`, the estimate, `rcond₁`, `min|U_ii|/max|U_ii|` (ADR 0004 D3.4's screen — recorded and never decisive), the permutations by reference, the method string, and the thresholds.

| Threshold | Value | Argument |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `τ_ill` | **1e-8** on `rcond₁` | The Jacobian is exact AD evaluated in double: entry error ~`n ε` ≈ 1e-14 (K03 *measured* 5.3e-15 against closed forms). A matrix with `rcond₁ ≥ 1e-8` stays nonsingular under any perturbation below 1e-8 relative, six orders above that error, so nonsingularity is decided and not guessed; and `κ₁ ≤ 1e8` keeps a sensitivity computed from this Jacobian (M-packages) within 1e-6 relative of exact. The registered states sit at ≥ 1.3e-4, four orders above; the constructed fixture at n = 32 sits at 1.5e-11, three orders below. |
| SVD rank tolerance | `n · ε · σ_max`, `ε = 2⁻⁵²` | the standard numerical-rank rule; below it a singular value is indistinguishable from the factorization's own backward error |
| `n_svd` | 2000 | the dense SVD budget; above it the escalation is `INCONCLUSIVE(reason = "svd_budget")`, retained, never guessed |

### 7.3 Statuses and escalation

    if splu raises "exactly singular" or the estimate is non-finite  →  escalate
    elif rcond₁ ≥ τ_ill                                              →  NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED
    else                                                             →  escalate
    escalate: if n > n_svd → INCONCLUSIVE(svd_budget)
              else dense SVD of Ĵ; rank = #{σ_k > n ε σ_max};
                   rank < n → RANK_DEFICIENT (rank, σ_min, σ_max, tol recorded)
                   rank = n → ILL_CONDITIONED (rcond₁, σ_min/σ_max recorded)

The U-diagonal ratio never enters the decision (blueprint [A08], ADR 0004 D3.4, brief §6): the fixtures of §7.6 have ratio exactly 1 and statuses `ILL_CONDITIONED` and `RANK_DEFICIENT`.

### 7.4 What a certificate promises about the solution, the recorded bound, and the absolute-conditioning limit (amended 2026-09-22)

**A certificate certifies residual accuracy.** Blueprint §8.1's acceptance rule is `|e_i| ≤ a_i + r_i s_i` on residuals and balances, the registered tolerances of ADR 0001 D6 are residual tolerances, and nothing in the authority promises that the *solution* is within those tolerances of the exact root. It cannot be promised on this problem: in scaled coordinates the residual-to-solution amplification is `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁`, *measured* 80–476 at the five `x(t*)` (144.8 at nominal), so a state that satisfies the residual rule exactly at tolerance is determined only to ~145–476 × `τ̂_min` in scaled solution terms. That is a property of the flowsheet, not a defect of the state, and a verdict rule that penalized it would fail every state converged to tolerance rather than to roundoff — which is what the first version of this section did (Status note above).

**The bound is recorded evidence, with a statement.** The first-order scaled solution-error bound

    b = ‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ · ‖F̂(x_final)‖_∞     (the screen's own norm estimate; no extra solve)

is computed on every certificate, recorded as `solution_error_bound_scaled`, and accompanied by the required statement: *"VERIFIED certifies the residuals at the registered tolerances; at first order the scaled solution is determined to within `b`; no solution accuracy beyond `b` is claimed."* It is **not a pass/fail check** and never limits the verdict by itself. *Measured* at the five `x(t*)`: `‖F̂‖_∞ ≤ 2e-15`, `b ≤ 6.9e-13`; at the §9 twins (states at the residual tolerance) `b` is 1.4e-7 to 7.2e-7 (*measured* by the Opus session), which is exactly `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ × ‖F̂‖` and is what the twins are for.

**Where a relative condition number cannot see, and what limits the verdict instead.** The plan's seed `x² = 0` under K03's Newton from `x⁰ = 1` halves `x` each step (`x − x²/2x = x/2`, every step accepted at `α = 1`, merit ratio 1/16) and stops at the first `x` with `x² ≤ τ`: at `τ = 1e-8`, step 14, `x = 2⁻¹⁴`, `F = 2⁻²⁸`, `Ĵ = 2⁻¹³`, `rcond₁ = 1`. The relative estimate is blind to the *absolute* smallness of the Jacobian in scaled coordinates, and that smallness is what [A08]'s "thresholds account for scales and derivative/evaluation uncertainty" is about: residual evaluation carries noise of order `n ε` in scaled units (K03 *measured* 5.3e-15 against closed forms at n = 47; ADR 0004 D3.2 uses the same `n ε`), and that noise, amplified through `Ĵ⁻¹`, is a solution uncertainty that no residual tolerance can see. The registered tolerance is meaningful in solution terms only if that amplified noise stays below it. Hence the **absolute-conditioning limit**, part of the regularity status:

    ‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ · n ε ≤ τ̂_min      ⟺      ‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ ≤ τ̂_min / (n ε),     ε = 2⁻⁵²

Violation is `ILL_CONDITIONED` with `reason = "absolute"` (the relative branch of §7.3 carries `reason = "relative"`), and the verdict is `UNVERIFIED` with the rank limitation — the residual truths are retained, the solution is declared undetermined at the tolerance. Thresholds: SYN-001 (`n = 47`, `τ̂_min = 1e-8`): `9.58213e5`, against a *measured* maximum `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ = 476.1` — a margin of 2000×; the `x²` seed (`n = 1`): `τ/ε`, i.e. `4.5036e7` at `τ = 1e-8` and `4503.60` at `τ = 1e-12`. Two registered seed states separate the cases in closed form: **SQ-1** (`τ = 1e-8`): `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ = 2¹³ = 8192`, 5500× under its threshold → `NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED`, verdict `VERIFIED`, `b = 2⁻¹⁵ = 3.0517578125e-5` recorded (3052 × `τ̂_min`) with the statement — the certificate says plainly that the solution is determined only to 3e-5; **SQ-2** (`τ = 1e-12`): 20 halvings, `x = 2⁻²⁰`, `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ = 2¹⁹ = 524288`, 116× *over* its threshold → `ILL_CONDITIONED(absolute)`, `UNVERIFIED`. The K03 Newton on `x²` trips the limit iff `τ^{3/2} < ε`, i.e. `τ < 3.7e-11` — a closed-form statement of when a residual tolerance on a double root stops meaning anything, which is the honest content of this section.

### 7.5 Identity and reuse

The screen evaluates `jacobian(x_final, context)` itself and asserts the pairing identity with §4.1's residual (ADR 0008 D2.4). **Reuse of a solver factorization is not implemented in v0.0** and is recorded as `factorization_source = "evaluated_at_final_state"`: K03 never factorizes the 47 × 47 matrix (it factorizes the 44 × 44 inner block and the 3 × 3 reduced system), so there is nothing that could belong to the final Jacobian. A later package that adds reuse must match `jacobian_identity` (K03 §12.5) against `target_state_sha256` and, on mismatch, report `INCONCLUSIVE(identity_mismatch)` — A24 registers that refusal on a constructed call so the path exists before anyone needs it (VER-02, VER-03).

### 7.7 What the screen does not claim

Residual satisfaction and regularity are separate (blueprint [A08]): `RANK_DEFICIENT` at SQ-0 does not make `F = 0` false and does not assert a continuum. The screen is local numerical evidence at one state; it says nothing about other roots (T03), about sensitivities beyond the first-order bound, or about any matrix other than §7.1's.

## 8. The verdict

### 8.1 Rules

Required checks: §4.1–§4.8. Regularity (§4.9, including §7.4's absolute-conditioning limit) is required evidence whose *status* is graded, not pass/fail; the solution-error bound (§4.10) is required evidence that is recorded and never graded.

| Verdict | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `VERIFIED` | every required check `pass`; regularity `NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED`; `check_policy_sha256` equals the registered default's, or the supplied policy is pointwise at least as strict; `derivative_path` of the solve is `assembled_schur` (K03 §12.1; a certificate is refused on the oracle path); no `NEAR_THRESHOLD` flag on a required check is *required* for `VERIFIED` but every such flag is recorded (ADR 0007 D2.4) |
| `RELAXED` | as `VERIFIED` but under a supplied policy that loosens at least one tolerance or waives a required check; the certificate lists every relaxation by check id with the registered and the applied tolerance. Never on the solver's or verifier's initiative: a policy is an input with a hash. Plan §6.2: a `RELAXED` certificate is never counted as verified success |
| `UNVERIFIED` | every required check that ran passed, and at least one of: regularity `RANK_DEFICIENT`, `ILL_CONDITIONED` (relative or absolute, §7.4) or `INCONCLUSIVE`; `derivative_witness` fail; a required check `unsupported`; `x_final` absent (§3.1) |
| `FAILED` | any of §4.1–§4.7 `fail` |

Precedence: `FAILED` over everything; then `UNVERIFIED`; then `RELAXED` over `VERIFIED`. A `FAILED` verdict on a `CONVERGED` solve is the false-success detection G04 requires, and it is recorded as such (`false_success_detected: true`).

### 8.2 Limitations, always listed

`limitations` is a list of typed entries, never prose alone: `rank_limitation(status, reason, rank, σ_min, inverse_one_norm, threshold)`, `derivative_limitation(max_diff)`, `unsupported_check(id, reason)`, `relaxation(id, registered, applied)`, `near_threshold(id, value, threshold)`, `shared_provider(provider_id, implementation_sha256, data_sha256)` (always present for §4.4 and §4.7 — [A09]), `final_state_absent`. The certificate also carries three sentences as text: the two blueprint §8.1 requires — that it is a target-model verification and not an experimental validation or an optimality claim, and that no finite test suite proves the verifier defect-free — and §7.4's solution-accuracy statement with the recorded `b`.

### 8.3 Partial states

A `SolutionCertificate` is issued only for a `CONVERGED` solve's `candidate_root` checkpoint. For a `partial` checkpoint (any failed solve's best iterate, or the closing checkpoint of a `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED` attempt), the same check set **may** be run and its `CheckReport` (the list of `CheckResult`s and the regularity evidence, with no verdict word) is attached to the `FailureBundle`; the checkpoint's `verification_scope` then widens from `unverified` to `checked_partial`. **No verdict from the four is ever attached to a `partial` checkpoint** (A20), so a budget-exhausted iterate that happens to satisfy every check still reads as the failure it is; blueprint §8.2: "an injected failure must not become a successful certificate through fallback". `Checkpoint.verification_scope` becomes `Literal["unverified", "checked_partial", "certified"]`, and `certified` is written only by the verifier, only on a `candidate_root`, only together with a certificate id. This widens a K03 literal; §18 finding 1 records it as the one K03 schema change and its migration.

## 15. What this specification does not establish

- **Correctness of any verifier.** It registers what a correct one must produce; the *measured* figures are floors from a scratch pass that is not shipped.
- **Thermodynamic validity.** Every energy and phase check shares the SYN-001 correlations with the solver ([A09]); the oracle cross-check (A12) is code independence, not data independence. SYN-001 is synthetic and validates nothing empirical (derivation §10).
- **Global uniqueness.** The regularity screen and the solution-error bound are local evidence at one state (§7.7). Other roots are T03's.
- **Generality of the check set.** The balances of §4.3–§4.4 are written for the SYN-001 topology; T01's incidence graph is where a general assembler comes from. The equilibrium-row tolerance (§5.2) is registered for this lifted form.
- **Performance.** A dense SVD at n ≤ 2000 and a 47-column finite difference are affordable here and say nothing at size (ADR 0003 D2.2).
- **Validation of structural over-specification** (STR-03): `unsupported` until K06 (A22).
- **Reproducibility of any float.** What a certificate promises across machines is ADR 0007's; nothing here pins a digest (ADR 0008 D2.1).
- **Human numerical or process-modeling review**: `review.numerical` and `review.process_model` remain `pending`.

Points where the design turns on a judgement only Frank holds. Each has a recommended default so unattended work can proceed; none was decided here.

**F1 — What the project promises, in public, about floating-point reproduction.** The blueprint's R1 promise is "within the declared numerical policy" on the same hardware class, and the measurements say that even two `ubuntu-latest` x86-64 runners differ in the last bits of a converged state. ADR 0007 D3 proposes that the project **reports** bitwise identity when it observes it and **never promises** it — not across platforms, not on one platform, not on one machine — and promises only R0 bit-identity of structure plus agreement within the policy for floats. That is a product promise in the README's voice and the release notes' voice, not a technical fact. *Recommended default:* adopt D3 as written. *Alternative:* promise bitwise reproduction on a pinned single-threaded environment and add it to the gate; it would be measured and might fail on the first BLAS update.

**F3 — Whether `VERIFIED` should promise solution accuracy at all.** §7.4 (amended) reads the authority as it stands: blueprint §8.1's acceptance rule is on residuals and balances, so `VERIFIED` certifies residual accuracy at the registered tolerances and *reports* the first-order solution bound `b` without promising it. On this flowsheet `b` is 145–476 × `τ̂_min` for any state converged to tolerance rather than to roundoff, so a certificate that promised solution accuracy at the registered tolerances would have to demand residuals ~500× tighter than the registered ones, or register solution tolerances of its own. That is a scientific-requirement change (a new acceptance rule beside §8.1's) and only Frank can ask for it. *Recommended default:* no — residual accuracy is the promise, `b` and its statement are the disclosure, and the absolute-conditioning limit is the guard against a bound that means nothing. *Alternative:* register per-kind *solution* tolerances in ADR 0001 D6 and make `b` a required check against them; the twins of §9 would then be re-registered ~500× tighter and the SYN-001 residual tolerances would effectively tighten with them.

**F2 — Whether the registered budget instrument of the fixture registry changes before T06 scores.** ADR 0007 D5 keeps `property_calls` as a hard safety cap (deterministic by construction, A15 exact) and declares it unfit for scoring and for cross-platform comparison, and names the traversal count (`residual_calls`) as the deterministic budget for tests and scoring. Making that the *registered* budget means changing `benchmarks/registry.yaml`'s `budgets` and adding a `SolvePolicy` field (a migration of a K03 schema). Blueprint §13.4 says preregistered budgets change only with versioned evidence and not retroactively; nothing has been scored yet, so the change is not retroactive — but it is a change to a registered fixture value. *Recommended default:* keep the property-call cap as registered for v0.0, record the classification, and add `max_residual_calls` by migration at T06 when the first scoring campaign is registered. *Alternative:* migrate now, in K04's window, before any replay bundle stores a policy hash.
