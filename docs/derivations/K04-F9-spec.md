## 2. The verdict in one paragraph

A Newton solve stops when every row is inside its tolerance, and K04's fresh-flash checks were argued from floors at states converged far below that. At a lifted two-phase stream the fresh flash then disagrees with the state by up to ~200 τ (the lifted block amplifies the equilibrium rows' residual), and at a saturated single-phase product by the kink of a flash across its phase boundary. **The verifier now takes one Newton step of the very matrix its regularity screen factorizes, from the certified state, and judges its fresh-flash categories at that projection**: the first-order error the row tolerance allows is removed, the second-order one remains (≤ 2.1e-5 of the thresholds over the A02 family), every tolerance is unchanged, and every wrong root — a root of the compiled rows by construction — is judged as before. Two smaller rules close what projection cannot: the fresh flash reads a stream within K03's `ε_adm` of saturation as single-phase (the kink at roundoff), and a two-phase split whose band is too narrow for the fresh flash to resolve at double precision is judged by its stored split, as ADR 0012 D7 judges a degenerate one. All 179 A02 roots, DZ-3, DZ-10 and NP-G are then `VERIFIED`; every registered false success is still `FAILED` by ≥ 10³ τ; SYN-001's identity, the `t05` key and the policy hash do not move.

## 4. Derivation: why a state at its row tolerance fails a fresh-flash check

### 4.1 The lifted block

A lifted split at fixed `(n, T, P)` carries `w = (v, l, V, L)` and the rows `R(w)`: `eq_i = v_i L − K_i l_i V` (tolerance `τ_eq = 9.3e-8 (mol/s)²`), `split_i = v_i + l_i − n_i`, `V − Σ v`, `L − Σ l` (each `τ_flow = 3.1e-8 mol/s`); a products-style split has the same rows over its two outlets. The fresh flash is the exact root `w*(n, T, P)`. A state whose rows sit at `e` differs from it by

    w − w* = B⁻¹ e + O(|e|²),      B = ∂R/∂w at (n, T, P),

so the independent split reads `(B⁻¹e)_V` and a heater-style stream's fresh-flash enthalpy differs from the state's by `c·B⁻¹e`, `c = (h^V, h^L, 0, 0)`. T04 registered the worst case over `|e_j| ≤ τ_j` at the 355 K root: 24.33 τ_flow on `S3.V`, 12.67 on a component, 21.03 τ_E on the heater balance (`ref.bound.finding_F9.amplification_at_355K_root` of T04). *Measured* over the A02 family this pass: the worst-case factor on `S3.V` ranges 10.95–208 τ_flow and on the balances 8.8–174 τ_E.

Newton stops with `e` a fraction of the tolerances, so the fresh-flash values land between 0 and a few τ: the twin's family scan gives **155 clear, 9 near threshold, 15 failing, the worst 27.28** (352 K from 360 K) — T04 §17 F9's counts, re-derived (`closed_form.a02_family_projection.raw_counts`); the implementation measures the same.

### 4.2 The saturated product: a kink

A products-style split's outlets are single-phase streams exactly at their saturation points at the root. Let `V = S_V.N`, `L = S_L.N`, and the rows at a state carry `e_i` (equilibrium), `e_V`, `e_L` (the `Ndef` rows). Then, exactly to first order and without `B⁻¹`,

    liquid product:  b = Σ x_i K_i − 1 = (V e_L − L e_V − Σ_i e_i) / (V (L − e_L)) ≈ e_L/L − e_V/V − Σ_i e_i/(V L),

(and the symmetric expression for the vapour product's dew excess). A fresh flash of the product *alone* is single-phase when its excess is `≤ 0` and splits when it is positive; to first order the split is `β = b / Σ x_i (K_i − 1)²` of vapour with composition `K x`, so

    |Hdot_fresh − Hdot_L| = A_L · max(0, b),   A_L = N_L Σ x_i K_i Δh_i / Σ x_i (K_i − 1)²;
    vapour:            A_V = N_V Σ (y_i/K_i) Δh_i / Σ y_i (1 − 1/K_i)².

**DZ-3 and DZ-10** (*measured*): the liquid product S3 is `b = 2.125e-8` past its bubble point at Newton's stop, and `A_L = 6.247e4 W` (twin, `closed_form.kink.DZ-3:S3`), so its fresh flash forms `1.3272e-3 W` of vapour — exactly the `energy_balance.U-PHF` and envelope failure (1.314 τ_E) T05b's stop-and-reports recorded. The envelope sees it again through the purge `S6`, a copy of S3.

### 4.3 The fresh flash's own resolution

At an exact root in double precision the fresh flash still differs from the state by its sensitivity to the last bits of its inputs:

- **Vapour flow against temperature.** Across the band `[T_b, T_d]` of width `w` the flash's vapour flow rises from 0 to `N`, mean slope `N/w`, so one ulp of `T` moves it by `≈ N ulp(T)/w`. At NP-G (`w = 4.1226e-6 K`, `N = 2`, `ulp(360 K) = 5.684e-14 K`) that is **0.8896 τ_flow**; at NP-3 (`w = 2.1419e-4 K`) 0.01712 τ_flow; at every wide mixture registered ≤ 2.7e-6 τ_flow (`closed_form.routing`).
- **The kink at one ulp of excess.** `A · ulp(1)`: NP-G's liquid product 0.205 τ_E, its vapour product 0.027 τ_E, NP-GC's two copies 0.103 τ_E each, NP-3's products ≤ 1.03e-3 τ_E, DZ-3's and SYN-001's ≤ 2.4e-8 τ_E (`closed_form.kink`).

*Measured* at NP-G: after one Newton step toward its root, the independent split reads 0.44 τ_flow and the energy balances −2.78e-3 W (2.75 τ_E) — the floor, not the state. A check at its own floor measures roundoff; its verdict is a platform lottery.

## 5. The rule (normative)

### 5.1 D1 — the verifier's Newton projection

**Where it runs.** In the certificate path only — `verify`, `verify_bound` (`_certify`) and `verify_revision` — after the residual checks and the regularity screen at `x_final`, before the fresh-flash categories. `run_checks` (the primitive that a partial checkpoint's `CheckReport` uses, K04 §8.3) is never projected.

**Preconditions, in this order; the first that fails names the refusal.**

1. Every `residual` check — the compiled rows and every zero-flow label row (T05b §9.3) — passes under the certificate's policy. Else `residual_not_passed`.
2. The regularity status at `x_final` is `NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED`. Else `regularity_<STATUS>` (`RANK_DEFICIENT`, `ILL_CONDITIONED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`).
3. The solve `Ĵ δ̂ = −F̂` with the screen's own factorization (`numerics.linear.factorize`, ADR 0004's options) returns a finite `δ̂`. Else `linear_solve_failed`. (After 2 this is defensive: a matrix the screen passes is factorizable.)

**The map.** `Ĵ` and `F̂` are K04 §7.1's scaled target Jacobian and residual at `x_final` — the declaration's rows minus its certified aliases, by its columns (T04 §4.8 item 3), with every `ZERO_FLOW` split and dormancy-form outlet reduced to its form and its label rows appended (T05b §9.3) — the same pair the screen used, under ADR 0008 D2.4's pairing identity. For every column `j` of `Ĵ`,

    x̃_j = x_final,j + S_x,j · δ̂_j,

except that **a column of kind `molar_flow` that is exactly `+0.0` at `x_final` stays `+0.0`** (after ADR 0001 D1.5's signed-zero normalization). Columns a zero-flow form removed from `Ĵ` keep their `x_final` values. `x̃` is a vector of Python `float`s (a NumPy scalar leaking into a check value breaks canonicalization: finding F5).

*Why the exact zeros are kept.* A flow that is exactly zero at a converged state is structural: an absent component (its rows form a square subsystem in its own columns with zero right-hand side), a pinned side of a single-phase split (at `v = V = 0` the equilibrium and `Vdef` rows have no entry in a free column, and their block in the pinned columns is nonsingular off the bubble point, so its step is zero), or a dormant stream (removed by its zero-flow form). The exact Newton step is zero on all of them; the factorization returns roundoff (*measured* ≤ 2.87e-22 mol/s over the suite) that would turn `V = 0.0` into `V ≠ 0` and a branch's `.bubble` check into `.closure`. *Measured:* projecting without the rule turns SYN-001's nominal `phase_admissibility.S3.bubble` into `.closure` and moves SYN-001's identity.

**Acceptance of `x̃`, in this order.**

4. Every flow of `x̃` is `≥ 0`, and every flowing stream's `(T, P)` lies in the provider's declared domain. Else `projection_outside_domain`.
5. Every compiled row and zero-flow label row at `x̃`, evaluated afresh, passes its tolerance under the certificate's policy. Else `projection_rows_not_passed`.

*(Amended 2026-09-27: ADR 0013 Amendment 2; T07 design note, ruling round 5, M1.)* In guards 1 and 5, "passes under the certificate's policy" means `|value| ≤ ρ_k`, where ρ_k = max(τ_k(policy), τ_k(registered)) and k is the row's kind: its `row_kinds` entry, or `temperature` for a label row. These guards route: they decide where the fresh-flash categories are judged. A policy that only tightens must therefore not move them. At the registered policy ρ = τ, and the guards are bit-identical. A loosened policy gets ρ = its own value.

*(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S5 (2)–(3), as implemented at `ae6c27d`.)* In guard 4, a **flow** is a stream flow — K04 §4.6's flow predicate (`checks.is_flow_column`: `<S>.n.<c>`, `<S>.V`, `<S>.L`, `<S>.N`, `<S>.vap.<c>`, `<S>.liq.<c>`) — not every column of kind `molar_flow`: a reaction extent has that kind and is signed, and a negative extent is not outside any domain. The exact-zero rule above keeps reading the kind (a dormant reactor's extent is a structural `+0.0` too, and X03 bounds every discarded component). In guard 5, a row that cannot be evaluated at `x̃` (the compiled problem or the provider returns a non-`ok` status or a non-finite value) **does not pass**: the refusal is `projection_rows_not_passed`, recorded, and every category is judged at `x_final`. One code for both is deliberate: the certificate's consequence is the same, and a second code would add grammar no consumer reads.

**What is judged where.**

| At `x̃` (when 1–5 hold) | Always at `x_final` |
| --- | --- |
| `energy_balance.*` (unit balances, the envelope, the `energy_balance.enthalpy.<S>` dormant entries) and the enthalpy map every one of them reads; `phase_admissibility.*` (branch checks, `.saturation`, declared ports); `independent_split.*`; the degeneracy test (ADR 0012 D7) and D3's routing that select among them | `residual.*` (rows and labels), `alias_certificate.*`, `material_balance.*`, `specification.*`, `bounds_and_domain.*`, `derivative_witness.*`, the regularity screen and its evidence, `solution_error_bound_scaled`, `target_state_sha256`, `phase_branch`, `branch_found`, the zero-flow selection (T05b §9.3) |

When any of 1–5 fails, every category is evaluated at `x_final` (today's rule, with D2 and D3). The check list's ids and order are unchanged by where a check is evaluated.

**Why this is the right point.** One Newton step from a state whose rows are inside their tolerance is quadratically convergent: `‖F̂(x̃)‖ = O(‖Ĵ⁻¹‖² ‖F̂(x)‖²)`. On the twin's family `‖F̂(x̃)‖∞ ≤ 3.15e-15` from `‖F̂(x)‖∞` up to 9.705e-9, and the S3 values fall from ≤ 27.28 to ≤ 2.107e-5 of their thresholds (`closed_form.a02_family_projection`). The step `‖x̃ − x_final‖` is the first-order solution error K04 §7.4 already discloses: `‖Ĵ⁻¹F̂‖∞ ≤ n ‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ ‖F̂‖∞ = n b`.

### 5.2 D2 — the fresh flash's phase decision at `ε_adm`

`checks.enthalpy_flow` — the one function both engines use for a fresh-flash enthalpy — becomes, for a flowing stream `(n, T, P)` with `z = n/N` and the provider's `K`:

1. `b = Σ_i z_i K_i − 1 ≤ ε_adm` → `Σ n_i h_i^L(T, P)`;
2. else `d = Σ_i z_i / K_i − 1 ≤ ε_adm` → `Σ n_i h_i^V(T)`;
3. else the provider's flash, as today.

`ε_adm = 1e-12` is K03 §8.2's single-phase admissibility tolerance, which K04 §4.7 already applies to every single-phase branch: a stream within it of its boundary is an admissible single phase, and the verifier's own enthalpy now says the same thing its admissibility check says. Where the provider's decision (`≤ 1.0`) already reads the stream single-phase the value is **bitwise unchanged** (the same `evaluate_phase` call). The rule applies wherever `enthalpy_flow` is called — at `x̃` and, when D1 is refused, at `x_final`; it does not apply to the independent split's flash of a split's feed (a feed inside its band is two-phase by definition). No qualification note is added: the rule is part of how the verifier's fresh flash decides a phase, as the provider's `≤ 1.0` was.

*(Ruled 2026-09-25, Q-S5 (1), as implemented at `9912a67`; R-060 amended.)* **The order is the provider's decision first.** The list above, read literally, contradicts its own promise: a stream that the provider reads as vapour (dew excess `≤ 0`) while its bubble excess lies in `(0, ε_adm]` — its whole band within about `ε_adm / (d ln K/dT) ≈ 4e-11 K` of `T`, a pure or near-pure component at `T_sat` — would be read as liquid by step 1, which is not "bitwise unchanged where the provider reads single-phase", and the two readings differ by the whole latent heat. The normative order is therefore: (a) the provider's flash of `(n, T, P)`; if it returns a single phase, its value, bit for bit; (b) only if it splits the stream: liquid when `Σ_i z_i K_i ≤ 1 + ε_adm`, else vapour when `Σ_i z_i / K_i ≤ 1 + ε_adm`, else the provider's two-phase value — each test in exactly the arithmetic of `admissibility_checks` (so the verifier's enthalpy and its own admissibility check decide a phase identically). The two orders differ only at such a stream, which is temperature-degenerate (ADR 0012 D7) and is never routed to `enthalpy_flow` by the revision table; SYN-001's legacy streams are ≥ 17.5 K from one. X20's six cases are unchanged by the ruling.

### 5.3 D3 — unresolved two-phase splits

After the degeneracy test (ADR 0012 D7, which keeps precedence), a flowing lifted split is **unresolved** iff all three hold at the evaluation point:

- its stored branch is `TWO_PHASE` (`V > 0` and `L > 0`);
- its temperature lies inside its feed's band: `T_b ≤ T ≤ T_d`, the verifier's own bisection (`verify/saturation.band_ends`, adjacent doubles);
- `N · ulp(T) / (w · τ_flow) ≥ 1/10`, with `N = Σ n_feed`, `w = T_d − T_b`, `ulp(T)` the spacing of binary64 doubles at `T` (`math.ulp`), `τ_flow` the certificate policy's molar-flow tolerance, `1/10` ADR 0007 D2.4's margin.

An unresolved split is judged by ADR 0012 D7's substitution with two differences: its admissibility is its branch's own check (the two-phase closure, unchanged id) rather than `.saturation` (the split is not degenerate, and its `δ` exceeds `τ_T`); and its streams' qualification reads `enthalpy of <S> from the state's split: fresh flash unresolved (ADR 0013 D3)`. So: enthalpies from the stored split (heater style `Σ v_i h_i^V + Σ l_i h_i^L` at `(T, P)`; products each in its own phase at its own `(T, P)`); `independent_split.<U>.<S>` `not_applicable` with reason `fresh_flash_unresolved` in place of `.total` and `.<c>`. Implemented in the revision table (`verify/table.py`), as D7 is; SYN-001's legacy set has no split within 3.7e4× of the threshold (`ref.closed_form.routing`, X21) and is not changed.

*(Amended 2026-09-27: ADR 0013 Amendment 2; T07 design note, ruling round 5, M1.)* In the third condition, `τ_flow` is the routing tolerance ρ_flow = max(τ_flow(policy), τ_flow(registered)), not the policy's own value.
- **Why.** A policy that tightens `molar_flow` raised the ratio past 1/10 and removed the independent split. Measured at `b36a018`: 24 of 44 corpus revisions at 1e-6 × τ_flow, each `RELAXED`.
- **The rule now.** Such a split is resolved exactly as under the registered policy. Its `.total` and `.<c>` are judged at the tightened τ, and they may fail at their floor. At the registered policy nothing changes.

*Why the criterion is what it is.* It asks whether one ulp of the temperature the fresh flash is given moves its answer by a tenth of the tolerance the answer is judged at (§4.3). *(Amended as above: that tolerance is never taken below the registered one, because the route decides what evidence exists, not how strict it is.)* It registers no constant, and it cannot be met by a pure component off saturation: a zero-width band contains no temperature but `T_sat`, so a pure stream 2e-5 K above it is resolved and judged by the fresh flash (INJ-B2′).

### 5.4 D4 — K04 §4.7's flash-outlet clause (T04 F10)

For revision-built flowsheets the clause is already implemented (`table._split_checks` runs on every lifted split, the TP flash's included). For SYN-001's legacy set it is **discharged by §4.4**: a wrong flash branch is a root of the compiled rows whose products are not at their saturation points, and the fresh flash of S5 (and of its copy S7) reveals it at the full latent heat of the missing vapour. INJ-F10 registers the case: SYN-001 once-through with the flash forced all-liquid (`S4 = 0`, `S5 = S3.n = (1, 1, 1)`, `S7 = S5`) and its duty closed; every compiled row passes, the regularity is `NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED` (`rcond₁` 1.8508e-3, *measured*), the projection is applied and leaves the state where it is, and exactly `energy_balance.flash` and `energy_balance.envelope` fail, each by `−38 338.069 446 743 53 W` (the vapour the fresh flash of S5 forms, 1.3608 mol/s, times its latent heat). The flash outlets' own split at the projection is ≤ 3e-33 τ_flow on every family state (twin) — T04 F10's "179 clear", confirmed at `x̃`. The legacy clause is not added because five new ids on SYN-001's nominal certificate move SYN-001's identity (§12 Q1).

### 5.6 Why this is not a relaxation

1. **No tolerance moves.** Every check is judged at the tolerance it had.
2. **A wrong root is judged exactly as before.** A false success that passes every row is, by construction, a root of the compiled rows: its projection is itself to roundoff (INJ-2: `‖F̂‖` 5.6e-17 scaled; INJ-T1 ≤ 5.6e-37; INJ-F10 7.3e-12 W), so its failing checks and values are reproduced (§7). A state that fails a row is judged at `x_final` (precondition 1), so every residual-failing injection keeps its registered failing set.
3. **What the projection removes is what the residual tolerance admits.** `x̃ − x_final` is the first-order solution error the certificate's residual rows cannot see and K04 §7.4 already discloses (`b`); two roots within it are not distinguished by the certificate's residual rows at their registered tolerances, which the certificate's third statement says.
4. **D2 reads admissible streams as admissible.** A stream within `ε_adm` of saturation passes K03 §8.2's admissibility; beyond it, the fresh flash splits it and the energy checks see the split (INJ-F10's S5 is 0.384 past its bubble point).
5. **D3 drops a comparison only where its floor exceeds a tenth of its tolerance,** and D7's substitution keeps both failure modes it exists for: a wrong branch (a single-phase stored branch inside the band fails its bubble or dew check by the band's excess, ≫ `ε_adm`), and a wrong vapour fraction (the energy balance on the stored split fails at `τ_E`). A wrong root that passes every row cannot lie inside an unresolved band on the wrong branch, because the rows fix the branch (the PH closure's `H(β)` is strictly increasing, ADR 0012 D1).
6. **The old INJ-B2 was not a false success** (finding F1): every compiled row of it is inside its tolerance, it is 2 τ_T from SC-1's exact root, and its solution error is within the disclosed `b`. It is re-registered as PRJ-B2 (`VERIFIED`), and the window's outer side keeps an end-to-end test in INJ-B2′.

## 11. What this specification does not establish

- **Correctness of the implementation.** The *measured* numbers are a prototype's; W0 re-measures them on the implementation.
- **Solution accuracy.** A certificate still certifies residual accuracy at the registered tolerances (K04 §7.4); the projection is an evaluation point for independent comparisons, not a promise about `x_final`'s distance from the root beyond the disclosed `b`.
- **Cross-platform promise of verdicts with a residual flag.** 15 family states, DZ-3, DZ-10, NP-G and PRJ-B2 carry `near_threshold` flags on residual rows at `x_final` (Newton stopped at 0.1–0.4 τ); their verdicts are `VERIFIED` but not promised under ADR 0007 D2.4. That is the rows', not the fresh flash's.
- **States whose projection is refused** (preconditions 2–5 at a converged state): judged at `x_final`, F9's exposure intact — a possible false alarm, never a false pass. No registered state is refused (*measured* 0 of 164 over the suite).
- **Linearly convergent stops** (T04 F3): material envelope sums are judged at `x_final`; PTC's polish covers T04's case.
- **Near-pure heater-style splits in the unresolved band outside any registered case**, and near-pure mixtures at flows far from 2 mol/s: covered by the same rules (D3's criterion scales with `N`), not measured.
- **F10's clause in SYN-001's legacy set as its own checks** (§12 Q1).
- *(Added 2026-09-25, ruling round.)* **Solution accuracy of an EO state from `b`.** X26 (as amended) asserts the realized deviation of the registered comparisons only; `b` is disclosed and bounds nothing at a stated confidence. T05 §11.5's `b ≤ 1e-8` rule for C1–C3 has the form X26 abandoned; C1–C3 meet it and it is not amended here.
- **Empirical validity** of anything (synthetic fixtures); **human numerical or process-modelling review**.

## 13. Findings

- **F1 — INJ-B2 was F9's false alarm registered as a false success** (T05b §12.6). Every compiled row of it is inside its tolerance (energy 0.396 τ_E, equilibrium 0.078 τ_eq); it lies 2 τ_T from SC-1's exact root; the fresh flash says `VAPOR` because the degeneracy window (`τ_T`) is narrower than the temperature the rows pin at that tolerance (~5 µK). A consistent certificate cannot fail it. Re-registered as PRJ-B2; INJ-B2′ keeps the window's outer side. The brief listed INJ-B2 among the false successes to keep catching; this is the reason it is not one.
- **F2 — T05b §13 assumed roundoff convergence** for W0.9 (`b ≤ 1e-8`); DZ-3, DZ-10 and NP-G stop at their row tolerance. Amended (§10.6).
- **F3 — T04 Q8's formula assumed the linear rows exact** ("at any Newton iterate"): a verifier may not assume the solver's method (R-016). With all rows at their tolerance the propagation is larger still, and on any form it puts the check's tolerance on a computed float (§4.4).
- **F4 — K04 §5.4 says the check policy is the tolerance table plus §7's thresholds and the required-check list; `CheckPolicy.sha256` covers the tolerance table only.** Pre-existing; not changed here, and nothing here depends on it: every constant this document uses (`ε_adm`, D2.4's 10) was already outside the hash. For a later K04 revision.
- **F5 — A NumPy scalar in `x̃` breaks canonicalization** (`bool is outside the JSON data model`, measured in the prototype through a `near_threshold` computed on `np.float64`). The projection returns Python floats.
- **F6 — B22's `t05b` identity key already includes DZ-3's certificate with residual flags at 0.12–0.20 τ_eq.** Under D2.4 its verdict is not promised; the flags are `x_final`'s and do not change here. For the design lane's T05b review to note.
- **F7 — T04 F10's "179 clear" holds at `x̃`** (flash outlets ≤ 3e-33 τ_flow, twin).
