## 1. Scope, authority, and what is out of scope

**What T02 delivers.** For a closed declaration, a deterministic **execution plan**: an ordered list of acyclic unit evaluations, tear loops and equation-oriented (EO) regions, each region carrying an unchanged K03-shaped `SolvePlan` (§3). A **recycle solver** for the tear loops: successive substitution with safeguarded Anderson acceleration, every safeguard a registered value (§5), running in the scaled coordinates and under the bounds, the invalid-trial rule and the phase-attempt contract K03 already fixed. A **solver-choice rule** deciding, from the declaration alone, whether a loop is converged by K03's Newton on the tear or by Anderson, and a **merge edge** that re-solves a stalled loop as an EO region when the units support it (§4). The **EO region solve**: K03's Newton core on a region's square system, with the phase policy the lifted form needs so that it cannot land on a trivial root unnoticed (§6). The **cross-unit specification** [A02]: the region rule, its degree-of-freedom validation, its capability failure, and its SYN-001 instance with closed-form expectations (§7). And the registered cases — the five oscillatory and hard recycles Frank required, the auxiliary cases that reach every safeguard, the nested loop, the EO-versus-tear comparison and the A02 variants — with numbered, falsifiable assertions (§8–§10).

**Design invariants.**

1. **Expectations are closed forms or second derivations.** Fixed points, spectra, best damping factors, transient-growth ratios, Krylov grades, duties and phase boundaries are closed forms. The trajectory of the registered policy is a *definition-level* reference: the policy of §5 is implemented a second time, from its statement, at 40 digits, in `t02_reference.py` (as T01's canonical matching was). An implementation of the same policy must reproduce it; a different policy will not. Nothing in the reference file comes from the solver being tested.
2. **The plan is structural.** Every step, every region, every solver choice and every tear set is a function of the declaration, the parameters and the policy — never of an iterate, a Jacobian value or a measured contraction. The plan and the per-step `SolvePlan`s are R0 (ADR 0007 D1).
3. **No accidental zeros, and every branch reached.** REC-05 is registered *because* its spectrum says "contract" while its norm says "grow"; NEST-1 *because* no single edge breaks it; RCY-STALL *because* it has no root; RCY-COEF *because* its secant coefficient is 1e6; the A02 liquid-guess cases *because* they fail. Every safeguard of §5 and every failure path of §6–§7 has a registered case that reaches it (§8, "why each is on the list").
4. **Unsupported is a result.** A loop whose units lack the derivatives the chosen method needs, a region whose units lack EO derivatives, a merge that cannot be made, a phase sequence that cycles — each is a typed outcome naming the object; no path returns silence, and no path invents a nested loop (§4.4, §7.4, §6.3).
5. **Every claim this document makes about its own numbers is executable** in `t02_reference.py --check` (§11.2).

**Out of scope** (brief §10, restated): T03's general phase-attempt controller and ADR 0005 — §6.3 states the interim rule the lifted EO path needs and §6.3.6 says exactly what T03 must add; PTC, homotopy and SER (T04); new unit models (T05); NET-07 and reference comparisons (T06); jobs and bindings (T07); the general revision compiler from manifests (the ADR 0002 D2.7 obligation T01 §19 named still belongs to whichever package first assembles unit rows from manifests, and it is not this one); Wegstein and Aitken (blueprint §7.2: not without measured benefit; none is measured here); trust-region globalization (blueprint §7.3: "when a concrete benchmark family motivates it" — §6.3.6 names the family that does); production code.

## 2. The verdict, in one paragraph

A closed declaration is executed at unit granularity over the process graph: units outside every loop are evaluated in the canonical topological order of the condensation, each process loop is converged as one tear problem over the tear set T01's rule yields when iterated (one edge per round until the loop is acyclic, so a nested loop is one simultaneous tear vector, never a loop inside a loop), and a cross-unit specification promotes the units it couples — with every unit on a process path between them and every loop containing any of them — into one EO region solved by K03's Newton core with upstream values fixed. A loop is converged by K03's Newton on the tear when every unit in it declares exact residual derivatives, by safeguarded Anderson otherwise or on request; Anderson runs in the scaled tear coordinates with depth `min(5, k, n)`, mixing 1, a condition filter at 1e8, a coefficient bound at 1e4, no merit test, K03's bounds and invalid-trial rule, a stagnation rule (five ratios above 0.99) that restarts the history at most twice and then closes the attempt, and an oscillation detector that damps the plain steps; a closed attempt is re-solved as an EO region when the units allow it and is a typed failure when they do not. On the five manufactured recycles Frank required the linear variants terminate at exactly iteration 4 (Krylov grade 3 + 1), the nonlinear variants converge in 7–9 iterations where plain substitution needs 54–535 or never converges, and the non-normal one with the quadratic tail converges to a fixed point that is *not* the designed one — registered as such, with the root pinned to 20 digits. The lifted EO path freezes an active phase set per attempt, removes a phase when its lifted total reaches its bound, and admits a phase only at attempt closure through K03's admissibility check or the kernel's verdict on a failed attempt; on SYN-001 that gives two attempts at nominal and one elsewhere, all agreeing with P01's 20-digit values. The A02 instance inside SYN-001 — heater outlet temperature freed, flash duty specified — is a 42 × 42 region whose closed-form answers are the energy invariant `Q_heater + Q_flash = 56 338.069 446 743 527 W` and the temperature at which the specified flash duty is met; from a two-phase guess it converges in three or four iterations; from the liquid guess it fails honestly by cycling through the single-phase branches, and that failure is registered for T03.

## 3. The execution plan

### 3.1 Inputs, and what is cited rather than recomputed

The plan is built from the `StructuralReport` T01 attaches to every run (T01 §12.2) — its `block_triangular_form`, its `tear` (per loop: units, cycle edges, candidates, chosen stream, tear variables and rows, inner blocks, signature units, or `unsupported: multi_edge_feedback_set`) — plus the revision's specifications, the units' manifests (their derivative declarations, §4.2) and the `SolvePolicy`. The plan builder calls the structural analysis **once** and reads its result; it does not recompute a matching, a block order or a tear (A01). It never evaluates a residual, a Jacobian or a property (A01, spy).

### 3.2 The rule

Let `P` be the process multigraph (T01 §3.1), `C` its condensation, and `U_rows` the units that own at least one row (a sink owns none and has no step).

1. **Steps are units, loops and regions, at unit granularity.** A **step** is one of `evaluate(unit)`, `converge(loop)`, `solve_eo(region)`. `evaluate(unit)` runs the unit's local evaluator with every inlet known (the same evaluator K02's traversal calls); `converge(loop)` runs §4–§5 on the loop's tear problem, evaluating the loop's units in traversal order inside `G`; `solve_eo(region)` runs §6 on the region's square system.
2. **Order.** Steps follow the canonical topological order of `C`: whenever several nodes of `C` have every predecessor placed, the one whose *first unit in declaration order* comes first is placed next (T01 §7.2's rule, on the process condensation). The order is total and R0.
3. **Loops.** Every non-trivial SCC of `P` is one `converge` step whose tear set is §3.3's. A loop is never split into nested loops and no loop is ever "controlled" by another: the tear vector is the concatenation of the torn streams' tear variables, converged simultaneously ([A02]'s "no nested SM secant/control loops" applied to recycles as well as to specifications).
4. **Regions.** Every cross-unit specification (§7.1) defines a region; regions that share a unit are merged; a region absorbs every loop and every `evaluate` step of a unit it contains. A region is one `solve_eo` step placed where its earliest unit would have been placed.
5. **Upstream values are fixed.** A step reads the outlet streams of every earlier step as fixed values (blueprint [A02]: "upstream boundary values can remain fixed from the sequential solve"). Nothing downstream of a step feeds back into it — that is what the condensation guarantees.

**Registered instances** (`ref.syn001.plans`): SYN-001 nominal (and the four other variants, identically): `[evaluate U-FEED, converge {U-MIX, U-HEAT, U-FLASH, U-SPLIT} tear S6 (S6.n.A/B/C; rows SPLIT-recycle:A/B/C; method newton_tear; signature (U-FLASH))]`. Sinks `U-PRODUCT`, `U-PURGE` have no step. The A02 variants: `[evaluate U-FEED, solve_eo {U-MIX, U-HEAT, U-FLASH, U-SPLIT} + SPEC:SPEC-flash-duty (42 × 42; signature (U-HEAT, U-FLASH))]` (§7.3). NEST-1: `[evaluate FEED, converge {A, B, C} tear (s4, s5)]`.

### 3.3 Tear sets for a loop no single edge breaks (T01 → T02)

T01 §9.4 reports a loop no single cycle edge breaks as `tear: unsupported(multi_edge_feedback_set)`. T02's rule, from T01's score: **iterate T01's single-edge rule** — among the loop's cycle edges take the one with least dimension, then least consumer boundary distance, then earliest declaration order; remove it; recompute the SCCs of what remains; repeat until no non-trivial SCC is left. The tear set is the removed edges in removal order; the tear vector is their tear variables concatenated in that order. Each round is a minimum over a finite non-empty set with an injective last key, so the rule is total and R0 (T01 §9.4's argument). It is greedy and does not claim minimality of the feedback set (blueprint §7.2: "simple documented rules"); §12 says so.

**Registered:** NEST-1 (§8): loop `{A, B, C}` with cycle edges `s1, s2, s4, s5`; boundary distances of the consumers `s1 → 2, s2 → 3, s4 → 1, s5 → 2`; round 1 removes `s4`, the remaining loop `{B, C}` has cycle edges `s2 (→ 3), s5 (→ 2)`; round 2 removes `s5`; tear set `[s4, s5]`, dimension 2. The generator verifies that no single edge breaks the loop. The alternate tear set `{s1, s5}` is registered beside it (blueprint §13.2 "alternate tearing"): a policy override `recycle.tear_streams` may name any set that leaves the loop acyclic, and the answer must not change (A18).

### 3.4 The record — `ExecutionPlan`, and why `SolvePlan` does not change

`ExecutionPlan` is a new schema (ADR 0009): `plan_id`, `model_version`, `constants_sha256`, `policy_id`, `structural_report_ref` (the report's `model_version`/`constants_sha256`, so that the plan is checkably built on it), `steps[]` in order, `estimates` (`loop_count`, `region_count`, `largest_region_dimension`). A step carries `kind`, `units` (in traversal order), and for `converge`/`solve_eo` a `solve_plan` that is a K03 `SolvePlan` verbatim — for a loop: the tear partition and the inner block (K03 §3.1) with `signature_units` from T01 §9.6; for a region: `tear_variable_ids = tear_row_ids = []`, `inner_*` = the region's square system after certificates, `signature_units` = every lifted unit in the region (§6.3.1), `scale_provenance`, `bounds`, `initializer_chain` (§6.2, §7.5), `eliminated_rows` (K03 §7's certificates restricted to the region), `estimates` (`inner_dimension`, `jacobian_nnz`). A `converge` step additionally carries `method` (§4.1), `tear_streams`, and `region_on_merge` — the region that would replace it under §4.4, precomputed so that the merge is a plan-time fact and not a run-time invention. A `solve_eo` step built for a specification carries `specification_rows`, `adjusted_variables`, `target_variables`, `removed_specification_rows` (§7.1).

`SolvePlan` keeps its field set and its schema byte for byte. Two of `test_k03_schemas.py`'s rules keep their meaning per step: the inner block is square, and the tear rows match the tear variables (both empty for a region). The whole `ExecutionPlan` is R0 (ADR 0007 D1: ids, integers, orderings; the scales and bounds are declared values). Its R0 projection enters the G05 identity job beside T01's structural report (A02).

## 4. The solver-choice rule and the merge edge

### 4.1 The policy value

`SolvePolicy.recycle.method ∈ {auto, newton_tear, anderson}`, default **`auto`**. The choice is made per loop at plan time and recorded on the `converge` step as `method` (never `auto` on the step: the resolved value).

### 4.2 `auto`

A unit is **EO-capable** iff its `ModelManifest.derivatives` has an entry with `output == "residuals"`, `"free_variables" ∈ with_respect_to`, and `method ∈ {analytic, ad, implicit}`. (`finite_difference` and `unavailable` are not EO-capable; a finite-difference derivative is a test oracle, plan §4.2 K03 constraint.) Every SYN-001 unit with rows declares `residuals / free_variables / ad`; its `outlet.state` sensitivities are declared `unavailable` and are irrelevant here — the tear Newton differentiates the assembled rows, not the evaluators (K03 §2).

`auto` resolves to **`newton_tear`** iff every unit of the loop is EO-capable, and to **`anderson`** otherwise. The rule is structural (a declaration), deterministic and R0. **Rejected:** a measured contraction estimate (blueprint §7.1 keeps "recorded estimates" apart from "structural rules"; an estimate is an iterate-dependent number, so a plan built on it is not R0 and, *measured* on REC-05, a spectral estimate says "contract at 0.5" for a map whose residual grows 8.7× — the estimate would choose wrongly on exactly the case it exists for).

### 4.3 What each method is

`newton_tear` is K03 §2–§5 unchanged: damped Newton on `R(t)` with the Schur-complement derivative, K03's line search, bounds, budgets and phase contract. `anderson` is §5. Both use the same tear partition, scales, tolerances (K03 §5.2 on the unscaled tear rows), bounds (ADR 0001 D3.5), invalid-trial rule (K03 §5.4–§5.5: shortened, not clipped, not retried; the evaluator's typed non-`ok` status is recorded) and phase-attempt contract (K03 §9: signature frozen per attempt, `phase_update_required` rejections halved, wall patience 2, restart from the largest-α phase-rejected trial, one attempt per signature, at most 5 attempts).

### 4.4 The merge edge (blueprint §7.7 "recycle stagnation → … merge into EO"; plan §4.3 recovery minimum, edge 2)

**Trigger:** a `converge` step's attempt closes with `RECYCLE_STAGNATION`, `LINE_SEARCH_FAILED`, `BOUND_BLOCKED` or `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(recycle_iterations)` under `anderson` (under `newton_tear` the same closures are K03's and no merge is attempted: a Newton that has stalled with exact derivatives will not do better as the same Newton on a larger system). **Precondition:** every unit in the loop is EO-capable. **Action:** the step's `region_on_merge` (§3.4) is solved by §6 from the attempt's best iterate — the accepted iterate of least scaled residual norm, reconstructed to the region's full state by one traversal — with the phase set the kernel reports there. **Maximum count:** one merge per loop per solve. **Outcome:** the region's. **When the precondition fails:** the solve ends with the attempt's outcome and `merge_into_eo: unsupported(unit, "derivatives: <method>")` on the closing event and in the failure bundle. No secant, no control loop, no damping sweep is invented (blueprint [A02]'s last sentence, applied to recycles).

Evidence that fixed physics and specifications did not change across the edge (plan §4.3): the region's `model_version` and `constants_sha256` equal the loop's, and its rows are the loop's rows plus nothing — the merge changes the *solver*, not the *problem* (A32).

**Registered bites:** RCY-DIV (merge → one Newton step to `t*`), RCY-COEF (merge → one Newton step to `t = −1e6`), RCY-STALL (merge → `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED(exactly_singular)`), and a loop with one non-capable unit (test double) → `unsupported` (A32).

## 5. The recycle policy — successive substitution with safeguarded Anderson acceleration

Written for a tear vector `t ∈ ℝⁿ`, tear residual `R(t) = G(t) − t`, column scales `S_t` and row scales `S_R` from K03 §4 (by kind; a tear variable and its tear row have the same kind, so `S_R = S_t` componentwise — asserted, A03), lower bounds `lo` (0 for component flows, ADR 0001 D3.5; absent for a synthetic map that declares none), and the registered acceptance rule per row (K03 §5.2). Constants are `SolvePolicy.recycle` fields (ADR 0009) with the registered defaults of §5.10, policy id `T02-recycle-policy-v1`.

### 5.1 Coordinates

    x̂ = t / S_t,     f̂(x̂) = R(t) / S_t.

Every least-squares problem, every norm and every ratio below is in these coordinates (D06: scales before anything numerical; K03 §4.4: frozen within an attempt). SCL-1 (§8) is registered *because* a non-uniform scale changes the trajectory (iterate 2's residual 0.023 137 against 0.064 883 unscaled) while leaving the answer and the termination count unchanged.

### 5.2 Convergence, tested first

At every iterate, before any step: converged iff every tear row satisfies `|R_i| ≤ a_i + r_i s_i` (K03 §5.2; for SYN-001 `τ_R = 3.1e-8 mol/s`, scaled `1.033e-8`). An already valid root converges at iteration 0 (SYN-001 once-through and 420 K, A04).

### 5.3 The Anderson step (type II, Walker–Ni form)

History `(x̂_j, f̂_j)`, `j ≤ k`. Effective depth

    m_k = min(depth_max, k, n),   depth_max = 5.

The cap at `n` is not cosmetic: beyond `n` columns the difference matrix is rank-deficient in exact arithmetic, the least-squares coefficient is not unique, and two correct implementations could legitimately differ — the generator's own twin refused the system at iteration 4 of every nonlinear 3-variable case until the cap was written. `n` columns is the Krylov dimension and everything a linear map can use. With `m_k ≥ 1`:

    ΔF = [f̂_k − f̂_{k−1}, …, f̂_{k−m_k+1} − f̂_{k−m_k}]   (newest column first),   ΔX likewise,
    γ = argmin ‖f̂_k − ΔF γ‖₂,
    x̂_{k+1} = x̂_k + β f̂_k − (ΔX + β ΔF) γ,        β = 1.

With `m_k = 0` (the first step, or after every column has been dropped): the **plain step** `x̂_{k+1} = x̂_k + β_sub f̂_k` (§5.6 sets `β_sub`).

### 5.4 The two column safeguards (blueprint: "regularization … and coefficient safeguards")

Before the least squares is accepted, in this order and repeated until both pass or `m_k = 0`:

- **Condition filter.** `κ₂(ΔF) = σ_max/σ_min` (SVD of the `n × m_k` matrix). If `σ_min = 0` or `κ₂ > condition_max = 1e8`: drop the **oldest** column, `m_k ← m_k − 1`, record `column_dropped(condition)`.
- **Coefficient bound.** If `‖γ‖∞ > coefficient_max = 1e4`: drop the oldest column, record `column_dropped(coefficient)`.

Why filtering and not Tikhonov: a ridge term changes `γ` even when the problem is well posed, so it would make the finite-termination assertion of §5.9 false by design; dropping a column when it carries no information keeps the exact-arithmetic behaviour on every well-posed step. Why these values: `κ₂ ε_mach ≈ 1e-8` is the relative error the least squares can leave in `γ` at the limit, which is the scaled tear tolerance — a column that costs more than that adds nothing the tolerance can see; on the registered linear cases the largest κ₂ is 1 372 (SCL-1) and the largest ‖γ‖∞ is 18.9 (SCL-1), a decade and more inside the limits, which `--check` asserts so that no registered case sits in ADR 0007 D2.4's near-threshold band. Where they bite: RCY-STALL (every column has `σ_min = 0`; 27 condition drops, no Anderson step ever taken), RCY-COEF (the secant coefficient at `k = 1` is `(1 + 1e-6)/1e-6 = 1 000 001`; 12 coefficient drops).

### 5.5 Acceptance: any valid trial in the attempt's signature

The trial `t_{k+1} = S_t x̂_{k+1}` is taken through K03 §5.3's bound-aware `α_max` with exact landing (`+0.0`), and K03 §5.4's rejection rules: a typed non-`ok` evaluation → `invalid_trial`, halve `α`, at most `step_halvings_max = 20`, then `LINE_SEARCH_FAILED`; a signature other than the attempt's → `phase_update_required`, K03 §9.3 verbatim. **There is no merit test.** An accepted trial may have a larger residual than the iterate it came from. Reason, measured and registered: on the non-normal REC-05 the residual rises from 0.217 to 0.617 to 0.640 over the first two Anderson steps and the iteration then terminates at 4; on REC-03 with the quadratic tail it rises at iterations 4–5 (0.006 53 → 0.007 10 → 0.007 30) and converges at 7. An Armijo condition on an accelerator would reject exactly the steps that make it one; the stagnation rule of §5.6 is what bounds a run that grows without ever converging. Bounds, and the evaluator's refusal, are the only reasons a trial is not taken.

### 5.6 Stagnation, restart, and giving up (blueprint §7.7 "recycle stagnation → restart acceleration, damp … or merge"; plan §4.3 edge 1)

Over accepted iterates, `q_k = ‖f̂_k‖∞ / ‖f̂_{k−1}‖∞`. When `q > stagnation_ratio = 0.99` for `stagnation_window = 5` consecutive accepted steps while unconverged: a **stagnation closure**. The first `max_restarts = 2` closures **restart**: the history is cleared, the window counter reset, the iterate kept (`restart` event, `reason: stagnation`, `count`). The third closes the attempt **`RECYCLE_STAGNATION`**, and §4.4's merge edge applies. `q > 1` counts: a residual that grows is a residual that is not shrinking. That is why no separate divergence detector is registered — on RCY-DIV (ρ = 1.5, real positive, no bounds) the windows close at 6, 11 and 16 and the attempt ends with the residual grown by `1.5¹⁶ = 657×` (closed form `‖f̂_k‖ = 0.25 · 1.5^k / 3`), and on REC-05 with the tail the windows close at 10, 15, 20 with 17 residual increases; a 1e4 factor would fire at 23 and 30 respectively, later than the rule that already exists, while any factor small enough to fire first would misfire on REC-05's 8.7× transient. The window of 5 absorbs a two-step hump (REC-05 linear: no closure, A13) and catches every registered stall within 15–20 iterations.

**Oscillation detector and the damping response** (blueprint §7.2 "detect oscillation"). At iterate `k` let `i` be the index of the largest `|f̂_{k,i}|`. A **flip** is `f̂_{k,i} · f̂_{k−1,i} < 0` with both above the scaled tolerance. `oscillation_window = 3` consecutive flips → `oscillation_detected` (once per attempt, recorded with `k`), and the plain-step damping becomes `β_sub = β_substitution_oscillating = 0.5` for the rest of the attempt. It is a response for the *plain* steps only: an Anderson step already accounts for the sign, and REC-01 with depth 2 records a detection at iteration 7 with no effect on its Anderson steps (A20). Where it bites: REC-01 and REC-02 under plain substitution (depth 0), detection at iteration 3 (closed form: the dominant component is the first from `k = 1` on and alternates every step), after which `(I + A)/2 = diag(0.05, 0.75, 0.6)` for REC-01 and `diag(−0.25, 0.75, 0.6)` for REC-02 and both converge at iteration 54; REC-02's plain substitution *without* the response never converges (its first component cycles between `0` and `2.5` on the bound, *measured*). Where it must not fire: REC-04, a rotation — the dominant index changes and the flip count resets (registered: never fires in 348 iterations).

### 5.7 Budget

`max_iterations_per_attempt = 200` accepted iterations per attempt → `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(recycle_iterations)`, then §4.4. K03's property-call budget and attempt budget apply unchanged.

### 5.8 What is recorded (ADR 0009's additions to `SolveEvent`)

One `acceleration` event per Anderson or plain step: `depth_used` (`m_k` after filtering), `columns_dropped_condition`, `columns_dropped_coefficient`, `kappa_2` (of the accepted least squares, or `null` for a plain step), `gamma_inf`, `beta_substitution` in force, `oscillation_flag`. One `restart` event per restart with `reason ∈ {stagnation}` and `count`. `trial`/`step_accepted` as in K03 with `alpha`. Outcomes `RECYCLE_STAGNATION` and `CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE` added; rejection reasons unchanged. Reproducibility classes (ADR 0007 D2.3, added by this package): `depth_used`, the two drop counts, `count`, `oscillation_flag` are R0; `kappa_2` and `gamma_inf` are R1/R2 floats compared relatively, with no floor, on events whose scaled residual `‖f̂_k‖∞ ≥ 1e-4` and recorded uncompared below it (ADR 0009 D3 as amended 2026-09-24: their cross-platform agreement scales as `ε/‖f̂_k‖`, measured, so a constant floor cannot describe them; the floors `1.0`/`1e-12` first written here are withdrawn). `beta_substitution` on an event is the damping *applied to that step* — the plain step's `β_sub` in force, or the Anderson step's own mixing `β = 1` — which is what A20 reads.

### 5.9 What can be asserted about an accelerator, and what cannot

For a linear map `G(t) = A t + b` with `I − A` nonsingular, Anderson with `m_k = k`, `β = 1` and no safeguard firing is equivalent to GMRES on `(I − A) t = b` (Walker and Ni, 2011): `x^AA_{k+1} = G(x^GMRES_k)`, so the Anderson residual vanishes one iteration after the GMRES residual does, which is at the Krylov grade of `r_0` under `A` — the number of distinct eigenvalues present in `r_0`, at most `n`. The registered linear variants all have grade 3, so **termination at exactly iteration 4** is a theorem, and `--check` verifies both the grade and the termination in 40-digit arithmetic. What floating point does to it: *measured* on the prototype, the residual norms of iterates 0–3 agree with the 40-digit twin to `4.4e-16` absolute (scaled) and the terminating residual is `≤ 1.3e-15` on four cases and `6.2e-13` on REC-05 (κ₂ = 4e4 on its last least squares). The registered allowance: iterates 0–3 to `1e-9` relative (six decades over the floor; a wrong column sign, a type-I update, an unscaled least squares or a Tikhonov term changes iterate 2 by `≥ 1e-2`), and the count exactly 4 with the converged residual under the tolerance (which the floor clears by four decades). For a nonlinear map nothing terminates; what is registered is the twin's trajectory through the first six iterates (to `1e-8` relative while the residual exceeds `1e-6`, below which roundoff of the map's own evaluation dominates), the count within `+2` of the twin's, the events (no restart, no drop, no landing) exactly, and the landing point.

## 6. The equation-oriented region solve

### 6.1 The system

A region's square system is its units' rows minus the certified redundant rows (K03 §7 / T01 §8.2, restricted to the region), over its units' free variables, with every upstream stream fixed at the value the earlier steps produced (§3.2.5). Scales, bounds (`≥ 0` on every `molar_flow` column including the lifted `v_i, l_i, V, L`), the row tolerances by kind (ADR 0001 D6; `molar_flow_squared` per K04 §5.2: `τ = 9.3e-8 (mol/s)²`), the SuperLU configuration (ADR 0004) and the Newton core (K03 §5, including its outcomes) are K03's, unchanged. The compiled problem is the assembly's; the region's residual and Jacobian are its rows and columns selected by id (never by position).

**Registered instance:** SYN-001 under `recycle.method: eo` — an override that promotes every loop to a region, registered so that the EO path is exercised on the same flowsheet K03 solves by the tear — `[evaluate U-FEED, solve_eo {U-MIX, U-HEAT, U-FLASH, U-SPLIT}]`: 44 rows minus 2 certified = 42 over 47 − 5 = 42 columns, `signature_units = (U-HEAT, U-FLASH)`.

### 6.2 Initialization

The region's initializer chain is K03 §10's applied to the region: a user guess for any free variable that has one (§7.5), else the **sequential pre-solve** — the plan run with every region replaced by its tear/evaluate steps (for a specification region: with the adjusted variable pinned at its guess, §7.5), whose reconstructed state (K03 §3.3) is the region's `x⁰`. Every lifted split in `x⁰` is set from the kernel at its stream's `(n, T, P)` (K03 §8.3); a supplied split that disagrees is overwritten and the overwrite logged on K03's precedent for a projected guess (K03 A08): an `initializer_candidate` event whose structured message is `projected(<stream>, <supplied branch>, <kernel regime>)`, followed by the `initializer_accepted` event — no new event kind — and the registered case EO-TRIV (§8) exists to see it.

### 6.3 The phase policy on the lifted EO path (interim; T03 generalizes)

K03 §9 fixed the contract for the tear path, where the residual selects phases itself. On the lifted EO path the phases are *variables*, and R-010's warning applies: both trivial branches are exact roots of every row, and a bound-aware Newton lands on them freely. *Measured* on a plain bounded Newton over the 47-row system: started on the all-liquid branch of the once-through heater outlet (`Σ x K = 1.070`), it converges in one iteration to that inadmissible root with `|R| = 2e-16`; started at the nominal initializer, it ends `BOUND_BLOCKED` at iteration 1: the iteration-0 step has `α_max = 0.093 416 794 4` set by the vapor *component* `S3.vap.B`, which lands exactly on `0.0` while the vapor total stays at `4.427e-5 mol/s` (`vap.A = 3.82e-5`, `vap.C = 6.05e-6`, `L = 3.896`), and at iteration 1 the direction points out of the feasible set (*measured* by the Opus session on the implementation, 2026-09-24, and by the prototype). The iterate sits at `s = 0.5467` on the ray through `t*`, and the closed-form point where the heater outlet turns liquid is `s_flip = 0.546 818 841 362 984 79`. The rules below are what those two measurements demand and no more.

1. **Signature.** For each lifted unit in the region, its **active phase set** `∈ {TWO_PHASE, LIQUID, VAPOR}`. `LIQUID` pins `v_i = V = 0` (the columns leave the free set) and drops its three equilibrium rows and `Vdef` (identically satisfied); `VAPOR` symmetrically pins `l_i = L = 0` and drops the equilibrium rows and `Ldef`; `TWO_PHASE` keeps everything. The system stays square. The attempt signature is the tuple over the region's lifted units in declaration order; the flash's regime enters through the same rule (T01 §9.6's ancestry rule places every lifted unit of a region in its signature, because in a region every lifted block is solved, not read).
2. **Opening.** The first attempt's signature is the kernel's regime at `x⁰` for every lifted stream (§6.2).
3. **Disappearance, during an attempt, by the bound.** A **lifted variable of an active phase** — its total (`V` or `L`) while the stream's total `N > 0`, *or* one of its components (`v_i` or `l_i`) while the stream's component `n_i > 0` — that *reaches its bound* — exact landing at `α_max` accepted as an iterate (K03 §5.3), or `α_max = 0` with the direction pointing outward — closes the attempt **`PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED`** with `reason: phase_disappeared(unit, phase, variable)`. The restart point is the current iterate with that phase pinned (`v = 0, V = 0, l = n, L = N`, or symmetrically); the new signature removes the phase. **Premise, declared:** for an equilibrium model whose K-values are finite and positive on its domain — the SYN-001 provider's class, `K_i = (P_r/P) exp(…) > 0` everywhere — every component of a flowing phase is strictly positive at a two-phase root (`y_i = K_i x_i` with `x_i > 0` whenever `n_i > 0`), so a phase component at zero with its stream component positive is not a state the two-phase branch can hold: the phase is leaving, whether the Newton says so through the total or through a component first. *Measured*, nominal: it says so through `S3.vap.B`, which lands on `0.0` at iteration 0's step while `V = 4.4e-5` remains, and attempt 1 therefore closes at iteration 1 (A21). No kernel is consulted: at the landing iterate the kernel's verdict is a hair's breadth either way (*measured*: `Σ z K − 1 ≈ +1e-5` at `s = 0.5467`), and the bound is the fact. The one lifted-component case that is *not* a phase change is `n_i = 0` — a component absent from the stream (ADR 0001 D3.3), where `v_i = l_i = 0` is the state; no registered case reaches it (§12), and a provider declaring a component that can vanish from one phase while present in the stream (`K_i = 0` or `∞`) is outside this premise and is T03/T05's to rule (§6.3.6).
4. **Appearance, at attempt closure only.** (a) On `CONVERGED`: K03 §8.2's admissibility check on every lifted stream; `INADMISSIBLE(stream, branch, value)` converts the outcome to `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED` with the admissible signature, and the restart point is the converged iterate with that stream's split re-initialized from the kernel. (b) On `STAGNATION`, `LINE_SEARCH_FAILED`, `BOUND_BLOCKED` or `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(newton_iterations)`: the kernel's regime at the attempt's best iterate is compared with the active set; if it differs, the closure is converted to `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED` with the kernel's regime and split; if it agrees, the failure stands. No per-trial kernel test: a trial is never rejected for the kernel's opinion of it (R-012's lesson — a selection that does not change the residual must not freeze an attempt — applied in the other direction: a verdict that changes with every trial must not steer one).
5. **Cycle rule and budget.** K03 §9.4 verbatim: a signature is attempted at most once per solve (`ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING` otherwise), at most 5 attempts.
6. **What T03 must add**, and the registered case that will judge it: *appearance detected during an attempt*, so that an attempt whose single-phase branch has no root in the provider domain does not have to fail before the phase can appear; and a *restart into the adjacent regime* when a step overshoots two regime boundaries at once (the largest-α phase-rejected trial of K03 §9.3 lands in the far regime). The registered cases `SYN-001-A02-360-liquid-guess` and `-355-liquid-guess` (§7.6) fail honestly under rules 1–5 and their expectations are T03's to re-register as successes. This is also the "concrete benchmark family" blueprint §7.3 asks for before a trust-region globalization is added: the single-phase Newton step from 350 K aims at a liquid-branch root of 442.64 K, through the whole two-phase band `[351.444, 377.529] K`.

### 6.4 EO versus tear — the registered agreement

Two independent routes to the same state: K03's tear solve (its own solver, its own path, already gated against P01) and the EO region solve of §6.1 from the sequential initializer `x(t⁰)`. Their agreement is asserted against **P01's 20-digit values**, not against each other, per quantity kind: flows `3.1e-7 mol/s`, temperatures `1e-5 K`, pressures `0.1 Pa`, duties `1e-2 W` — ten times the residual tolerance of each kind. Argument: a Newton that meets a residual tolerance `τ` leaves a state error bounded by `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖ τ̂`; with `κ₂ ≤ 1 440` on the scaled system (K03 §4.3, *measured*) and the quadratic convergence that overshoots `τ` by orders (*measured* prototype residuals `≤ 5.8e-11` unscaled at convergence), the bound is below `1e-8 mol/s`, thirty times under the allowance; the smallest defect a wrong branch or a wrong row would make is `0.30 mol/s` (the heater outlet's vapor total at nominal, which must be exactly `0` on the liquid branch). *Measured* attempt and iteration counts on the plain bounded Newton with the active-set restart applied by hand: nominal 2 attempts (1 + 2 iterations), high-recycle 1 attempt (5), 310 K 1 (2), once-through and 420 K iteration 0. The high-recycle case has the heater outlet liquid at both ends of its ray (`s_flip = 0.028 78 < 0.05`, the initializer), which is why it needs one attempt where nominal needs two; both facts are closed-form and registered.

The second half of the agreement is the *same function* check: the EO region started from the tear solve's reconstructed state converges at iteration 0 with no Jacobian evaluated (A23) — the residual K04 verifies is the residual the region solves.

## 7. The cross-unit specification [A02]

### 7.1 The region rule

A specification is **cross-unit** when its target variable is owned by a unit other than the one owning the variable it frees (T01 §5.1's promotion row targets a column; the *adjusted* variable is the one whose own specification row is removed — here `HEAT-T`, so `S3.T` is free). Its **region** is the set of units consisting of: the target's owner, the adjusted variable's owner, every unit on a directed path of the process graph between them (either direction), and every process loop containing any of those; plus the specification row itself. Regions sharing a unit merge. Rows: all rows of the region's units (with the removed specification row gone and the promotion row added), minus the certified redundant rows among them. Columns: all free variables the region's units own. Upstream units are evaluated first and fixed (§3.2.5). **A pair whose adjusted variable and target share an owner is a region too** (amended 2026-09-24, review M1): that unit and every loop containing it. Blueprint [A02]'s "the unit's evaluator serves it" presumes a local solver for the freed outlet, which no v0.1 unit has and no manifest declares; until a manifest declares one, a freed outlet is never traversed around — *measured*: with the shortcut, freeing `S3.T` and specifying the heater's own duty ran as a plain tear and reported `CONVERGED` at the guess with the duty unmet by 6 381 W.

**Why unit granularity and not the BTF block.** *Measured* through T01 (brief §3.3): the BTF puts `S3.T` into the heater's lifted block together with `FLASH-duty` (a 9-block) *downstream* of the 17-block recycle, so at the equation level the recycle could be converged first and the 9-block solved after. But a unit whose specified outlet is freed has no local evaluator any more: the heater cannot be traversed without `S3.T`, so the recycle's `G(t)` cannot be formed by the unit machinery this architecture has, and the loop must be solved *with* the region. Blueprint [A02] says exactly this: "and any connected recycle SCC needed for closure". The equation-level region is not foreclosed — it is what a block-granular executor (T06 or later, if the cost of the 42-variable region ever matters) would recover — and it is listed in §12.

### 7.2 Degrees of freedom after promotion (blueprint: "validate its degrees of freedom after promotion")

Structural, at plan time, before any numerics: T01's discriminator (T01 §8.1) on the region's rows and columns with the upstream columns treated as parameters. `STRUCTURALLY_CLOSED` after certificates is required; `STRUCTURAL_OVER_SPECIFICATION` (the adjusted variable's own specification row was not removed — the registered conflicting shape, A26), `STRUCTURAL_UNDER_SPECIFICATION` (a specification removed with no promotion row: `S3.T` free and nothing pinning `U-FLASH.Q`), and `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT` are T01's findings, reported by validation, and no plan is built. ## 12. What this specification does not establish

- **Correctness of any solver.** It registers what a correct one must produce; the *measured* figures are prototype observations (a numpy Anderson of the registered policy on the manufactured maps and on the real traversal; a plain bounded K03 Newton on the 47-row and 42-row systems with the active-set restart applied by hand) and are not shipped.
- **Minimality of a tear set** for a multi-edge feedback set (§3.3 is greedy); **the quality of a tear** beyond dimension and boundary distance (T01 Q3; T06 measures coupling).
- **Behaviour when the registered policy's basin structure is finer than the registered margin.** REC-05's `S1` landing is asserted with a ±5 % start margin; a fourth fixed point at `γ = 0.02` exists and is not registered; the merge edge's landing on REC-05 (A14) is recorded, not asserted.
- **Any trajectory beyond the iterates registered.** Anderson trajectories below a residual of `1e-6` are roundoff-dominated (*measured*: a truncated window on a 6-variable non-normal map deviates from the twin by `2e-4` relative at iteration 14) and are deliberately not registered; a six-variable truncation case was examined and rejected on that ground (§15.4).
- **Appearance of a phase during an EO attempt, or the adjacent-regime restart** (§6.3.6): the two liquid-guess A02 cases are registered failures, and their success is T03's evidence.
- **A lifted component at zero because the stream lacks that component** (`n_i = 0`, §6.3.3's excepted case), **a provider whose K-values can vanish or diverge** (outside §6.3.3's premise), a **saturated lifted stream** (K03 §16), and **more than one region or loop per plan** on a registered flowsheet (SYN-001 has one loop; NEST-1 has one loop): the rules are stated, no registered case exercises them beyond the synthetic.
- **The equation-level (BTF-block) region** for A02 (§7.1): stated as recoverable, not delivered.
- **Wegstein, Aitken, trust regions, Krylov linear solves**: blueprint §7.2/§7.3's later methods; nothing measured here motivates them, and REC-03 is the case that shows a scalar damping cannot substitute for acceleration.
- **Empirical validity** of anything: every case is synthetic (the addendum's last section says why a physical oscillating recycle waits for T05 and T06).
- **Human numerical or process-modeling review**: `review.numerical` and `review.process_model` remain `pending`; no agent sets them.
- **Cost at size**: 47 variables and 3-variable tears measure nothing about a plant (T01 review S9 stands: measure before the first large flowsheet).

## 15. Findings about the brief, the addendum, the plan and the ADRs

1. **REC-05 with the tail has more than one fixed point, and the registered start is in the basin of the wrong one** (addendum §"The five cases"; brief §1 question 4). Under the registered policy — and under the addendum's own unsafeguarded Anderson(3), which the twin matches iteration for iteration — the iteration from `1.1 t*` converges in 9 iterations to `S1 = (3.888 736…, 2.576 892…, 3.111 099…)`, not to `t*`; starts from `1.05 t*` to `1.15 t*` all land there. A weaker tail (`γ = 0.05`) sends `1.1 t*` to `t*` but then plain substitution no longer diverges from that start, and `1.12 t*` lands on a third fixed point. The quadratic tail that makes substitution diverge is the same tail that manufactures roots. The case is kept exactly as defined and registered as the recycle multiple-root case with `S1` pinned; the addendum's table row "Anderson m = 3: 11" was a count to *some* root, and the addendum should say which.
2. **The depth must be capped at the tear dimension** (§5.3). Not in the brief, not in the addendum; found by the twin's refusal of a rank-deficient least squares at iteration 4 of every 3-variable nonlinear case. An implementation using `lstsq`'s minimum-norm solution would have "worked" and been irreproducible.
3. **A separate divergence detector is redundant with the stagnation rule** (§5.6) once growth counts as non-progress, and any factor small enough to precede it misfires on REC-05. Brief 4.3's "restart when … the scaled residual grows" is the rule this document rejects with a measurement.
4. **A six-variable non-normal truncation case is not registrable.** With a superdiagonal of 2.0 the truncated window reaches `κ₂ = 2e9` and `‖γ‖∞ = 2.9e4` and the double-precision trajectory departs from the 40-digit twin by `2e-4` relative before termination; with a diagonal spectrum of six eigenvalues the departure is still `1.4e-4` at iteration 25. A specification demanding agreement there would be the finite-difference mistake this repository has already paid for once. RCY-TRUNC (depth 2 on a 3-variable case) exercises truncation at a registrable floor.
5. **The blueprint's phase-attempt contract needs two mechanisms on the lifted EO path that K03 did not need on the tear path** (§6.3): a bound-driven disappearance (measured necessary: without it the nominal EO solve is `BOUND_BLOCKED` at iteration 1) and an admissibility- or kernel-driven appearance at closure (measured necessary: without it a forced trivial branch converges in one iteration to an inadmissible root). Per-trial appearance detection and the adjacent-regime restart are named for T03 with registered cases (§6.3.6). ADR 0005 remains pre-allocated for T03; K03 §9 plus §6.3 here is the interim normative text (register entry R-012's successor should point at both).
6. **Plan §4.3's "downstream-T" wording** — F1 above.
7. **`SolvePolicy`'s `additionalProperties: false` turns every new solver constant into an ADR.** Right for a frozen interface and the reason ADR 0009 exists; recorded so that no later package "adds a field" without one.
8. **The K03 tear path and every K03 assertion are untouched** (A35). `SolvePlan` is unchanged; the Newton core, scales, elimination and phase contract are reused, not copied; the one K03 rule this document *extends* is the meaning of a bound landing on a *lifted* phase total, which K03 never had.
9. **The addendum's physics section is agreed with** and is why every recycle case here is synthetic; NET-07 (T06) remains the first physical oscillating loop.
10. **The first version of §6.3.3 fired only on a phase *total*, and the registered nominal case leaves the two-phase branch through a *component*** (Opus session, implementation measurement, 2026-09-24: `S3.vap.B` lands on `0.0` at `α_max = 0.093 416 794 4` while `V = 4.4e-5` remains; then `BOUND_BLOCKED`). My prototype had seen the same iterate and I read it as the total's landing. The amended rule covers every lifted variable of the phase and states the premise that makes a zero component a phase change (finite positive K-values). A rule can be wrong and agree with the number in front of you; the M4 lesson of T01, again.
