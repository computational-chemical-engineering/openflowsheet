## 2. The verdict, in one paragraph

PHS-05 fails because a Newton step from 375 K overshoots the lifted split of the heater; the step is driven by the *specification*, not by any inventory, so the fix is to move the specification, not to slow the inventories. **Specification continuation** re-binds the promoted specification's pinned value from where the start already satisfies it (λ = 0) to its target (λ = 1); each λ-level is a real flowsheet with a distinct `constants_sha256`, the λ = 1 level is the target instance itself, and K03's Newton core corrects from the last accepted root. On PHS-05 it takes three λ-trials with no rejection; across a 180-run A02 family scan it rescues 9 of the 10 contract failures and stops the tenth with a typed `HOMOTOPY_STALLED` that brackets the dew point on its path within 2⁻⁹ in λ. **Edge 3** runs it once, after a region solve ends in a globalization failure, from that solve's own starting point. The **PTC family** — the SYN-001 flash and heater with holdups equal to a common residence time times their phase-resolved outflow content — passes every check route (a) of blueprint §7.5 asks for: steady-state equivalence, dimensions, the ADR 0008 row-sign map and `M` pattern, reference invariance of the step, and local attraction, with the loop's six inventory modes in closed form (`s = (−1 ± √(r d))/θ`, `d ∈ {1, d₂, 0}`), all negative for every `r < 1`. But it **improves no tested basin**: at r = 0.95, damped Newton and PTC converge from all 18 admissible off-ray starts of the registered lattice, PTC at 1.9–8.8 times the cost. By structure it also cannot help PHS-05 — the heater temperature there is an algebraic variable, and a linearly implicit pseudo-step Newton-solves every algebraic row. It is therefore retained **experimental** and V14's qualified-PTC clause is recorded incomplete, as the plan directs.

## 4. The typed homotopy (normative; ADR 0010 D2)

### 4.1 Type and applicability

**Type `specification_continuation`.** Applicable to a region with at least one *promoted specification row* (T02 §7.3: `SolvePlan`/`ExecutionPlan` step `specification_rows`), each of the written form `v_j − p_j` (`binding._promotion_row`), where `v_j` is a column of the region and `p_j` a pinned input. The continued inputs are exactly those `p_j`. No other pinned input moves (feeds, set temperatures, split fractions, pressures). A region without such a row has no continuation parameter; edge 3 then records `unsupported(no_continuation_parameter)` (§5.2).

Why this type and not the others blueprint §7.4 names (register R-030): *recycle closure* has no registered failure to rescue — SYN-001's recycle reduces to one flash of the fresh feed (SYN-001 §5.1) and damped Newton converges from every registered start (§8.3) — and its easy endpoint needs a separate once-through solve; *heat coupling*, *reaction activation* and *nonideality* have no v0.1 model. The global Newton homotopy `F(x) − (1 − λ) F(x⁰)` coincides with specification continuation on every registered case (at an A02 opening `F(x⁰)` is nonzero only on the promoted row), but its λ < 1 problems are not physical problems and its offset would live outside the pinned-input identity.

### 4.2 The map, its identity, and its endpoint

    H(x, λ) = F(x; p(λ)),      p_j(λ) = p_j* + (1 − λ)(p_j⁰ − p_j*),      p_j⁰ = x⁰[v_j]

with every other pinned input at its target value, `x⁰` the opening state (§4.3) and `λ ∈ [0, 1]`.

- **Easy endpoint.** At λ = 0 the promoted rows vanish at `x⁰` by construction; the other rows vanish because `x⁰` is the A02 pre-solve (T02 §7.5: the ordinary plan with `v_j`'s owner pinned at its guess), converged by the tear to K03's tolerances. The λ = 0 corrector therefore converges at iteration 0 on every registered case.
- **Target endpoint.** `H(x, 1) = F(x)`, including all original specifications: at λ = 1 the corrector uses **the target compiled instance itself** (no re-binding), so its residual and Jacobian are the target's bit for bit and carry the target's `(model_version, constants_sha256)`.
- **Identity of a level (ADR 0008 D1.2(ii), D1.3).** A λ-level is a re-binding of pinned inputs: a compiled instance with the target's `model_version` and its own `constants_sha256` (the parameter vector with `p(λ)` in place of `p*`). Re-binding without recompilation is permitted (ADR 0008 D4.1); recompilation is not forbidden, and nothing below depends on which is used (W0.1). λ never reaches the evaluation boundary; no field of `EvaluationContext` carries it.
- **Endpoint mapping:** the identity on `x` (same variables, rows, bounds, scales). **Scales:** the target's, frozen along the path. **Path domain:** λ ∈ [0, 1] on the dyadic grid of §4.3; `x` within the region's bounds and the provider's domain; the opening signature frozen.
- **Arithmetic of λ.** Every λ the controller forms is a dyadic rational (Δλ₀ = 1/4, factors 2 and 1/2), so `1 − λ` and every λ are exact in binary floating point; `p(λ)` is formed with one rounding, and `p(1) = p*` exactly because the λ = 1 level is not re-bound at all. λ is recorded as an exact fraction string `"p/q"` (§10).

### 4.3 The adaptive λ controller

**Opening state and signature.** `x⁰` and the signature are those of attempt 0 of the region solve the homotopy runs in (for edge 3: the failed solve's item 0, §5.3), after T02 §6.2's projection of the lifted splits.

1. **Easy endpoint:** corrector at λ = 0 from `x⁰`. If it does not converge: `HOMOTOPY_STALLED` with cause `easy_endpoint` (not reached by any registered case; typed so that silence cannot read as success).
2. **Trial:** λ' = min(λ + Δλ, 1). **Corrector:** K03 §5's Newton core, unchanged in every rule (Armijo, halvings, bound-aware `α_max` with exact landing, stagnation window, the T03 screen and wall observer), in the frozen signature, on the level-λ' instance, from the last accepted state, capped at `corrector_max_iterations` accepted iterations. Its outcome is the λ-trial's verdict: **accepted iff `CONVERGED`**.
3. **Accepted:** λ ← λ', the accepted state becomes the rollback checkpoint (§4.4), and, if λ < 1, Δλ ← min(2Δλ, 1 − λ).
4. **Rejected** (any other corrector outcome — `BOUND_BLOCKED`, `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED`, `LINE_SEARCH_FAILED`, `STAGNATION`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(newton_iterations)`, `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED`): the state is **rolled back** to the last accepted checkpoint (the corrector's end state is discarded), Δλ ← Δλ/2; if Δλ < `delta_lambda_min`: **`HOMOTOPY_STALLED`**. A corrector `EVALUATION_ERROR` is not a rejection: the homotopy ends `EVALUATION_ERROR` (a defect, K03 §5.5).
5. **Budget:** at most `max_lambda_trials` λ-trials after the easy endpoint → `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` with budget `homotopy_steps`; the property budget is the solve's (`BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(property_calls)`, the meter's refusal, T03 review S1's shape: history, checkpoint and provenance kept).
6. **Convergence:** λ = 1 accepted → the core outcome `CONVERGED` at the corrector's final state.

**Registered constants** (`SolvePolicy.globalization.homotopy`):

| Constant | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `delta_lambda_initial` | 1/4 | dyadic; with growth 2 an unobstructed path takes three λ-trials (1/4, 3/4, 1). Not fitted: PHS-05 converges from 1, 1/2, 1/4 and 1/8 alike (3, 2, 3, 4 trials; §9.9) |
| `growth`, `shrink` | 2, 1/2 | keep every λ dyadic (§4.2); growth is load-bearing (without it PHS-05 takes 4 trials) |
| `delta_lambda_min` | 1/1024 | the stall resolution: a stall brackets the obstruction within 2 Δλ_min = 2⁻⁹ in λ (§4.6); at most ~2 log₂(Δλ₀/Δλ_min) ≈ 16 rejections per obstruction, which `max_lambda_trials` covers |
| `corrector_max_iterations` | 10 | every registered corrector converges in ≤ 4; a tangent-predicted Newton that needs more than 2.5× that is not in its quadratic basin. Inert on the registered paths (50 changes nothing, §9.9) — registered, not load-bearing |
| `max_lambda_trials` | 64 | ≥ 3.5× the longest registered path (18 trials, HOM-04; HOM-03 takes 17) |

### 4.4 λ < 1 is never target-verified

- Every accepted λ-level with λ < 1 — the easy endpoint λ = `"0"` included (*confirmed 2026-09-24*, review Q1 decision 2; K04 refuses it `continuation_level(0)`) — is a **checkpoint of a modified problem**: `Checkpoint.continuation_lambda = "p/q"` (< 1), `label = "partial"`, `verification_scope = "unverified"` — enforced by the schema (ADR 0010 D7.4).
- **K04 refuses** a checkpoint whose `continuation_lambda` is present and not `"1"` with a typed reason `continuation_level(<p/q>)`, before any check runs; its identity (`constants_sha256` of the level) is not the target's anyway.
- A solve that ends `HOMOTOPY_STALLED` or `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(homotopy_steps)` issues **no certificate**; its failure bundle carries the last accepted checkpoint (`partial`, with its λ).
- A converged homotopy's certificate is issued on the λ = 1 state under the target identity, like any other `CONVERGED` solve (K04 §3), by K04 applied to the bound declaration (§4.8).

### 4.5 Composition with the phase-attempt contract

- The homotopy is the **core of one attempt**; the attempt's signature is frozen for every corrector and every λ-level (ADR 0005 [A01]). The homotopy **never changes the active set**: a corrector that meets a phase wall or a bound fails, and the λ-trial is rejected.
- Its core outcome enters `phase_contract.decide`: `CONVERGED` → row 1 (T03 §4.7(a), by the branch found; an inadmissible λ = 1 root restarts per the contract, and every attempt the contract opens after it runs the **Newton core on the target**); `HOMOTOPY_STALLED`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(homotopy_steps)` and every other outcome → row 6 (terminal). None of rows 2–5 applies: their outcomes are corrector outcomes, which the homotopy turns into λ-rejections.
- **One context per level** (K03 §12.4 as amended by ADR 0010 D6): the attempt's `AttemptContext.evaluation_context` is the target's (λ = 1); each λ-level uses a level context, field-equal to it except `constants_sha256`, constructed when the level is opened; every residual and Jacobian call of a corrector passes its level's context.
- **Path crossing a phase boundary.** A frozen-signature continuation cannot cross one: in the `TWO_PHASE` signature the lifted split meets its bound at the bubble point (HOM-04), in a single-phase signature the screen flags the trials past the dew point (HOM-03). The result is a bracketed, diagnosed stall (§4.6). Crossing — each level a full contract solve, or arclength continuation — is deferred (§14).

### 4.6 `HOMOTOPY_STALLED` and its diagnosis

`HOMOTOPY_STALLED` is blueprint §7.4's "failure to advance": after a rejection Δλ < `delta_lambda_min` (or the easy endpoint fails). **The stall brackets its obstruction:** every accepted λ lies below it and every rejected λ above it on both registered stalls, so `λ_reached < λ_boundary < λ_reached + 2⁻⁹` (§9.2, generator-checked against the closed-form λ of the phase boundary on the path).

The failure bundle carries one **hypothesis** (never an observation; blueprint §7.4, "branch loss is a hypothesis unless supported by diagnostics") from a registered vocabulary, decided by the corrector outcomes of the rejections since the last acceptance:

| All rejections since the last acceptance were | Inferred cause |
| --- | --- |
| `BOUND_BLOCKED` with a watched variable (T03 §4.6) of a `TWO_PHASE` unit in `blocked_by`, or `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED` | `phase_boundary_on_path(<unit>)`, `<unit>` the unit whose watched variable was blocked or whose reported regime differed (the first in signature order) |
| `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED` | `singular_path_or_fold` |
| anything else, or a mixture | `corrector_failure` |

The observations (non-R0) are the last accepted λ's watched-variable values and the admissibility values of the rejected trials. Class and action: ADR 0010 D8 (`supply_initial_guess` — correct on both registered stalls: a guess on the far side of the boundary converges without any recovery).

### 4.7 Records

One **`homotopy_step`** event per λ-trial, the easy endpoint included, recorded after its corrector: `iteration` = the trial's index (0 for the easy endpoint), `lambda_value` (`"p/q"`), `delta_lambda` (`"p/q"`, `"0"` for the easy endpoint), `trial_status` (`accepted` \| `rejected`), `corrector_outcome`, `corrector_iterations`, `level_constants_sha256`, `state_sha256` of the corrector's end state, `message` `""` or, on the stall, `homotopy_stalled(<corrector_outcome>)`. Every event a corrector records carries **`homotopy_level`** = that trial's index (stamped by the trace, as `step_index` is). Rejected λ-trials never move the checkpoint. The attempt's `AttemptContext` has `core = "homotopy"` and `continuation = {type, parameter_ids}`; the provenance item carries `continuation = {type, parameter_ids, lambda_levels, lambda_reached, rejected_trials}` (§10). **The item's `iterations`** (*amended 2026-09-24*, review Q1 decision 1) is the total of every corrector's Newton iterations in the attempt — the easy endpoint's and every λ-trial's, accepted and rejected — equal to the attempt's `attempt_closed` count: a count of work, R0; the accepted path is `continuation.lambda_levels`. Registered: `ref.hom.<case>.homotopy.provenance_item_iterations` = 12, 12, 30, 28, 9 on HOM-01…05 (HOM-03's four accepted correctors ran 4 of its 30; the implementation measured 30 at `ecc88d5`). No R0 string carries a float.

### 4.8 The certificate of a converged specification solve: K04 on the bound declaration (ruling, 2026-09-24)

*Added 2026-09-24 on the build lane's finding at `1f632ed`: A03, A04, A05 and A08 required certificates that no verifier path could issue. This section is the ruling (ADR 0010 D9, register R-035). K04's checks, tolerances and verdict rules are unchanged except where it says so.*

**The defect, measured.** `verify(flowsheet, result)` builds its problem as `Syn001TearProblem(flowsheet)`, i.e. the **nominal** declaration (`U-HEAT:HEAT-T` present and pinned at the flowsheet's heater temperature), and reads K04 §4.5's specification values from the flowsheet. An A02 solve is a solve of the **bound** declaration (T02 §7.3: `HEAT-T` removed, `SPEC:SPEC-flash-duty` promoted; 49 rows over 47 columns), and the binding's flowsheet carries `S3.T` at the *guess* (T02 §7.5). *Measured* by the design lane at `1f632ed`:

- handed HOM-01's region result, the verifier returns `UNVERIFIED(final_state_absent)`, because a region result carries `state`, not `final_state`;
- handed HOM-01's state explicitly, it returns **`FAILED` with `false_success_detected = true`**, failing exactly `residual.U-HEAT:HEAT-T` and `specification.S3.T` (355 − 375 = −20 K: the guess judged as a specification). HOM-02 and HOM-05 behave the same way.

Neither result says anything about the problem that was solved, and the second is a false alarm on a correct root. No A02 solve has ever been certified; T02 registers no certificate for one.

**Ruling: option (a).** Every `CONVERGED` solve is certified against **the declaration it solved**, using K04's check set unchanged in kind. A certificate is a function of four things only: that declaration, the revision's specification values, `x_final` and the check policy. It never depends on the flowsheet object a pre-solve traversed.

Option (b), striking the clauses, is rejected for two reasons. It would leave the recovered PHS-05, the package's headline result, `CONVERGED` on the solver's word alone. And the refusal of rule 1 below is needed under either option, because the unguarded path already produces a false `FAILED`.

1. **Identity comes first.** Before any check, the verifier compiles its declaration and compares `(model_version, constants_sha256)` with the solve's. The solve's identity is read from **the result itself**: its `root_fingerprint` (ADR 0005 D7) and its `SolvePlan` where it carries one; for a plan result, from its last step's solve result. *(Amended 2026-09-24, review S1.)* A `SolvePlan` the **caller** supplies (§4.2's alias comparison) is compared as an additional claim and must agree too, but it never identifies the solve: a result that carries neither a fingerprint nor a plan of its own is refused `declaration_unidentified` whatever the caller passes. When `x_final` is taken from the result, its state hash (ADR 0002's `state_sha256` of the state vector, the hash `residual(x_final).state_sha256` would carry — no residual call is needed, review N9) must also equal `root_fingerprint.full_state_sha256`. *(Amended 2026-09-24, review S2.)* On the bound entry point the revision document must be the one the binding was bound from, compared by its canonical SHA-256 (ADR 0002 D3): otherwise `declaration_mismatch(revision)` — a mismatched pair would judge one problem's state against another problem's specification values.

   A difference is a **refusal**: `VerifierError` with message `declaration_mismatch(<field>)`, where `<field>` is the first differing of `model_version`, `constants_sha256` and `revision`, or `state_mismatch(full_state_sha256)`. A `CONVERGED` result that carries neither a fingerprint nor a plan is refused with `declaration_unidentified`. A refusal issues no certificate, no check result and no verdict word.

   The guard applies at the nominal entry point too: a bound solve handed to the nominal verifier is refused with `declaration_mismatch(model_version)` and never judged. K04 §3.1 already requires the equality; this ruling moves it from an assertion in a test to a refusal inside the verifier. The `state=` override of K04 §9 skips only the state-hash comparison. (A31)
2. **`x_final`** is the converged result's full state over the declaration's 47 `variable_ids`: `final_state` of a tear result, `state` of a region or plan result. For an edge-3 recovery it is the recovery region solve's state at λ = 1, whose identity is the target's (A02).
3. **The check set.** The nominal path is the special case in which nothing is freed or promoted.

## 5. Recovery edge 3 (normative; ADR 0010 D3)

### 5.1 Trigger

A **region solve** — a `solve_eo` step's, or the region solve a T02 §4.4 merge ran — whose terminal outcome is one of:

`LINE_SEARCH_FAILED`, `STAGNATION`, `BOUND_BLOCKED`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(newton_iterations)`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(ptc_steps)`, `PTC_STALLED`, `ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING`, `ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`.

**`ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING` and `ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED` are triggers** because on the lifted path the contract converts a core's globalization failure into a restart (T03 §4.8 rows 2–5), so the failure surfaces as the contract's terminal outcome: PHS-05's Newton failure *is* its cycling (T03 §7). Excluding them would make the edge unreachable on the case T03 handed over.

**Not triggers:** `CONVERGED`; `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED` (rank deficiency has its own row in blueprint §7.7); `EVALUATION_ERROR` and `CHECKPOINT_INCOMPATIBLE` (defects); `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(property_calls)` (nothing left to spend); `INITIALIZATION_FAILED`, `CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `UNSUPPORTED_RANK_STRUCTURE`, `PTC_MAPPING_INVALID` (refusals before any attempt); `HOMOTOPY_STALLED` (no recursion); the tear path's outcomes (edge 3 follows region solves only; a stalled Anderson loop has edge 2).

### 5.2 Preconditions, and the unsupported result

In order: (i) `SolvePolicy.globalization.eo_recovery = "homotopy"` — otherwise there is no edge (not an unsupported result); (ii) the region has a continuation parameter (§4.1) — otherwise the failed outcome stands and the step's `region_closed` records `eo_recovery = "unsupported"`, `eo_recovery_unsupported = "no_continuation_parameter"`; (iii) the edge has not fired for this region step in this solve (`eo_recovery_max_count = 1`). A merged recycle region (edge 2's) has no promoted specification, so edge 3 after edge 2 is always `unsupported` in v0.1 — recorded, never silent.

### 5.3 Action, checkpoint policy, maximum count

- **Action:** one **recovery region solve** of the same region, whose attempt 0 runs the homotopy core (§4) from the failed solve's **item-0 opening state** in its **opening signature**, with a **fresh contract state** (used signatures and attempt count start empty; ADR 0009's merge precedent: a region solve is "a solve" for T03 §4.8(ii)). The failed solve's attempts, checkpoint and provenance are kept; the recovery's `branch_provenance` **continues the list** densely, its first item with `opening_source = "eo_recovery_start"` and `initializer_source` = the failed item 0's. **"Item 0" is the failed region solve's own first item** — its attempt-0 opening state and that item's `initializer_source` — never the first item of a dense list that a merge began earlier (a merged loop's Anderson attempt). *(Fixed 2026-09-24, review N3, before edge 3 after edge 2 is ever supported; in v0.1 that path is `unsupported`, §5.2.)* Property calls count against the one policy cap.
- **Why the opening state and not the failed end state** (register R-031): the end state is where the globalization got lost — a pinned iterate fourteen kelvin inside the two-phase band in PHS-05 — and is not a root of any level: from it the λ = 0 corrector fails and PHS-05 stays unrescued (*ablation*, §9.9). The opening state is a root of H(·, 0) by construction.
- **Maximum count:** 1 per region step per solve. **Outcome:** the recovery's (`CONVERGED`, `HOMOTOPY_STALLED`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, …). A failed recovery does not fire the edge again. **The step's checkpoint** is the recovery's last accepted level, or the failed solve's checkpoint when the recovery accepted none; a failed recovery's `state` is where it ended (the opening, if the easy endpoint failed), so a failure bundle's consumer reads the checkpoint, not the state (review N2).
- **Cost estimate** recorded on the plan step (blueprint §7.7): at most `max_lambda_trials × corrector_max_iterations` = 640 Newton iterations.

### 5.4 Evidence that physics and specifications did not change

For every recovery (A10): the λ = 1 instance's `model_version` and `constants_sha256` equal the failed solve's; the region's row and column ids are unchanged; every λ < 1 level's parameter vector differs from the target's **only** at the continued ids (by id, under a spy on the re-binding); a certificate, when issued, carries the revision's `constants_sha256`.

## 6. The flash family: the residence-time mass mapping (derivation; ADR 0010 D4)

### 6.1 The pseudo-dynamics and the row-sign map

Blueprint §7.5: `M(x̂) dx̂/dτ = −F̂(x̂)`, linearized step `(M_k/Δτ + Ĵ_k) Δx̂ = −F̂_k`. ADR 0008 D3.3 writes every balance row as `F_row = Σ inflow − Σ outflow + Σ sources`, so that the physical balance reads `d(holdup)/dt = +F_row`. Hence, with `M_row = ∂(holdup_row)/∂x ≥ 0` in the column sense of §6.3:

    M dx/dτ = −F̃,     F̃_row = σ_row F_row,     σ = −1 on holdup_balance rows, +1 on every other row.

**`PTC_ROW_SIGN`** is this map, one named constant keyed by `row_accumulation` (`holdup_balance → −1`, `zero_holdup_balance → +1`, `algebraic → +1`), applied to the residual and to the Jacobian together (ADR 0008 D3.4, P02's `OUTPUT_ROW_SIGN` precedent): `F̂_σ = σ ⊙ S_F⁻¹ F`, `Ĵ_σ = σ ⊙ S_F⁻¹ J S_x`, `M̂ = S_F⁻¹ M S_x`, and the step is `(M̂/Δτ + Ĵ_σ) Δx̂ = −F̂_σ`. Check in one line: one vessel, `F = n_in − w`, holdup `θw`: `(θ/Δτ + 1)Δw = n_in − w`, the implicit-Euler step toward `n_in` (PTC-S1). With σ = +1 the matrix is `(θ/Δτ − 1)`, singular at Δτ = θ and anti-dynamical below it (the sign ablation, §9.9).

### 6.2 The holdup model, and why the energy holdup is the enthalpy content

Each SYN-001 vessel (`syn001.tp_heater`, `syn001.tp_flash`) is modelled, for pseudo-time only, as a **well-mixed vessel whose every phase inventory drains at a rate proportional to itself with a common residence time θ**, at the outlet state and at the pressure its pressure rows pin:

    N_i = θ Σ_π n_i^π,          H = θ Σ_π Σ_i n_i^π h_i^π(T_π, P_π),

`n_i^π` the outflow of component `i` in outlet phase `π`: for the flash the vapour and liquid products (`S4.n`, `S5.n`); for the heater the lifted split of its outlet (`S3.vap`, `S3.liq`). This is the physical content of a vessel under ideal proportional level and pressure control; nothing more is claimed.

**Why `H` and not `U`.** ADR 0008 D3.5 names the energy holdup `U`, "the quantity an open-system energy balance accumulates", and defers "the U–H relation of its holdup" to T04. For a *rigid* vessel `dU/dt = Σ Ḣ_in − Σ Ḣ_out + Q`. The residence-time vessel is not rigid: its contents' volume `V = θ V̇_out` moves while its pressure is pinned, and the first law with boundary work gives `dU/dt = Σ Ḣ_in − Σ Ḣ_out + Q − P dV/dt`, i.e. `d(U + PV)/dt = F_energy` at constant `P`. The accumulated quantity is therefore the enthalpy content `H = U + PV`. The manifests' symbol and dimension (J) stand; the relation is this paragraph (finding F2, §17).

**Why phase-resolved.** Every energy row's outflow terms are phase-resolved enthalpy blocks (`H_S3_vapor`, `H_S3_liquid`; `H_S4_vapor`, `H_S5_liquid`). If the mole holdup is written on the same phase amounts, a change of enthalpy reference `h_i → h_i + c_i` shifts each energy row by `Σ c_i` times a combination of mole and split rows and shifts `M_energy` by `Σ c_i M_mole,i`, which is the same row operation: **the step is invariant**. With the heater's mole holdup on the stream total `S3.n` the shift and the row operation differ by `Σ c_i θ F_split,i`, and the step moves off the split manifold. Generator-checked (§9.7): off the manifold (`F_split,A = 0.1 mol/s`) the phase-resolved step is invariant to 1e-25 and the stream-total step moves by more than 1e-6 scaled; on the manifold — where every registered path stays, because the split rows are linear and satisfied from the projected start — the two give the same step to 1e-25. The choice is made on the principle, not on a registered case (register R-032).

### 6.3 M entry by entry

`M_row = ∂(holdup_row)/∂x`, evaluated at the accepted iterate and frozen during its retries (blueprint §7.5's "locally frozen-mass" step). SYN-001 enthalpies: `h_i^V = c_p(T − T_r) + L_i`, `h_i^L = c_p(T − T_r) + v_i(P − P_r)` (SYN-001 §2); in the implementation every entry is taken from the provider's own enthalpy and derivative values (so a test double with a shifted reference shifts `M` consistently, A16).

| Row (`holdup_balance`) | Holdup | Columns and entries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `U-HEAT:HEAT-mole:i` | `N_i = θ_H (S3.vap.i + S3.liq.i)` | `S3.vap.i`: θ_H; `S3.liq.i`: θ_H |
| `U-HEAT:HEAT-duty` | `H = θ_H Σ_i (S3.vap.i h_i^V(S3.T) + S3.liq.i h_i^L(S3.T, S3.P))` | `S3.vap.i`: θ_H h_i^V; `S3.liq.i`: θ_H h_i^L; `S3.T`: θ_H c_p Σ_i (S3.vap.i + S3.liq.i); `S3.P`: θ_H Σ_i S3.liq.i v_i |

Every other row has a zero `M` row: the mixer's `zero_holdup_balance` rows **by physics** (a junction, ADR 0008 D3.5) and every `algebraic` row by declaration. **Pattern (ADR 0008 D4.5):** the rows with a nonzero `M` row are exactly the eight `holdup_balance` rows of the region (generator-checked; A13). ### 6.4 Checks route (a) requires

- **Steady-state equivalence.** A steady state of `M ẋ = −F̃` is `F̃ = 0 ⇔ F = 0` (σ is ±1): the PTC fixed points are exactly the roots, for every `M` and every Δτ. Executable: at the P01 root (r = 0.95; scaled residual 5.8e-20) the step is at most ten times that residual at Δτ = 1e-6, 1 and 1e10 (A15).
- **Dimensions.** A mole row's entries are `θ` (s), so `M_mole ẋ` is mol/s, the row's dimension; an energy row's entries are `θ h` (s·J/mol) on flow columns, `θ c_p Σn` (s·W/K) on a temperature column and `θ Σ l v` (s·m³/s) on a pressure column, so `M_energy ẋ` is W. The holdups themselves are mol and J — ADR 0008 D3.2's holdup dimensions, the rows' raised by time. In scaled form `M̂` has units of seconds and `M̂/Δτ` is dimensionless.
- **Sign.** `σ` as §6.1; the one-vessel check and the sign ablation.
- **Algebraic consistency and index** — §6.7.
- **Bounded domain handling** — §7.3.
- **Reference invariance** — §6.2.

### 6.5 The pseudo-time unit, and the accumulation identity

**θ is the unit of pseudo-time, not a tuned magnitude.** `M = θ M₁` for a common θ, so the step depends on `Δτ/θ` only; SER's update is multiplicative. Scaling θ, τ₀, τ_min and τ_max by the same power of two reproduces every trajectory bit for bit in binary floating point (generator-checked ×8 on OFF-A: same count, every state equal to 1e-25; A18). With no vessel data in SYN-001 there is no ratio θ_H/θ_F to derive; §6.6 shows local attraction for **every** positive ratio, and the common value is the choice that assumes nothing (register R-032).

**The accumulation identity.** For a linear row with a linear holdup (the six mole rows), one accepted pseudo-step at `α` gives exactly

    F_r(x_{k+1}) = (1 − α) F_r(x_k) + (N_r(x_{k+1}) − N_r(x_k)) / Δτ_k,

the implicit-Euler statement "imbalance = accumulation rate". It pins `σ`, θ and the mole-row pattern in context on every step of every PTC run (A21; generator: ≤ 7.7e-33 relative on every accepted step of the basin set).

### 6.6 Which modes attract, and why (route (a))

**At the SYN-001 root with the vessel temperatures pinned** (`HEAT-T`, `FLASH-T`; the nominal flowsheet at every r), the duties are *index-2* variables: `U-HEAT.Q` appears only in `HEAT-duty`, which with `S3.T` pinned is a constraint on the derivative of the heater's inventory, and likewise for the flash. The energy rows therefore contribute **no finite mode**; the pseudo-dynamics are the six component inventories of the two vessels in the recycle loop. Linearized at the root, with `w_H = S3.n` (heater outflow) and `w_F = S4.n + S5.n` (flash outflow), the splitter and mixer algebraic, and `D = ∂ℓ/∂w` the Jacobian of the flash's liquid split at 360 K:

    θ_H ẇ_H = r D w_F − w_H,     θ_F ẇ_F = w_H − w_F      ⇒      (θ_H s + 1)(θ_F s + 1) = μ,   μ ∈ spec(r D).

**`spec(D) = {1, d₂, 0}`, exactly.** Adding saturated liquid of the flash's own composition `x` to its feed at the same `T, P` changes only `L` (SYN-001 §9's affine-ray fact), so `D x = x`; by the same lever-rule argument adding its saturated vapour `y` changes only `V`, so `D y = 0`; the third eigenvalue is `d₂ = tr D − 1` (0.707 and 0.960 at the two registered roots; generator-checked). Whenever `0 ≤ μ < 1` — every `r < 1` with `d₂ ≤ 1`, which holds at both registered roots — the quadratic `θ_Hθ_F s² + (θ_H + θ_F)s + (1 − μ) = 0` has all coefficients positive, so **every mode is stable for every positive θ_H, θ_F**. With θ_H = θ_F = θ: `s = (−1 ± √μ)/θ`. (That `d₂ ≤ 1` for every two-phase TP flash of this provider is plausible — `I − D` is the vapour split's Jacobian — and is not established here.)

| r | d₂ (closed form) | the six finite modes s (1/s) | slowest time constant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 | 0.706 671 036 064 627 005 | −1.707 107, −1.594 420, −1, −1, −0.405 580, −0.292 893 | 3.414 21 s |
| 0.95 | 0.960 145 734 146 325 868 | −1.974 679, −1.955 059, −1, −1, −0.044 941, −0.025 321 | 39.49 s |

The slowest mode is the liquid inventory of the loop (μ = r): its time constant `θ/(1 − √r)` is the reason PTC is slow at high recycle (§8.3). **Generator-checked:** the finite generalized eigenvalues of the full 42 × 42 pencil `(Ĵ_σ, M̂)` at the P01 root are exactly these six, to 1e-18, at both r (A17). **The double mode at −1/θ is defective** (a 2 × 2 Jordan block; generator-checked: `Ĵ_σ⁻¹M̂ − θI` has nullity 1 and its square nullity 2). It is the μ = 0 mode — the flash's saturated-vapour direction `D y = 0`, which the recycle does not return — so heater and flash form a cascade without feedback, and a cascade of two equal lags is a Jordan pair. In double precision a Jordan pair splits by O(√(ε‖·‖)) into a complex or a real pair; A17 is written for that (F14).

What this does **not** say: nothing about attraction far from the root, about the A02 region (where `S3.T` is free, §6.7), or about any unit with a heat of reaction. Physical derivation is "a useful basis, not stability by construction" (blueprint §7.5); §8 asks the basin question separately.

### 6.7 What a pseudo-step cannot restrain: algebraic variables, and PHS-05

A linearly implicit pseudo-step Newton-solves every row with a zero `M` row at every Δτ: as Δτ → 0 the step tends to the Newton step of [algebraic rows; holdups frozen] — DAE practice's consistent-initialization projection — and **nothing in Δτ limits the change of a variable the algebraic rows determine.** In the A02 region `SPEC:SPEC-flash-duty` pins `U-FLASH.Q`, `FLASH-T` pins the flash temperature, and `FLASH-duty` then fixes the heater outlet enthalpy: the ten rows other than `HEAT-duty` of T03's heater block determine `S3.T`, the split and `U-FLASH.Q` by themselves, and `U-HEAT.Q` — which appears in `HEAT-duty` only — absorbs the pseudo-time term. Hence (generator-checked, A24): **on PHS-05's opening state the PTC step's `S3.T`, split and `U-FLASH.Q` components equal Newton's at Δτ = 1e-3, 1 and 1e3** — identically in exact arithmetic; to 1e-15 in the twin, whose reconstructed opening carries a loop residual of 1e-21 (P01's reference accuracy) that couples through the loop's `M` — and the first pseudo-step lands `S3.vap.C` at Newton's own `α_max = 0.642 309 461 269 271`. PTC cannot rescue PHS-05; the registered trajectory under PTC is §9.6. PTC-S3 is the same mechanism in two variables.

Index-2 duties also set the scale of τ_min: the PTC matrix's condition grows as Δτ⁻² (*measured* on OFF-A at r = 0.95: 2.2e2 at Δτ = 1 s, 3.6e5 at 1e-2, 3.5e9 at 1e-4, 3.5e13 at 1e-6), so below τ_min = 1e-4 s the duty components of a step would carry fewer than eight significant digits (§7.7).

## 7. The PTC core and the SER controller (normative; ADR 0010 D4)

### 7.1 Selection and scope

`SolvePolicy.globalization.eo_core = "ptc"` selects the PTC core for **every attempt of every region solve** of the run **except edge 3's recovery solve**, whose attempt 0 is the homotopy and whose later attempts run Newton whatever `eo_core` says (§4.5; *amended 2026-09-24*, F15 — the recovery is a specification continuation with Newton correctors, and PTC is never an automatic path); `"newton"` (the default) selects K03's. The tear path is unaffected (its problem has no holdup rows). No automatic path selects PTC (§8): it is not an edge action and it is not chosen by block size (plan §4.3).

### 7.2 The mapping at attempt open, and `PTC_MAPPING_INVALID`

Before the region's first attempt opens, the mapping `T04-residence-time-v1` is built from the region's `row_accumulation` and the registered entries of §6.3 (by model id; `syn001.tp_heater`, `syn001.tp_flash`) and validated: (V1) every `holdup_balance` row **and every `absent` row** of the region has a mapping entry (*amended 2026-09-24*, F15: an `absent` row declares nothing, so it is refused either way — no entry fails V1, an entry fails V2 — and never read as `M = 0`); (V2) no entry lies on a row that is not `holdup_balance` (ADR 0008 D4.5 as a runtime refusal); (V3) θ > 0; (V4) the entry's holdup dimension equals the manifest's `holdup.dimension`. The checks run V1, V2, V3, V4 in that order, each over every row in declaration order (*amended 2026-09-24*, F15; it matters only for a mapping failing two checks), and the first failure ends the solve **`PTC_MAPPING_INVALID`** with message `ptc_mapping_invalid(<row>, <check>)`, `<check> ∈ {missing, not_holdup_balance, residence_time, dimension}`: no `attempt_opened`, no residual or Jacobian call, taxonomy class "model domain/conservation/derivative defects", action `report_defect`. A model with no registered mapping (for example an opaque evaluator, ADR 0008 D3.6, whose rows are `absent`) fails V1. Silence never reads as `M = 0`.

### 7.3 One pseudo-step

At accepted iterate `x_k` (`k` = pseudo-step index), with `F̂_σ,k`, `Ĵ_σ,k`, `M̂_k` formed once. *(Amended 2026-09-24, review M1.)* **A Jacobian or mass-matrix evaluation at an accepted iterate that is not `ok` ends the attempt `EVALUATION_ERROR`**, whatever its status: the residual there evaluated `ok`, so a failing derivative is a defect, never a bad trial. It is never handed to the linear solve — the first implementation did, as an all-zero matrix, and turned the defect into eleven `linear_solve_failed` retries and `PTC_STALLED` (an edge-3 trigger, which §5.1 excludes for defects), or into a SuperLU crash (review M1, S5). The same sentence holds for Newton (K03 §5.5 as amended). No registered path reaches it: on SYN-001 the compiled Jacobian makes no metered provider call at an already evaluated state (review P4); a provider whose derivative fails where its value did not (T05, an opaque evaluator) can. *(Amended 2026-09-24, F15.)* The start `x_0` is evaluated as K03 §5 evaluates Newton's initial point: `error` → the attempt ends `EVALUATION_ERROR`; any other non-`ok` status → `PTC_STALLED`, the PTC core's failure to advance in the place of Newton's `LINE_SEARCH_FAILED` (§7.6).

1. Stop test (§7.4) — before anything else.
2. `Δτ_try ← Δτ_k`; **retry loop:** solve `(M̂_k/Δτ_try + Ĵ_σ,k) d̂ = −F̂_σ,k` (SuperLU, ADR 0004's options; a new factorization per trial because the matrix changes with Δτ). `d = S_x d̂`.
3. **Bounds:** K03 §5.3 verbatim — `α_max` over the bounded columns, exact landing at `+0.0`. `α_max = 0` → rejection `bound_blocked` (the blocking columns kept). Otherwise **one trial** `y = x_k + α_max d` (no halving of α, no merit test: an accepted pseudo-step may increase the residual).
4. **Trial verdict**, in K03 §5.4's order: a failed linear solve → `linear_solve_failed`; evaluation `error` → the attempt ends `EVALUATION_ERROR`; any other non-`ok` evaluation → `invalid_trial`; a reported signature other than the frozen one (T03 §4.3's screen) → `phase_update_required`, reported to the wall observer (§7.6); else **accepted**.
5. **On rejection:** keep `x_k` and `F_k` (nothing of the trial is kept), `Δτ_try ← 0.5 Δτ_try`, retry. After `retries_max` = 10 retries (11 trials), or when `0.5 Δτ_try < τ_min`: the attempt ends **`BOUND_BLOCKED`** with `blocked_by` if the last rejection was `bound_blocked` (so T03 §4.6's disappearance row applies — a phase whose variable is blocked at every pseudo-step down to 2⁻¹⁰ Δτ or τ_min is leaving by the pseudo-dynamics' own verdict), otherwise **`PTC_STALLED`**.
6. **On acceptance:** `x_{k+1} = y`; SER (§7.4).

### 7.4 SER: the update, the floor, the stop, the reset, the budget

- **Update** (blueprint §7.5, on accepted iterates only, fixed scales): `φ_k = ‖F̂_σ,k‖₂`; unless `x_{k+1}` meets the stop test,

      Δτ_{k+1} = clip_[τ_min, τ_max]( Δτ_used · clip_[γ_min, γ_max]( φ_k / max(φ_{k+1}, φ_floor) ) ),

  where `Δτ_used` is the pseudo-step the accepted trial was taken at. **No quantity of a rejected trial enters** — neither its Δτ (the proposed one) nor its residual: each alternative changes a registered count (§9.9).
- **The floor is inert, and why.** The ratio is formed only when `x_{k+1}` fails the stop test, i.e. some row has `|F_i| > tol_i`, so `φ_{k+1} ≥ |F̂_i| > tol_i/S_i ≥ τ̂_min = 1e-8` (K04 §5.3) > φ_floor = 1e-12. The floor therefore never binds under the registered tolerances (generator: the smallest φ at which a ratio was formed is 2.11e-8 over the basin set), and removing it changes nothing (§9.9). It is registered because blueprint §7.5 requires it and because a policy with a zero or smaller tolerance on some row would need it; the brief's ablation "SER without the floor" is registered as *no change*, with this proof (finding F4).
- **Stop:** K03 §5.2's per-row test on the unscaled residual, at pseudo-step 0 and after every accepted pseudo-step, **before** any SER update — blueprint's "stop on the original verification criteria before a near-zero residual inflates the ratio". An already valid root stops at pseudo-step 0 with no factorization and no polish.
- **Reset:** Δτ ← τ₀ at the opening of every attempt. The SER state belongs to the attempt; every restart the contract makes is an active-set restart (blueprint: "reset the controller on a scale, mass-policy, or active-set restart"; there is no scale or mass-policy restart in v0.1). Load-bearing: without it PTC-S5 ends `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` (§9.9).
- **Budget:** `max_steps_per_attempt` = 200 accepted pseudo-steps → `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` with budget `ptc_steps`.

### 7.5 The polish, and why

When a PTC attempt stops at `x_c` after at least one pseudo-step, the core takes **one Newton step** from `x_c` (the unsigned Jacobian at `x_c`, K03 §5.3's `α_max` with exact landing, one trial). The trial replaces `x_c` iff it evaluates `ok`, reports the frozen signature, meets K03 §5.2, and its worst row ratio `max_i |F_i|/tol_i` is no larger than `x_c`'s; otherwise `x_c` stands. The attempt is `CONVERGED` either way — *amended 2026-09-24 (F15; review M1)*: except when an evaluation of the polish returns `error` (the Jacobian or residual at `x_c`, or the trial) or the Jacobian at `x_c` is otherwise not `ok` (a derivative defect, §7.3), recorded as a `trial` event `polish(rejected:error)` with `x_c` kept, which ends the attempt `EVALUATION_ERROR` as §7.3 step 4 and K03 §5.4 do; a spent property budget arrives this way, and the plan run reports it `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(property_calls)` with the attempts, `x_c` as the `partial` checkpoint and the provenance kept (T03 review S1's shape, A28); a trial whose evaluation is non-`ok` but not `error` is a rejected polish (`polish(rejected:invalid_trial)`) and `x_c` stands; the pseudo-step count excludes the polish; it is recorded as a `trial` event with `message` `polish` (accepted or rejected with its reason) and `pseudo_step` null.
*Note 2026-09-25 (design lane, T05b Q-S13; `docs/reviews/T05b-review.md` re-review N-W7; R-064).* **"K03 §5.3's `α_max`" here and in §7's pseudo-step (item 3, "K03 §5.3 verbatim") means that section's first paragraph** — `α_max`, exact landing and `bound_blocked` — **without the structural-zero release** K03 §5.3 gained on 2026-09-25. The release's proof uses the nonsingular Newton matrix `J` restricted to the closed rows; a pseudo-step's matrix `J + D/Δt` pairs rows with columns through the mass mapping, so the proof does not transfer as it stands, and no PTC attempt is registered that ends `BOUND_BLOCKED` on structural zeros. This ratifies T05b W9.5's Newton-only scope; nothing registered here moves. Extending the release to the PTC core (the polish, whose matrix is `J`, would be the easy half) is a T04 amendment to be taken on a measured case.

**Why** (register R-033). PTC's terminal convergence is linear: it stops the first time every row is under its tolerance, and the slow inventory mode leaves every unit balance just under the tolerance *with the same sign*. K04's envelope balance (feed = vapour product + purge) is the sum of four unit balances with the tolerance of one: at OFF-B, r = 0.95, the unpolished PTC stop has a material envelope of **1.31 τ** — K04 §8.1 would issue `FAILED` with `false_success_detected` on a `CONVERGED` solve. One Newton step makes the linear rows exact and the others quadratically small: every registered polish is accepted and every polished named final state lies within 1e-10 (mol/s, K, Pa) and 1e-4 W of the P01 root (§9.5). This is blueprint §7.5's "Newton polishing … when useful", useful here by measurement; it is not applied to a Newton core's stop, which never needs it.

### 7.6 Composition with the contract (ADR 0010 D6)

- **Frozen signature** for every pseudo-step, retry, polish, residual and Jacobian call of a PTC attempt (T03 §4.2), one evaluation context per attempt (K03 §12.4).
- **Wall observer.** Phase-rejected trials are reported to the one `WallObserver` with `iteration` = pseudo-step index and `alpha = 2^−retry` (*confirmed 2026-09-24*, F15: the observer may hear every rejection, but only a `phase_update_required` rejection is a wall or a candidate, T03 §4.4; any other rejection changes no decision); the candidates of an attempt are its phase-rejected trials of the most recent pseudo-step that had one, in retry order (decreasing Δτ); T03 §4.5's selection (first adjacent, else first) applies unchanged. Patience: two consecutive pseudo-steps each with a phase rejection close the attempt `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED` (row 2). *Order (amended 2026-09-24, F15, the twin's):* patience is tested only after an **accepted** pseudo-step; a pseudo-step whose retries are exhausted ends the attempt by §7.3 step 5 first (`PTC_STALLED` or `BOUND_BLOCKED`), and row 4 then restarts from the phase-rejected trial when the wall lies within the stagnation window — so a wall met while retries run out is honoured as `phase_wall(stall, …)`, not `phase_wall(patience, …)`.
- **Rows 4–5 extended.** T03 §4.8 row 4 and K03 §9.3's stall trigger ("a stall with the last wall within the window") gain `PTC_STALLED`; row 5 (lifted: the kernel's regime at the end state) gains `PTC_STALLED` and `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(ptc_steps)`. Both are the PTC core's failure-to-advance outcomes and take the place `LINE_SEARCH_FAILED` and `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED(newton_iterations)` take for Newton. `BOUND_BLOCKED` from §7.3.5 enters row 3 like Newton's. No outcome a Newton or Anderson core produces changes path, so every registered K03, T02 and T03 trajectory is unchanged (A25).
- **Reset** per attempt (§7.4).

## 8. Qualification: the verdict and its evidence

### 8.1 The criterion, stated before the comparison was run

Blueprint §7.5 accepts a family on route (a) — a physical derivation documenting which modes and branches attract and why — or route (b) — empirical basin comparisons against damped Newton — "both require steady-state equivalence, dimensional/sign checks, algebraic consistency, and bounded domain handling", and "the artificial dynamics must have useful attraction properties on the tested region". Its risk table: "PTC lacks a defensible mapping **or improves no tested basin** → keep it experimental". The plan registers the tested region: "SYN-001 at r = 0.95 is the registered basin comparison against damped Newton". The criterion applied here is therefore: **the family qualifies iff route (a) holds and, on the registered comparison, the set of starts from which PTC reaches the P01 root is not contained in damped Newton's.** Both under the same contract, the same tolerances and the same budgets.

### 8.2 Route (a) holds

Steady-state equivalence, dimensions, the row-sign map and pattern, reference invariance (§6.1–§6.4); the attracting modes and why (§6.6); algebraic consistency and index, including what it cannot restrain (§6.7); bounded domain handling (§7.3). All generator-checked.

### 8.3 The comparison: PTC improves no tested basin

**Registered start family** (K03 §13's convention for off-ray starts, applied to the EO region): tear guesses `t⁰ = m · (i, j, k)/4`, `(i, j, k)` the 15 points of the quarter-lattice on the simplex (`i + j + k = 4`), `m ∈ {0.3, 3, 30}` mol/s — three decades bracketing `Σ t* = 31.1 mol/s` — 45 guesses, each reconstructed by one traversal (K03 §3.3) and projected (T02 §6.2). The mixer refuses **27**: nine of the fifteen directions — 112, 121, 130, 202, 211, 220, 301, 310, 400 — at every magnitude, because the recycle candidate itself is not a subcooled liquid at `T_f` (K03 §10.1's margin `1 − Σ z_i K_i(T_f)` ≤ −0.115; the mixer's enthalpy-gap rule, K02's `single_phase_admissible`, refuses each by ≥ 1e3 × its 1e-6 K). The rule is intensive, so a refused direction is refused at every magnitude. It is the initializer's own check, not a selection. **18 starts remain**: six directions × three magnitudes, each candidate subcooled or exactly saturated (direction 040 is pure B at its normal boiling point, gap 0). *Amended 2026-09-24 (F14):* the first version judged the *mixed* feed at `T_mix`, a rule the mixer does not enforce, and admitted 37; the implementation refused 27 and W0.2 stopped on it. r = 0.95, `eo_recovery: none` (the comparison is of cores, not of recoveries).

**Result (twin, 40 digits; reproduced exactly by the implementation on all 18, W0.2 at `feb6ebc`):** damped Newton reaches the P01 root from **18 of 18**; PTC from **18 of 18**; PTC's total iterations are 1.9 to 8.8 times Newton's on every start (Newton 5–10, PTC 15–55 per start; every PTC polish accepted). The success sets are equal: **PTC improves no tested basin.** Named runs (§9.5): the registered initializer — Newton 5, PTC 39; OFF-B — Newton [2, 1, 3] over three attempts, PTC [4, 26] over two.

**Verdict:** the residence-time PTC family for the SYN-001 vessels is **experimental** (`ptc.status = "experimental"`), selectable by explicit policy only. **V14's "qualified PTC/SER" clause is recorded incomplete** (plan §4.3: "retain PTC as experimental and record the v0.1 gate as incomplete"). The SER controller itself is fully specified and tested (A19–A22), which is what A03 requires.

*Measured, not registered (design-lane exploration with a prototype of the §7 core on the implementation's residual, float64):* on 40 draws of the A04 generator (every scaled region coordinate perturbed by U[−0.2, 0.2] about the r = 0.95 initializer, seed 12345), Newton converged on 38 and PTC on 39; each method converged on starts the other did not (2 and 1); the differences trace to the algebraic snap of perturbed *pinned* temperatures in the first step, not to inventory dynamics. This ensemble was not registered before it was run and is not evidence for qualification in either direction (brief §2: a case "chosen after the fact" does not qualify); T06's registered ensemble is where such a comparison belongs.

### 8.4 What would reopen it (preregistered now)

### 9.10 Margins: every decision on a registered path is far from its threshold

The full-region twin's residual and Jacobian equal the implementation's compiled problem to roundoff at three registered states (*measured* by the design lane: residual ≤ 2.9e-12 relative, Jacobian ≤ 9.8e-16 relative), and its Newton-with-contract reproduces the implementation's measured region trajectories: r = 0.95 initializer 5 iterations, OFF-A 5, OFF-B [2, 1, 3] (T02 A22's counts and K03's OFF-B mechanism). ## 14. What this specification does not establish

- **Correctness of any solver.** It registers what a correct implementation must produce. The Newton twin's agreement with the implementation (§9.11) is evidence that the twin is the implementation's system, not a test of the new code; the PTC twin has nothing to agree with yet.
- **A qualified PTC family.** The residence-time family is experimental: it improves no tested basin (§8.3). Nothing here says PTC is useless in general; it says the registered comparison does not show it useful, and that on the A02 region it cannot help by structure. V14 stays incomplete until a later package qualifies a family on a preregistered case (§8.4).
- **That the continuation converges wherever a root exists.** A frozen-signature path cannot cross a phase boundary (HOM-03, HOM-04 stall there, typed and bracketed); folds and turning points are not followed (arclength deferred); a specification whose path has no root at some λ stalls. The family scan found one natural stall in 180 runs.
- **That the homotopy's type covers anything but promoted specifications.** A region without one has no continuation (`unsupported`); SYN-001 under `eo` is such a region.
- **Attraction far from the root, or dynamic stability of the process.** §6.6 is local. PTC "can favor some roots over others and does not prove dynamic stability" (blueprint §7.5); `claims.dynamic_stability` stays `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- **That the residence-time model is a physical model of the SYN-001 vessels.** It is the pseudo-time model of §6.2 — a vessel under ideal proportional control with a common residence time — with no vessel data behind θ.
- **Behaviour on energy dynamics.** With pinned temperatures the energy rows carry no mode; no registered case has a free vessel temperature with a genuine thermal mode (NET-07, T06).
- **The A04-ensemble comparison** (§8.3's measured paragraph): exploration, not a registered result.
- **A consistent K04 tolerance for lifted splits** (F9, Q8): T04 promises certificate verdicts only outside ADR 0007 D2.4's band and does not amend K04's tolerances; `SYN-001-A02-355` from 358 K has no promised verdict. Nor does T04 implement K04 §4.7's flash-outlet clause (F10).
- **A broad basin comparison.** The registered lattice admits 18 starts in six compositions (F14); the verdict rests on them and on the structural result of §6.7, not on coverage of the start space.
- **Performance** (the recompilation cost per λ-level, the 1.9–8.8× PTC cost) beyond SYN-001; **empirical validity** of anything (synthetic fixture); **human numerical or process-modelling review** (`review.*` stay `pending`).
