## 1. Scope, authority, and what is out of scope

**What K03 delivers.** A damped Newton with a bound-aware line search on a declared scaled residual merit function (blueprint §7.3), applied to two residual problems: the **analytic linear recycle** NUM-02 (derivation §8) and the **SYN-001 tear residual** `R(t) = G(t) − t` on the three recycle component flows (blueprint §7.2, plan §3.2). Around it: physical scales from registered nominals applied *before* initialization and frozen within an attempt (D06, ADR 0001 D3.5); the §7.4 initializer chain with every candidate checked and every rejection logged; a bounded phase-attempt controller that freezes the phase set within an attempt and restarts on `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED` ([A01], D09); a structural rank policy for the two consistent-redundant pressure rows K02 found; an admissibility check that excludes the trivial roots of the lifted equilibrium; three budgets with typed exhaustion; a typed outcome vocabulary mapped onto blueprint §8.2; and the five schema field sets plan §2.2 assigns to K03.

**Design invariants.**

1. **Expectations are closed forms.** Every expected value in §13–§14 is from `benchmarks/k03/reference_values.yaml` (closed forms of derivation §5.1) or from `benchmarks/syn001/reference_values.yaml` (P01, 20 digits). Where a number is a prototype observation it is labelled *measured* and used only to argue a tolerance or to bound an iteration count; it is never the expectation.
2. **Residual and Jacobian describe the same function at the same state.** For the tear problem this is made *checkable* (§3.4): at every Jacobian evaluation the inner rows are re-evaluated and their scaled residual is bounded; a violation is a typed failure, not a warning.
3. **No accidental zeros.** The 360 K flash has `K_B = 1` exactly, which makes two entries of every two-phase tear Jacobian vanish identically; a 365 K Jacobian-only state is registered *because* no entry vanishes there (§13.4), and the 360 K zeros are asserted as exact zeros beside nonzero neighbours.
4. **Unsupported is a result.** Every path on which K03 cannot proceed returns a typed outcome (§11); no path returns silence or a best iterate dressed as success (CLAUDE.md; plan §1).
5. **Every claim this document makes about its own numbers is executable** in `k03_reference.py --check` (§15.2).

**Out of scope** (brief §8, restated so nothing below is read as delivering it): K04's certificate and regularity screen (K03 records the final Jacobian's identity and touches nothing regularized); pseudo-transient continuation (T04); homotopy beyond the initializer chain (T02); multi-start and root completeness (T03); rank diagnostics beyond the structural pressure rule (T06); successive substitution and Anderson acceleration on the tear (blueprint §7.2 names them; V13/T01 — the (1 − r) contraction along the ray recorded by K02 is the fact they would be measured against); a full equation-oriented Newton on the 47-variable system (§2.3, deferred to T02 with its reasons); any change to the backend (ADR 0003), the canonicalization (ADR 0002) or a unit model (K02).

## 2. The verdict: which residual Newton runs on, and how `dR/dt` is obtained

### 2.1 Decision

**Newton runs on the three-dimensional tear residual `R(t) = G(t) − t`. `G(t)` is K02's sequential traversal. `dR/dt` is the exact block elimination (Schur complement) of the assembled sparse equation-oriented Jacobian, evaluated at the traversal's reconstructed state, factorized by SuperLU.** This is reading (A) of the brief's §4, and it is what the plan's K03 constraint says: "the tear Newton needs dR/dt through the unit chain. Use the assembled sparse Jacobian of the reduced tear residual from the selected backend as the primary path; a finite-difference tear Jacobian is retained only as a test oracle."

Register entry: R-010.

### 2.2 Reasoning

1. **It is what the plan and the gate say.** Plan §4.2 titles K03 "damped Newton on analytic and flash recycle residuals"; G00 requires "one numerical tear"; blueprint §7.2 defines the recycle problem as `R(t) = G(t) − t = 0`. The plan's constraint sentence exists precisely because, on a tear residual, the naive derivative is finite differences of the traversal; it demotes that to a test oracle and names the assembled Jacobian as the primary path. Under reading (B) the sentence would be redundant.
2. **The traversal is the blueprint's Default v0.0 formulation.** §6.3's table names "procedural flash with active-phase management" as the default v0.0/v0.1 simulation formulation, and [A01] explicitly permits "sequential unit evaluation" to select phases at its own boundaries, "the accepted signature becomes the EO starting regime". The procedural flash *cannot* return a trivial root (its Rachford–Rice branch is chosen by the stability test, derivation §5.1), so finding 3 is not a hazard on the solve path (§8).
3. **The exact derivative is available at no formulation cost.** K02's `spec()` assembles all 49 rows and K01 differentiates them exactly and sparsely. The Schur complement of that Jacobian at the traversal's state *is* `dR/dt` when the inner rows vanish there, which they do to 2.0e-15 scaled (*measured*, §3.4). Measured agreement with the closed form of derivation §5.1: 5.3e-15 absolute, worst over five variants and three states each.
4. **What (A) costs and (B) would cost.** Reading (B) — a 47-variable Newton with the tear as a label — needs the same rank policy (§7), the same scales (§4) and the same SuperLU path, *and* a phase-branch machinery for the two lifted splits, because in the lifted form both trivial branches are exact roots of every row (K02 review S3) and a bound-aware Newton lands on them freely. That is T02/T03's "frozen phases, multiple-root evidence" (V15), not v0.0's "one closed recycle with damped Newton" (plan week 3). Reading (C) — finite differences of the traversal — is demoted by the plan and is retained here as the oracle (§13.4).
5. **The Newton core is generic.** §5 is written for `F(x) = 0` with a residual, a sparse Jacobian, bounds and scales; the tear problem and NUM-02 are its two K03 instances; the full EO system is a later instance of the same core. Nothing in §5 knows about traversals.

### 2.3 What the choice forecloses, and what it does not

- It **forecloses** treating the heater outlet's phase regime as part of the tear attempt's frozen set (§9.1): the tear map does not depend on it, and freezing it turns the one-step registered cases into wall-crawls (*measured*, §9.5).
- It **does not foreclose** the full EO path: the same `CompiledProblem`, scales, elimination certificate and Newton core serve it; T02 adds the branch machinery.
- It **does not** make the traversal's inner tolerances part of the tear residual's definition: the inner rows are re-evaluated on the exact EO function and bounded (§3.4), so the solver cannot drift from the function K04 will verify.

## 3. The tear problem, precisely

### 3.1 Variables, rows and the partition

The assembled system (`Syn001Flowsheet.spec()`, K02) has 47 variables in allocation order and 49 rows in assembly order (the ids are those printed by `CompiledProblemMetadata.variable_ids` / `equation_ids`). Partition:

| Block | Members | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tear variables `t` | `S6.n.A`, `S6.n.B`, `S6.n.C` | 3 |
| Inner variables `u` | every other variable: `S1..S7 .n.i/.T/.P` except `S6.n.*` (32), `U-HEAT.Q`, `U-FLASH.Q`, `S3.vap.i`, `S3.liq.i`, `S3.V`, `S3.L`, `S4.N`, `S5.N` | 44 |
| Tear rows `ρ` | `U-SPLIT:SPLIT-recycle:A/B/C`, statement `n_rec,i − r n_in,i = 0` | 3 |
| Inner rows `φ` | every other row | 46, of which 44 are retained and 2 eliminated (§7) |

**Sign.** The tear rows evaluate `S6.n_i − r S5.n_i = t_i − G_i(t) = −R_i(t)`. The solver works with `R = −ρ`; every trace records `R`, in mol/s, never `ρ`.

**Bounds.** `t_i ≥ 0` (ADR 0001 D3.5: a tear at exactly 0 is a valid iterate; D3.6: no log transform). No upper bound. `T` and `P` are not tear coordinates (plan §3.2: the recycle's `T` and `P` are the flash specification; K02's `traverse` refuses a guess at any other `T`, `P`, review S5).

### 3.2 `G(t)` and `R(t)`

`G(t)` is `Syn001Flowsheet.traverse(StreamState(n = t, T = T_f, P = P))`: feed → mixer → heater → flash → splitter, each unit selecting its own phase state (blueprint [A01], last paragraph). `R(t) = traversal.recycle_residual`. A traversal that does not complete (`status ≠ "ok"`) has **no** residual (K02 enforces this in `Traversal.__post_init__`); the solver treats it as an invalid trial (§5.5) with the unit and status recorded.

Closed form (derivation §5.1, `k03_reference.py`): with `n = F + t`, `l(n)` the liquid of the TP flash of `n` at `(T_f, P)`, `R(t) = r l(F + t) − t`. Regimes: two-phase, `l_i = n_i (1 − β)/(1 + β(K_i − 1))` with `β` the Rachford–Rice root; liquid, `l = n`; vapor, `l = 0`.

### 3.3 Reconstruction of the EO state from a traversal

`x(t)` is the 47-vector assembled from the traversal, by name:

| Variables | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `Sk.n.i`, `Sk.T`, `Sk.P` for `k = 1..7` | `traversal.streams[Sk]` (`S6` is the guess itself, at `T_f`, `P`) |
| `U-HEAT.Q`, `U-FLASH.Q` | `traversal.duties` |
| `S3.vap.i`, `S3.liq.i`, `S3.V`, `S3.L` | the TP-state kernel at the traversal's `S3` (`tp_state(provider, S3, context)`): `vapor.n`, `liquid.n`, their sums. This is an exact-cache hit (*measured*: 0 provider calls), because the heater already flashed `S3` at (350 K, P). At a liquid heater outlet it gives `vap = 0`, `V = 0` exactly — the trivial branch *is* the physical one there |
| `S4.N`, `S5.N` | `Σ S4.n`, `Σ S5.n` |

### 3.4 The Schur complement, and the consistency check that makes it honest

At `x(t)`, with the scaled Jacobian `Ĵ = S_F⁻¹ J S_x` (§4) partitioned into `Ĵ_ρt` (3×3), `Ĵ_ρu` (3×44), `Ĵ_φt` (44×3), `Ĵ_φu` (44×44, the *retained* inner rows, §7):

    d R̂ / d t̂  =  −( Ĵ_ρt − Ĵ_ρu · Ĵ_φu⁻¹ · Ĵ_φt ),        dR/dt = S_ρ (dR̂/dt̂) S_t⁻¹,

where `Ĵ_φu⁻¹ · Ĵ_φt` is three SuperLU solves against one factorization (ADR 0004), and the leading minus sign is §3.1's row sign. For SYN-001 `S_ρ = S_t = 3 mol/s`, so `dR/dt = dR̂/dt̂` numerically; the implementation applies the transformation regardless (A31 checks it on NUM-02 with unequal scales).

**Consistency check (mandatory, every Jacobian evaluation).** The EO residual `F(x(t))` is evaluated on the same `CompiledProblem` under the same context, and

    η(t) = max over inner rows φ of |F_φ(x(t))| / S_F,φ   ≤   η_inner = 1e-10.

If `η > η_inner`, the Jacobian is not the derivative of the function the traversal computed; the attempt terminates with `INNER_SOLVE_INCONSISTENT` naming the worst row. Floor and margin: *measured* worst `η` = 2.0e-15 (row `U-MIX:MIX-energy` at `t*`, r = 0.95); the registered tolerances in scaled units are all ≈ 1e-8 (3.1e-8/3, 1.01e-3/1e5, 1e-6/100), so `1e-10` sits five orders above the floor and two below the point where an inner solve would perturb a certified quantity. Pairing identity: the `EvaluationResult` and `JacobianResult` at `x(t)` must have equal `model_version`, `constants_sha256`, `state_sha256`, `phase_signature` (ADR 0008 D2.4); a mismatch is `EVALUATION_ERROR`.

**Regime-freeness of the lifted function.** The lifted EO function has no phase switch (K02: every enthalpy block is phase-specific by declaration); the regime lives in the state. The compiled problem is therefore evaluated with `EvaluationContext.phase_signature = None`, and A33 asserts its residual and Jacobian at `x(t)` are bit-identical under contexts differing only in `phase_signature`. The attempt's frozen set (§9) is orchestration state, not an argument of the compiled function.

**Closed-form checks of the Schur complement** (§13.4, A20–A22): at every registered state the 3×3 agrees with `k03_reference.py`'s `dR_dt` to 1e-12; on the ray, `J(t*) t* = −(1 − r) t*` (the affine-ray relation `R(s t*) = (1 − r)(1 − s) t*` differentiated in `s`); in the liquid and vapor regimes it is exactly `(r − 1) I` and `−I`.

## 4. Scales

### 4.1 Construction (ADR 0001 D3.5; blueprint §7.3)

Scales come from the registered physical nominals (derivation §9, registry `scales`): component flow **3 mol/s**, temperature **100 K**, pressure **1e5 Pa**, duty **1e5 W**. They are assigned **by quantity kind**, never by iterate value, and they are computed *before* the initializer chain runs (D06). `x = x_ref + S_x x̂` with **`x_ref = 0` for every variable**: a Newton step is invariant under the affine offset, the merit function does not involve `x̂` itself, and an offset would have to be chosen for `T` and `P` without any registered nominal for it. `F̂ = S_F⁻¹ F`, `Ĵ = S_F⁻¹ J S_x`.

### 4.3 A row or column the nominals do not name

The kind of each variable and row is taken from the unit's declaration (`DeclaredEquation.dimension` for balance rows; the port `kind` for stream variables; the unit-owned duty is `heat_rate`). Where the kind is absent or is not one of {`molar_flow`, `temperature`, `pressure`, `heat_rate`, `molar_flow²`}, plan construction fails with **`SCALE_UNAVAILABLE`** naming the id. A silent `1.0` is forbidden: an unscaled pressure column against a flow row gives κ₂ ≈ 1e10 (*measured* 6.7e9–1.1e11 unscaled versus 28–1440 scaled on the 44×44 block), which is exactly the failure a default would hide. The SYN-001 assembly names every kind; the state that makes this bite is a `ProblemSpec` with one row whose dimension is `None` and whose accumulation is `algebraic` (A32).

### 4.4 Freeze and refresh

Scales are fixed when the `SolvePlan` is built and are **frozen within an attempt**. A refresh opens a recorded scale *segment* (`AttemptContext.scale_segment`, §12.4). **K03 v0.0 never refreshes**: the registered nominals are the only source, the segment counter exists and is always 0, and A34 asserts it. Blueprint §7.3's Jacobian equilibration "with caps" is a later refinement; SuperLU's own equilibration is switched off and is in any case inert on the `splu` path (ADR 0004 D1.3).

## 5. The Newton core

Written for a generic square problem `F(x) = 0`, `x ∈ ℝⁿ`, with lower bounds `lo` (upper bounds absent in K03 but handled symmetrically), scales `S_x`, `S_F`, a residual evaluator returning either `F(x)` with a phase signature or a typed non-evaluation, and a Jacobian evaluator. Constants are `SolvePolicy` fields (§12.1) with these registered defaults.

### 5.1 Merit and direction

    φ(x) = ½ ‖F̂(x)‖₂² = ½ Σ_i (F_i / S_F,i)²           (declared scaled residual merit, blueprint §7.3)
    Ĵ Δx̂ = −F̂,   Δx = S_x Δx̂                             (one SuperLU factorization, ADR 0004)

For a Newton direction `∇φ·Δx̂ = −2φ`, so the Armijo condition below is the standard one.

### 5.2 Convergence test — on the unscaled residual, against the registered tolerance, never on the step

Converged at iterate `x_k` iff **every** row satisfies `|F_i(x_k)| ≤ a_i + r_i s_i` with the registered constants for the row's kind (ADR 0001 D6; for tear rows `a = 1e-9 mol/s`, `r s = 1e-8 × 3 mol/s`, so `τ_R = 3.1e-8 mol/s`). The test is made **before** any Jacobian evaluation at `x_k`: an already valid root converges at iteration 0 with no factorization (blueprint §7.3, A11). A small step is never evidence of convergence (§5.7). Verification tolerances are independent of numerical scaling (§4).

### 5.3 Bound-aware step and exact landing

With `d = Δx`: `α_max = min(1, min_{i: d_i < 0, x_i + d_i < lo_i} (lo_i − x_i)/d_i)`. If `α_max = 0` (a component sits on its bound and `d` points outward) the attempt ends **`BOUND_BLOCKED`** — no trial can be taken along `d`. Otherwise the first trial is `α = α_max`, and **every component that reaches its bound at `α_max` is set to the bound value exactly** (`+0.0`, not `x_i + α d_i`, which may be `−4e-16`). A trial on a bound is an ordinary trial: it is accepted or rejected by §5.4.

*Amended 2026-09-25 (design lane, T05b ruling round Q-S11 (b); `docs/reviews/T05b-review.md` S2; register R-064).* **Structural zeros are released before `BOUND_BLOCKED` is declared.** When `α_max = 0`, let `Z₀` be the free components that sit exactly on a finite lower bound (`x_i == lo_i`, after ADR 0001 D1.5's signed-zero normalization) both at the attempt's opening state and at the current iterate. A set `Z ⊆ Z₀` is **released** when the rows `R(Z)` — every row `r` with `F_r(x) == 0.0` exactly and every nonzero Jacobian entry `J_{r,c}(x)` in a column `c ∈ Z` — number exactly `|Z|`. Then the exact Newton step is zero on `Z`: `J` is nonsingular (its factorization just succeeded), so the rows `R(Z)` are independent and `J_{R(Z),Z}` is square and nonsingular, and `J_{R(Z),Z} d_Z = −F_{R(Z)} = 0`. The union `Z*` of all released sets is itself released (for two released sets the closed rows of the union number at least `|Z₁| + |Z₂| − |Z₁ ∩ Z₂|`, and independence caps them there), so `Z*` is well defined — it is the square-and-overdetermined column part of the Dulmage–Mendelsohn decomposition of the rows closed on `Z₀` against `Z₀`; any algorithm that finds it will do. `Z*` has `d_{Z*} := 0` exactly — the factorization's roundoff there (e.g. `−1e-30`) is discarded, as K04's projection discards it at a converged state (ADR 0013 D1) — and `α_max` is recomputed. `BOUND_BLOCKED` is declared only if a component outside `Z*` still blocks, and then with `blocked_by` computed from the original `d`, exactly as before. No record is added: the step taken is the exact Newton step on `Z*`. *Why this is not a relaxation:* no bound is crossed, no tolerance moves, and a component is released only where the exact step is provably zero — never on a real outward direction (BND-02's `R = x + 1` has `F ≠ 0` and stays blocked), never on a component that reached its bound during the attempt (a disappearing phase's component — T02 §6.3.3's mechanism — was positive at the opening), never on an underdetermined set. *The case it exists for:* an absent component (feed flow exactly `+0.0`) at a lifted split — its product flows, its mole row and its equilibrium row form a closed square set with `F = 0` at every iterate — which ended NP-2 and NP-G `BOUND_BLOCKED` at iteration 0 on `S2.n.A` after a restart into two phases (review P5, P6, *measured*). *Inertness:* the rule acts only where an attempt would otherwise end `BOUND_BLOCKED` with every blocker in `Z*`; every attempt that converges today and every registered `BOUND_BLOCKED` whose blockers were positive at its opening is bitwise unchanged. The build lane proves it on the gate (K03 A00–A35, SYN-001's identity, T02's floats, T02–T04 fixtures and keys, the `t05` key) and reports every registered value that moves (T05b B33 (d) names the one expected: SC-2 under `T05-W13`).

*Note 2026-09-25 (design lane, T05b Q-S13; `docs/reviews/T05b-review.md` re-review S-W1 and N-W7; R-064).* **"The attempt's opening state" is the attempt's, not the call's.** A caller that runs one attempt through several `solve_newton` calls — T04's homotopy corrector, which starts each call from the last accepted λ-level — passes the attempt's opening state (T04 §4's `x⁰`) through a keyword (`opening=`); every other caller passes none, and the call's start is the opening, as before. Without it a phase that vanished at an earlier λ-level (`v = 0`, `V = 0`: `n` equilibrium rows and the `V` definition closed on `{v_i, V}` with `F = 0`) would qualify as a structural zero and be released — the disappearance this paragraph excludes. T05b B35. **Scope:** the release is the Newton core's (`solve_newton`). T04's PTC core — its pseudo-steps and its one-step polish, which cite this section by reference — keeps this section's first paragraph as its `α_max` rule (T04 §7.5's note).

### 5.4 Line search

Trials at `α = α_max, α_max/2, …, α_max/2^{k_max}`, `k_max = 20`. A trial is **accepted** iff its evaluation succeeds with the attempt's signature (§9) and

    φ(x + α d) ≤ (1 − 2 c α) φ(x),     c = 1e-4.

A trial is **rejected**, with its reason recorded, when (i) the evaluation fails with a typed non-`ok` status (`invalid_trial_state`, `out_of_domain`, `unsupported`, `not_converged`) — reason `invalid_trial`; (ii) the evaluation succeeds but reports a signature other than the attempt's — reason `phase_update_required`; (iii) the Armijo test fails — reason `armijo`. Rejections (i)–(ii) halve `α` exactly as (iii) does: shortening the step is blueprint §7.7's authorized action for an invalid trial, and a phase change one step away is treated first as an overshoot (§9.3). If no trial is accepted, the iteration ends in **`LINE_SEARCH_FAILED`** unless §9.3 converts it.

### 5.5 What a failed evaluation is

A typed non-`ok` status from the traversal or the compiled problem is a *result*: the unit id, the status and the message are recorded on the trial event. Nothing is retried at the same point; nothing is clipped. `error` from a unit is not a rejection but an **`EVALUATION_ERROR`** outcome (it means a defect, not a bad trial).

*Amended 2026-09-24 (design lane, on the T04 implementation review's M1; T04 §7.3).* The rule covers the Jacobian: **a Jacobian evaluation at an accepted iterate that is not `ok` ends the attempt `EVALUATION_ERROR`**, whatever its status, because the residual there evaluated `ok` and a failing derivative is a defect. A failed Jacobian is never handed to the linear solve: on the region path it was, as an all-zero matrix, and became `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED` or a SuperLU crash. No registered path reaches it; every registered trajectory is unchanged (the build lane's hex dump, `0b4ceb4`).

### 5.6 Iteration budget

At most `max_iterations_per_attempt = 50` accepted iterations per attempt (registry). Reaching it with the residual unconverged is **`BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`** with `budget = "newton_iterations"`.

### 5.7 Stagnation

Let `q_k = φ(x_{k+1})/φ(x_k)` for accepted steps. The attempt ends **`STAGNATION`** when `q > 0.99` for `stagnation_window = 5` consecutive accepted steps while the residual is not converged. The rule is scale-free and distinguishes the two blueprint §7.3 cases by construction: a small step *with* a small residual is caught by §5.2 before it can enter the window; a small step with a large residual has `q ≈ 1`. Where it bites: NUM-05 (§13.7), where `F(x) = x² + 0.01` has no root and the merit has a strict positive minimum; *measured* on the prototype: `STAGNATION` at iteration 8 with `|F| = 0.0100003`. A Newton converging quadratically never produces five consecutive `q > 0.99`.

### 5.8 Singular or inaccurate linear solve

`splu` raising `RuntimeError: Factor is exactly singular` → **`LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED`** with `reason = "exactly_singular"`; a recorded normalized linear residual above ADR 0004 D3's threshold → `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED` with `reason = "residual"`. Neither is retried with a regularized or least-squares step in v0.0 (blueprint §7.7: such a step is "numerical recovery", a later fallback edge, and it never authorizes discarding equations). Where it bites: NUM-02 at `r = 1` (§13.7), `dR/dt ≡ 0`.

## 7. The rank policy: two consistent-redundant pressure rows

### 7.1 The finding, restated as structure

Nine pressure rows over seven pressure columns: `FEED-P` (`S1.P − P_spec`), `MIX-pressure:0/1` (`S1.P − S2.P`, `S6.P − S2.P`), `HEAT-pressure` (`S3.P − S2.P`, `dP = 0` declared), `FLASH-P:vapor/liquid/inlet` (`S4.P − P_f`, `S5.P − P_f`, `S3.P − P_f`), `SPLIT-P:recycle/purge` (`S6.P − S5.P`, `S7.P − S5.P`). Rank 7 (K02 review). Every row is a manifest declaration and stays assembled (K02, blueprint §7.7). A square Newton on 46 inner rows and 44 inner unknowns cannot be factorized; a least-squares step is not a primary path (blueprint §3.1 fixes the factorization as SuperLU; §7.7 makes regularized least squares a recovery edge, and even then "not permission to discard equations").

### 7.2 Decision: structural alias elimination with a certificate

Register entry R-011. The rows are linear in the pressure columns with coefficients `±1` and a constant. That is a **graph**: nodes are the pressure columns plus one node `CONST`; each row is an edge (`P_a — P_b`, or `P_a — CONST` for a specification). A row that closes a cycle is linearly dependent on the rows of the cycle's tree path, and the dependency is *consistent* iff the signed constants around the cycle sum to zero.

**Algorithm (deterministic).** Visit rows in assembled order. A row qualifies iff every nonzero of its Jacobian row lies in a column of kind `pressure` and every coefficient is exactly `±1.0` (checked on the Jacobian at the initial state; the pattern is state-invariant, A30). Maintain a union–find forest over the nodes. A qualifying row whose two nodes are already connected is **eliminated**; otherwise it is retained and its edge added. For each eliminated row `e`, take the tree path between its nodes; the retained rows on the path with their orientation signs `σ_k` give the identity

    F_e(x) − Σ_k σ_k F_k(x) = m_e     for every x,

with `m_e` the constant mismatch. `m_e` is evaluated at **two** states differing in every pressure coordinate; the two values must agree to `1e-9 × 1e5 Pa` (linearity witness) and `|m_e| ≤ a_P = 1e-2 Pa` (consistency; ADR 0001 D6). If any `|m_e| > a_P`: the plan is not built and the outcome is **`SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`** naming `e`, the path and `m_e`. If a qualifying-but-unclassifiable row appears (three or more pressure columns, or a coefficient not `±1`) or if after elimination the inner block is not square: **`UNSUPPORTED_RANK_STRUCTURE`**.

**Registered result for SYN-001** (assembled order of K02; registered as data in `k03_reference.py`, not computed there). **Convention of the "Equals" column:** the signs `σ_k` are those of the identity stated above, `F_e(x) − Σ_k σ_k F_k(x) = m_e`, the identity `rank.py` certifies and K04 §4.2 re-verifies; the column lists what the row *equals* up to the constant, not what must be added to it to vanish. Closed form of both constants: `m_e = P_spec − P_f`. *(Stated explicitly 2026-09-23, after Frank asked that the correction below be argued rather than accepted; the alternative reading — "the combination to add" — is the only one under which the first version's second row was right, and it is excluded by the first row of this same table, which is constant only under the stated identity.)*

| `U-FLASH:FLASH-P:inlet` | `+FEED-P − MIX-pressure:0 + HEAT-pressure` | 0 |
| `U-SPLIT:SPLIT-P:recycle` | `+FEED-P − MIX-pressure:0 + MIX-pressure:1 − FLASH-P:liquid` | 0 |

### 7.3 What elimination is and is not

The eliminated rows are **not discarded**: they are evaluated at every accepted iterate as part of the §3.4 consistency check (they are inner rows), they are listed in `SolvePlan.eliminated_rows` with their certificates, and K04's reconstruction re-evaluates all 49 rows. Elimination is the blueprint's "alias elimination" (§7.2, [A08] "after legitimate fixed-variable/alias elimination"), performed by the orchestrator where the flowsheet is visible, which is why K02 correctly refused to do it in a unit. It is not a numerical rank decision: no threshold on a singular value is involved, and a numerically rank-deficient inner block after elimination is `LINEAR_SOLVE_FAILED`, reported, not repaired. Generalizing beyond `±1` pressure copies (nonzero drops as parameters are still `±1`; a pressure *ratio* is not) is T01/T06.

The K02 review's remark stands and is repeated as a finding (§18): `FLASH-P`'s inlet clause is redundant only because every drop is zero; with a nonzero heater drop it becomes an *inconsistency* that this policy will correctly report as `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`. Whether the P01 manifest should declare it is a later ADR's question.

## 8. The trivial roots of the lifted equilibrium

### 8.1 Where they can and cannot arise

`v_i L − K_i l_i V = 0` is satisfied identically by `v = 0` (all liquid) and by `l = 0` (all vapor), whatever the feed (K02 review S3, register R-008). Under §2 the solve path never *produces* one: the traversal's TP-state kernel selects the branch by derivation §5.1's stability order, and the lifted split in `x(t)` is *read off* that kernel (§3.3). The hazard would return with a full EO Newton (T02) and with any path that took the lifted variables as free iterates.

### 8.2 The admissibility check on the converged answer (blueprint §6.3, "final phase admissibility check")

Specified now, because K04 will cite it and because it is the check that makes the trivial-root state *registered* rather than merely feared. At the final `x(t*)`, for each lifted stream `S ∈ {S3 at (350 K, P), (S4, S5) at (T_f, P)}` with liquid-phase composition `x_i = l_i / L` and vapor-phase `y_i = v_i / V` where the phase flows:

| Branch found | Admissible iff | Registered value at the state that makes it bite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `V = 0` (all liquid) | `Σ_i x_i K_i ≤ 1 + ε_adm` | K02's registered trivial-root state (`test_the_lifted_equilibrium_admits_a_trivial_root_that_k03_must_reject`): once-through S3 forced all-liquid, `Σ x K = 1.0703142190807741552` → **INADMISSIBLE** |
| `L = 0` (all vapor) | `Σ_i y_i / K_i ≤ 1 + ε_adm` | 420 K flash, `Σ y/K = 0.31715802235115620433` → admissible |
| `V > 0` and `L > 0` | `v_i, l_i ≥ 0` and `Σ_i (y_i − x_i) ≤ 1e-10` (the normalized-composition tolerance) | every 360 K flash outlet; nominal S3 (`V = 0`, `Σ x K = 0.96231656003527667518`) admissible |

`ε_adm = 1e-12`. Finding 4 sets it: at exact saturation double precision evaluates `Σ z K` to `1 + 2e-16` (K02, the registered recycle), the single-phase and two-phase roots coalesce on that surface (the lifted block's determinant `L³(1 − Σ x K)` vanishes there, K02 review S3), so a root within `1e-12` of the surface is the same root on either branch and must not be rejected for a rounding direction; `1e-12` is four orders above that rounding and eight below the smallest registered margin (`1 − 0.9623 = 0.038` at nominal S3). The check is executed by K03 on the converged state, its verdict is carried in the result (`admissibility: ADMISSIBLE | INADMISSIBLE(stream, branch, value)`), and an `INADMISSIBLE` verdict converts a would-be `CONVERGED` into **`PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED`** with the admissible signature — on the tear path this cannot fire (§8.1), so A26 exercises the check function directly on K02's registered state and A27 on the five converged variants.

### 8.3 Initialization on the physical branch

The other half of blueprint §6.3's remedy is that nothing starts on a trivial branch: every lifted value in `x(t)` comes from the kernel at the traversal's state (§3.3). There is no separate "lifted initializer" in K03.

## 9. The phase-attempt contract ([A01], D09)

Register entry R-012. Interim normative text for what plan §4.1 pre-allocates as ADR 0005 (§17, Q2).

### 9.1 The signature

An **attempt signature** is the tuple of phase regimes of the phase-selecting units *whose selection the attempt's residual depends on*. For the SYN-001 tear residual that is exactly the flash: `σ = (("U-FLASH", regime),)`, `regime ∈ {TWO_PHASE, LIQUID, VAPOR}`, derived from the traversal's stream signatures by the fixed map `(S4, S5) ∈ {(VAPOR, LIQUID) → TWO_PHASE, (ZERO_FLOW, LIQUID) → LIQUID, (VAPOR, ZERO_FLOW) → VAPOR}`.

**Not in the signature, and why.** The heater outlet's regime (S3 at 350 K) affects `U-HEAT.Q` and the lifted split, which are dead-end variables of the inner system: `S3.n = S2.n` by the mole balance, so `G(t)` does not depend on it, and neither does `dR/dt` (the Schur complement's dependence on the lifted S3 block is through columns that reach only `HEAT-duty`). *Measured* consequence of freezing it anyway (§9.5): the nominal case's one-step landing on `t*` from `G(0)` is rejected as a phase change because S3 flips from `TWO_PHASE` at `0.5 t*` to `LIQUID` at `t*`, and the solver crawls to a wall it does not need to cross. The S3 regime is *recorded* on every accepted iterate (it is part of the state K04 verifies) but not frozen. Dormancy of `S6`/`S7` (`ZERO_FLOW` at `t = 0` or at 420 K) is likewise not a phase selection (ADR 0001 D3.5: a zero tear is a valid iterate) and is not in the signature. The generic rule — the signature covers phase selections in blocks upstream of or containing the tear rows in the block-triangular form of the inner system — is T01's to compute; K03 declares the SYN-001 instance.

### 9.2 Opening an attempt

The first attempt opens with the signature the accepted initializer's traversal reports ([A01]: "the accepted signature becomes the EO starting regime"). The attempt's `AttemptContext` (§12.4) fixes: the signature, the frozen scales, the one `EvaluationContext` used for every compiled-problem call, the flowsheet context used for every traversal, the checkpoint it opened from and the reason.

### 9.3 `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED`

A trial whose traversal succeeds with `σ' ≠ σ` is rejected with reason `phase_update_required` and the step is halved (§5.4) — once, because a Newton overshoot across a boundary with the root on this side is the common case and a restart would then cycle. The attempt **ends with `PHASE_UPDATE_REQUIRED`** when either

- `phase_wall_patience = 2` consecutive iterations each had at least one `phase_update_required` rejection in their line search (the wall is persistent, not an overshoot), or
- a line search fails (§5.4) or stagnation fires (§5.7) and at least one `phase_update_required` rejection occurred in the last `stagnation_window` iterations.

The **restart point** is the phase-change-rejected trial with the *largest* `α` in the last line search of the closing attempt (first encountered on ties — deterministic), and the new attempt's signature is that trial's. It is a point *inside* the new regime whose residual is already known, which is what "restore a valid checkpoint … select a new phase set and restart" must mean on a path where the residual function selects phases itself: the closing attempt's last accepted iterate is retained as its `Checkpoint` (label `partial`) but cannot start the new attempt, because the traversal at it reports the old signature. On the restart the caches are kept (they are exact) and the derivative state is rebuilt (a new `AttemptContext`, a fresh factorization; [A01]).

### 9.4 Bounds, cycle detection, tie-breaking

At most `max_attempts = 5` attempts per solve; exceeding it is **`ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`** (budget class). A signature may be the signature of **at most one** attempt per solve; a restart into a signature already attempted is **`ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING`** (blueprint §7.6 cycle detection, the v0.0 rule; T03 generalizes to signatures that legitimately recur). Ties: attempts are numbered in order; restart point by largest `α`, first encountered; signature equality is tuple equality in the fixed unit order.

### 9.5 Finding 4 at this boundary

The saturated stream is `S6`, and `S6` is not a lifted stream and not in the signature: its regime is fixed by declaration (the mixer's `LIQUID` block; K02 review, Finding 3 verdict). The boundary is met in exactly two places, both handled: the *mixer's admissibility band* (register R-007, `|H_true − H_liq| / (dH/dT) ≤ 1e-6 K`) admits `t*` and, *measured*, the second Newton iterate from OFF-A, which lies on the domain boundary to 2.3e-16 because at 360 K the mixer boundary `Σ t_i (K_i − 1) = 0` does not involve `t_B` (`K_B = 1`) and the first Newton step already places `t_A` and `t_C` at their solution values; and the *admissibility epsilon* of §8.2. No registered variant has a *lifted* stream on its saturation surface (smallest margin 0.038), so the singular-Jacobian case at such a surface is not in K03's registered set; it is named as unestablished (§16) and the U-diagonal screen (§6) would show it.

### 9.6 Where the contract bites

`OFF-B` (§13.3): the flash is all-liquid at the start, its Newton fixed point `rF/(1 − r)` is an equimolar stream the mixer refuses (closed form: margin `−0.38374131925393851449`), the wall is confirmed after two iterations, the restart is `TWO_PHASE`, and the second attempt converges to `t*`. Cycle detection bites on a constructed controller test (A29): force a restart into a signature already used.

## 16. What this specification does not establish

- **Correctness of any solver.** It registers what a correct one must produce; the prototype figures marked *measured* are floors and observations from a scratch implementation that is not shipped and is not the expectation.
- **A certificate.** `CONVERGED` is a solver outcome; `VERIFIED` is K04's, on the reconstructed 49 rows and the unregularized Jacobian, and K03 provides only the identities K04 needs (A35).
