## 2. The verdict, in one paragraph

Every T05 model is K02's pattern with one new ingredient: an outlet whose **temperature is fixed by an energy balance** instead of a specification. In the EO face that is K02's lifted two-phase outlet with its `T` free; in the causal face it is a bracketed solve of `Ḣ_TP(n, T, P) = H*`, which is well posed because the TP-state enthalpy of a SYN-001 stream is continuous and strictly increasing in `T` with slope at least `n_tot c_p` (§4.2) — except for a single flowing component, where it jumps at the boiling point and the kernel takes the saturation route (`T = T_sat`, split by the lever rule). No provider capability is added, so no SYN-001 identity moves. The reactor's energy balance needs a statement the project never made: that `SYN-001-ref-v1` is a formation datum. ADR 0011 makes it, and its consequence is honest rather than convenient — every mass-conserving liquid reaction on SYN-001 is exactly thermoneutral, so the registered reactor cases that must see a heat of reaction are vapour or two-phase ones (`Σ ν_i L_i = +25 000 J/mol` for the registered `2A + B → 3C`). The separator and the exchanger keep every port in a declared phase, which makes the exchanger's terminal second-law check exact and keeps every unit to at most one lifted split, so ADR 0005's contract applies unchanged. The definition of done is falsifiable at the level of the function: at twelve registered trial states every row value and every Jacobian entry is registered to 20 digits, and the generator shows that every registered mutation — a dropped or sign-flipped term, a permuted component, a swapped phase, a permuted stoichiometry, a mis-placed efficiency — moves some row by at least `2.4e7` row tolerances, except six registered ones the SYN-001 data cannot see at all (§13.5).

### 3.4 Two ways to know a stream's phase, and the rule for choosing

A unit knows a stream's enthalpy either from a **lifted split** (R-008: the producer allocates the split and authors its rows; the stream may be `LIQUID`, `VAPOR` or `TWO_PHASE`) or from a **declared phase** (the unit writes the single-phase enthalpy and the evaluator applies R-007's admissibility criterion, `|Ḣ_TP − Ḣ_φ| / (∂Ḣ_φ/∂T) ≤ 1e-6 K`, as K02's heater and mixer do). T05 uses a lifted split exactly where a result may be two-phase — the PH flash, the valve and the reactor outlets — and a declared phase everywhere else. Consequence, and a normative invariant of v0.1: **every unit authors at most one lifted split**, so ADR 0005 D2's "one regime per phase-selecting unit" applies unchanged (R-039). A lifted stream wired to a port declared single-phase is refused at assembly by K02's block-id rule (the same `H_<S>_liquid` block fed from `<S>.liq.*` and from `<S>.n.*`), which is loud. So the pump's inlet and both exchanger inlets must be streams their producers did not lift (a feed, a mixer outlet, a flash's liquid or vapour port, a separator or exchanger outlet); the separator, the valve, the PH flash and the reactor may take a lifted inlet (`inlet_phase = None`) and read its producer's blocks, as K02's flash does. §11 wires every case accordingly.

### 3.5 The typed-failure grammar

Every T05 `UnitEvaluation` with a status other than `ok`, and every T05 `SpecificationError`, carries a **registered code as the first line of its message**, exactly (§13.3 lists all of them); free text may follow on later lines. Codes carry no measured float (R-029). A test compares `message.splitlines()[0]` with the registered code, byte for byte.

*Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round (§3 block, engineers' interpretation U1 (2)).* One exception, which §3.3 already implies: a provider refusal the unit has no code for — a status other than `ok` returned by the provider on a request inside its domain — is surfaced with the provider's own status and message (K02's pattern), so its first line is the provider's text. ## 4. The PH closure (normative; ADR 0011 D1)

### 4.1 Decision

A PH-type outlet is K02's TP-state outlet with the temperature free: the lifted rows of `lift_two_phase_stream` (or the flash-style pair of outlets) plus an energy row in which the unit's heat input is pinned rather than solved. The causal evaluator solves `Ḣ_TP(n, T, P) = H*` for `T` by the kernel of §4.4. The provider is asked only for what it already offers: `flash(TP)`, `h`, `lnK` and their `T`, `P` derivatives. **Rejected:** a provider PH flash — it needs a new `FlashRequest` field (the target enthalpy has nowhere to go in `StreamState`), which changes a frozen field set, and an edit to `thermo/syn001.py`, whose source hash is in every SYN-001 `model_version` through R-009's label; and it would be an opaque inner solve where the lifted form is exact and differentiable (R-008's reasoning). The K02 mixer keeps its registered subcooled-liquid restriction and typed failure (R-038): generalizing it in place would change its rows (a lifted outlet adds variables), hence `model_version` of every SYN-001 result.

### 4.2 Why the bracket is well posed (derivation)

For a flowing SYN-001 stream with at least two flowing components at fixed `P` in the domain:

- Single phase: `Ḣ^L = Σ n_i [c_p (T − T_r) + v_i (P − P_r)]`, `Ḣ^V = Σ n_i [c_p (T − T_r) + L_i]` — affine in `T` with slope `n_tot c_p`.
- Two phase: `Ḣ = Σ n_i h_i^L(T, P) + Σ v_i Δh_i`, `Δh_i = h_i^V − h_i^L = L_i − v_i (P − P_r) > 0` (`≥ 24 990 J/mol` on the domain; generator-checked at `P_min`, `P_r`, `P_max`). `v_i = n_i β K_i / (1 + β(K_i − 1))` is increasing in `β` and in `K_i`; `K_i` is increasing in `T` (`∂ ln K_i/∂T = Δh_i/(R T²) > 0`, SYN-001 §3); `β` is increasing in `T` (implicit differentiation of Rachford–Rice: `∂RR/∂β < 0`, `∂RR/∂K_i > 0`). So `dḢ/dT = n_tot c_p + Σ Δh_i dv_i/dT > n_tot c_p`.
- At the bubble and dew points `β` is continuous (0 and 1), so `Ḣ_TP` is continuous.

Hence `Ḣ_TP(T)` is continuous and strictly increasing with slope `≥ n_tot c_p`: the root on `[T_min, T_max]` is unique when it exists. *Generator-checked* on a 161-point grid for four compositions, including one with a zero component. With **one** flowing component `k` the two-phase region collapses to `T_sat(P)` (`ln K_k = 0`) and `Ḣ_TP` jumps there by `n_k Δh_k`: the provider's TP flash classifies `T_sat` as `LIQUID` (its liquid test comes first, SYN-001 §5.1), so no `T` reproduces an enthalpy inside the jump. The kernel's saturation route (§4.4 step 2) exists for exactly that state.

### 4.3 The EO face

Heater-style (valve, reactor): the outlet stream `S` owns `(n, T, P)`; the unit allocates `S.vap.i, S.liq.i, S.V, S.L` and authors `<U>:<EQ>-equilibrium:<c>` (`v_i L − K_i(T, P) l_i V`, kind `molar_flow_squared`), `<U>:split:<c>` (`v_i + l_i − n_i`), `<U>:Vdef`, `<U>:Ldef`. Flash-style (PH flash): the two outlets are the split, with `<S_V>.N`, `<S_L>.N` and `Ndef` rows (TPFlash's form). The outlet's enthalpy in the energy row is `Σ v_i h_i^V + Σ l_i h_i^L` at the outlet `(T, P)`. **`T` is a free column**; the row that fixes it is the energy row. Both trivial splits (`V = 0` with the stream two-phase, `L = 0` likewise) remain exact roots of the lifted rows (R-008); for a PH outlet the trivial root also moves `T` (the latent heat is not absorbed), and it is caught by admissibility, the independent split and the fresh-flash energy balance (INJ-T1, §12.5).

### 4.4 The causal kernel (normative)

`ph_state(provider, n, P, H*) → (status, code, T, split)`, for a flowing `n` (the caller handles a dormant stream, §4.7):

1. If `P` is outside the provider's domain → `out_of_domain`, `pressure_outside_domain(outlet)`.
2. **Saturation route** — exactly one component `k` has `n_k > 0`. Find `T_sat` with `ln K_k(T_sat, P) = 0` by bisection on the domain (`ln K_k` is increasing in `T`); if it has no sign change on the domain, go to step 3 on the whole domain. Else `H_L = Ḣ^L(n, T_sat, P)`, `H_V = Ḣ^V(n, T_sat, P)`: if `H_L ≤ H* ≤ H_V`, return `T = T_sat`, `β = (H* − H_L)/(H_V − H_L)`, `v_k = β n_k`, `l_k = n_k − v_k`, every other component zero; signature `TWO_PHASE` (`LIQUID` if `β = 0`, `VAPOR` if `β = 1`). If `H* < H_L`, restrict the bracket of step 3 to `[T_min, T_sat]`; if `H* > H_V`, to `[T_sat, T_max]`. *(Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round, review N2.)* The `T_sat` bisection has a budget of the same size as step 4's, counted separately; exhausting it is `not_converged`, `ph_not_converged`. The lever-rule answer passes through step 5's acceptance like the bracket route's. On SYN-001 both are satisfied by construction: the bisection closes in about 44 steps, and the split's enthalpy is affine in `β`. The route's EO face is not solved in v0.1 (§4.7 (b)).
3. **Bracket route.** `f(T) = Ḣ_TP(n, T, P) − H*` with `Ḣ_TP` from the provider's TP flash and phase enthalpies (K02's `tp_state`). If `f(T_lo) > 0` → `out_of_domain`, `ph_outside_domain(below)`; if `f(T_hi) < 0` → `out_of_domain`, `ph_outside_domain(above)`.
4. Iterate a bracketing method — bisection, optionally accelerated by a step that stays strictly inside the bracket — until `f = 0` exactly or no double lies strictly between the bracket ends; return the end with the smaller `|f|`. At most **200** evaluations of `f`, else `not_converged`, `ph_not_converged`.
5. Accept iff `|f(T*)| ≤ τ_E = 1.01e-3 W` (on either route); else `not_converged`, `ph_ill_conditioned`. This is an **acceptance test on the answer**, not a detector of ill-conditioning. *(Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R1. The earlier text said this step made a near-pure stream's ill-conditioning "visible instead of silent". It does so only when the conditioning also defeats the test; see "What `ok` guarantees" below.)* *(Amended 2026-09-25, T05b, ADR 0012 D2, register R-052: step 5 now accepts a candidate iff it satisfies the closure's own rows — material, equilibrium and energy — at K04's registered tolerances, and a candidate of this step that fails it goes to a **band route** in the vapour fraction when at least two components flow; `ph_ill_conditioned` is returned only when no route passes. Normative text: `docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §5.)*
6. The split is the provider's TP flash at `(T*, P)`.

Single-phase endpoints are the TP flash's explicit classification (`Σ z K ≤ 1` liquid, then `Σ z/K ≤ 1` vapour) at every trial; zero components are handled by the K-value form (no `ln 0`) and by step 2 when only one component flows. *Measured* 53-bit floor of `T*` against the 40-digit root on the eight registered PH-type cases: `≤ 9.8e-14 K` (`ref.measured.floors_53_bit.ph_kernel_T_K`), seven decades under the registered `1e-6 K`.

**What `ok` guarantees, and the near-pure class** *(ruling round Q-R1, 2026-09-25; register R-049).*

`ok` means one thing: at the returned `T*`, the returned split — the provider's own TP flash, or the lever rule on the saturation route — carries `H*` to within `τ_E`. The kernel does not assess how well that flash is determined.

- **Where conditioning fails.** One class fails on SYN-001: all flowing components but one, `k`, are traces. To first order in `z`, the traces' mole fraction, the two-phase range then lies within `z max_i max(|K_i − 1|, |1/K_i − 1|) / (∂ ln K_k/∂T)` of `T_sat` (the bubble and dew conditions linearized about `K_k = 1`). Once one ulp of `T` moves `Ḣ` by more than `τ_E`, no double meets a target inside the latent jump except by coincidence. The provider's Rachford–Rice is also below its own tolerance there, and returns its first bisection midpoint, `β = 1/2`.
- **Measured** (ruling round; A29 re-measures it). Streams `(ε, 2, 0)` and `(0, 2, ε)` mol/s at `P_r`. Targets `12 000 + φ · 60 000 W`: pure B's liquid enthalpy at `T_sat = 360 K` plus a fraction `φ` of its latent jump `2 L_B = 60 000 W`, with `φ ∈ {0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9}`.
  - At `ε ∈ {1e-12, 2e-12, 1e-9}` every `φ ≠ 0.5` is `ph_ill_conditioned`, with `|f|` from `2.6e-2 W` to `1.2e2 W`.
  - Every `φ = 0.5` is `ok`, with `β = 1/2` exactly, `|f| ≤ 2.6e-5 W` and `|T* − 360 K| ≤ 1.9e-8 K`.
  - At `ε = 1e-6` every `φ` is `ok`, with `|f| ≤ 9.8e-5 W`, and `T*` is strictly increasing in `φ` within `3.3e-5 K` of 360 K.
- **Both outcomes are correct reports.** An `ok` answer in this class is as accurate as `τ_E` allows:
  - `T*` lies inside the two-phase range.
  - By §4.2, `Ḣ = Σ n_i h_i^L + Σ v_i Δh_i`. So the energy target fixes the total vapour flow to within `(|f| + ε (c_p |T − T_r| + max_i L_i)) / min_i Δh_i + ε`, which is `≤ 5e-8 mol/s` for `ε ≤ 2e-9` (`Δh_i ≥ 24 990 J/mol`, `max_i L_i = 35 000 J/mol`). That is the resolution of `τ_E` itself.
  - The measured distance from the pure-component lever rule is `≤ 7e-10 mol/s`.
  - The traces' own split is poorly determined, and is bounded by their flow.
- **Rejected: a conditioning criterion.** For example, "refuse whenever adjacent doubles of `T` differ in `Ḣ` by more than `τ_E`". It would refuse the `φ = 0.5` answers, which are right to `τ_E`'s resolution, and it would add a threshold that no registered case calibrates.
- **Registered limitation** (§5.1, §18, §19 Q11). For a near-pure feed whose target lies inside the jump, whether the answer is `ok` or `ph_ill_conditioned` depends on where the target lies. Every outcome is typed or accurate to `τ_E`. A29 makes both halves executable.
- ***Superseded 2026-09-25 by T05b*** *(ADR 0012 D2; R-049 reversed by R-052).* The near-pure class is no longer a limitation: the band route answers every target inside the jump, and `ok` means the closure's rows hold at K04's tolerances (`docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §5.3). A29 is retired and replaced by T05b B01; the measurements above remain the evidence of why.

### 4.5 Derivatives

The residual rows are differentiated exactly by the backend through K01 (`derivatives[].method = ad`, regime `all`): the lifted form is polynomial in flows and smooth in `(T, P)` through the K and enthalpy blocks, whose declared-sparse analytic derivatives K02 already supplies. Within an attempt the regime is frozen by ADR 0005 and the pinned phase's variables and identically satisfied rows are removed (T02 §6.3.1), so the Jacobian is regime-aware by construction. The causal kernel supplies **no** derivative; the sensitivity of an outlet state to an inlet state (the implicit `dT/dH*`, whose slope changes at each phase boundary) is declared `unavailable`, as for every K02 unit, and is never reported as zeros.

### 4.6 What the closure does not do

It makes no stability claim beyond the provider's two-phase classification (SYN-001 §5.1, ideal VLE, no third phase). It does not extrapolate: a target outside the domain bracket is `out_of_domain`.

### 4.7 Zero flow and a single flowing component: the two EO limitations T05 registers

*(Retitled 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R4 and Q-R8. It was "Zero flow, and the one singularity T05 introduces"; §4.7 (b) is the second limitation, found by review S3.)*

**(a) Zero flow.** A dormant inlet (ADR 0001 D3.1) gives dormant outlets whose temperature is a **retained label**: the inlet's `T` (PH flash, valve, pump, a duty-mode reactor) or the specified `T` (an outlet-temperature-mode reactor). The duty of a dormant PH flash or duty-mode reactor must be the specified one and the energy row forces it to 0, so a nonzero `Q_spec` is `error`, `duty_into_dormant_stream`.

**The EO face is singular there.** At exactly zero flow `∂Ḣ/∂T = Σ n_i c_p = 0`, so an outlet temperature fixed only by an energy balance is read by no row, or only by a copy row it shares with another such temperature. The registered structure (`ref.dormant_temperature_columns`, generator-checked): valve `S2.T` — no row; PH flash `S2.T`, `S3.T` — only `PHF-T`, together (rank one short); duty-mode reactor `S2.T` — no row; outlet-temperature-mode reactor — `RX-spec` (regular); pump `S2.T` — no row; separator — its copy rows (regular); exchanger with a dormant hot side — `S2.T` no row. No K02 unit had this: every K02 temperature is specified or copied. **v0.1 does not certify an EO solve in which such a temperature belongs to a dormant stream.** No registered coupled case contains a dormant PH-type outlet.

*Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R4 (W0.8, measured; `docs/t05-measurements.md` §W0.8).*

- **The rank loss is larger than the column structure.** The column structure above is the registered one, and it holds (A15). But the lifted outlet also contributes zero rows.
  - The region path runs a dormant split in its `TWO_PHASE` form (see "The branch record" below).
  - Each equilibrium row `v_i L − K_i l_i V` is bilinear in lifted flows that are all exactly zero, so every partial of that row is an exact zero.
  - That is **one zero row per component** (three for SYN-001), and the dormant temperature columns are among the null directions.
  - Measured on W0.8's two mini-flowsheets (VLV-Z's and PHF-Z0's inputs, 18 × 18 each): the zero rows are exactly `U-VLV:VLV-equilibrium:{A,B,C}` and `U-PHF:PHF-equilibrium:{A,B,C}`, and K04's screen reports `RANK_DEFICIENT`, rank 15 of 18.
- **What an EO solve returns** (this registers §19 Q4).
  - The traversal start is the exact root, because the causal evaluators return the dormant labels. The region Newton therefore meets its tolerance at iteration 0 without forming a Jacobian, and the outcome is `CONVERGED`.
  - The certificate is expected to be `UNVERIFIED`, with a `rank_limitation` (`RANK_DEFICIENT`) and no failing check (A28). This is argued, not yet measured: W0.8 ran K04's screen but not the verifier table.
    - At the dormant root every residual, material, energy and specification check is an exact zero.
    - The one-sided, admissibility, independent-split and declared-port checks are not applicable, because their streams are dormant.
    - K04's grade caps any regularity status other than `NO_RANK_LOSS_DETECTED` at `UNVERIFIED`.
    - If A28 measures `FAILED` instead, "never `VERIFIED`" still holds, but this text is wrong and is amended; it is not re-pinned.
  - The earlier text expected a typed solver failure on the rank path. That applies only when a Jacobian is formed, from a start that is not the root. No registered case has such a start, and its outcome is not registered.
  - Either way, a dormant PH-type outlet is never `VERIFIED`.
- **The branch record.** `branch_found` (T03 §8.2 as amended, applying K03 §8.2's rule `V = 0 < L` → `LIQUID`, `L = 0 < V` → `VAPOR`, else `TWO_PHASE`) reports a dormant split as `TWO_PHASE`. So does the attempt signature.
  - This is not a defect against ADR 0005, whose lattice is `LIQUID — TWO_PHASE — VAPOR`, with no `ZERO_FLOW`. Dormancy is not a phase selection (K03 §9.1).
  - The entry names the equation form the attempt ran, not a phase found.
  - The certificate's `phase_branch` reports the stream as `ZERO_FLOW` (design note §4.2). That is the record of dormancy.
  - Changing the branch rule or the lattice needs an ADR (R-029's fingerprint, ADR 0005 D2). It is deferred with the remedy (register R-050).
- **Remedy (deferred).** A `ZERO_FLOW` regime that pins the lifted flows at zero, drops the equilibrium rows and adds the label row `T_out − T_label = 0`. That changes ADR 0005's regime lattice.
- ***In force 2026-09-25 under T05b*** *(ADR 0012 D4 (c), D6, D7; R-050 reversed by R-053).* The `ZERO_FLOW` regime of every lifted split (pins, dropped rows, and for a PH-type split the label row in place of the energy row), `branch_found` `ZERO_FLOW` for `V = L = 0`, and the verifier's regularity on the zero-flow form: a dormant PH-type outlet is `CONVERGED` and `VERIFIED` (`docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §7–§9). A28 is retired and replaced by T05b B15. The non-lifted energy-determined outlets of the table above (pump, exchanger side) are not covered and stay never `VERIFIED` (T05b §17). ***Amended 2026-09-25, T05b second pass*** *(Frank's answer to T05b §18 Q2; ADR 0012 D12; register R-058):* they are covered too — the pump's outlet, an exchanger side whose outlet temperature is not the specification, and the K02 mixer's outlet with every inlet dormant get a zero-flow form (their energy row swapped for the label row, carried by a signature item `[<U>.<port>, ZERO_FLOW]`, `docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §7.6–§7.10), and are `CONVERGED` and `VERIFIED` (T05b B24–B27). No registered number of this document changes.

**(b) A single flowing component in the latent jump** *(ruling round Q-R8, 2026-09-25; review S3, measured; register R-050).*

- **The causal kernel solves it.** This is the saturation route (§4.4 step 2): `T = T_sat`, split by the lever rule, `TWO_PHASE` for `0 < β < 1`.
- **The EO rows are regular there** (argued). With `v_k = V` and `l_k = L`, the equilibrium row of `k` is `V L (1 − K_k(T))`. For `V, L > 0` that row fixes `T = T_sat`, and the energy row fixes `V`. Each absent component's pair of rows, `v_i + l_i = 0` and `v_i L − K_i l_i V = 0`, has determinant `L + K_i V > 0`.
- **The region path does not reach that state.**
  - ADR 0005's screen and closure conversions take their regime from the provider's TP flash.
  - That flash never reports `TWO_PHASE` for one flowing component: at `T_sat` its liquid test comes first (SYN-001 §5.1), and above `T_sat` the stream is vapour. So every attempt is pushed between `LIQUID` and `VAPOR`.
  - K04's independent split has the same blind spot (argued). A fresh flash at the double nearest `T_sat` gives `V = 0` or `V = n_k`, never `β n_k`. So even a correct EO root would be `FAILED` by the verifier.
- **Measured** (review P3, P4).
  - **Valve.** Inlet `(0, 2, 0)` at 370 K and `1.8e5 Pa`, LIQUID, to `P_r`.
    - Causal: `ok`, `T = 360 K`, `β = 0.0336`. This is exact: `β = (2 c_p·70 K + 2 v·8e4 Pa − 2 c_p·60 K)/(2 L_B) = 2 016/60 000`.
    - EO: ends `ACTIVE_SET_CYCLING`, also when re-seeded with the lever-rule split.
  - **PH flash.** PHF-6's inputs end `BOUND_BLOCKED` after one iteration.
  - **Control.** The same PH flash with `1e-6 mol/s` of A added converges.
- **v0.1 does not solve an EO system containing a PH-type outlet with exactly one flowing component and `0 < β < 1`.** The outcome is a typed failure, never `VERIFIED` (A30). No registered coupled case contains one.
- **The start is not the cause.** `initial_state` re-flashes a heater-style lifted outlet with the provider's TP flash (design note §2.2 step 5). On this route that gives `V = 0` where the unit's closure gave `β n_k`. Re-seeding from the exact split does not help, so the start is left as designed and its comment is corrected.
- **Remedy (deferred, beside (a)'s).** A phase classification that reports `TWO_PHASE` for a single flowing component at `ln K_k = 0`, in the kernel of ADR 0005 D3–D4 and in K04's independent split. That is an ADR 0005 change.
- ***In force 2026-09-25 under T05b*** *(ADR 0012 D4 (a)–(b), D5, D7, D8; R-050 reversed by R-053).* Under `T05b-phase-contract-v2` the kernel of a PH-type split is its PH closure at the split's own enthalpy (for one component its saturation route), its start is its unit's closure split and is not re-projected, and the verifier judges a temperature-degenerate split by the split it carries. P3 and P4 are `CONVERGED` and `VERIFIED` (T05b SC-1, SC-2). "The start is not the cause" above held under v1 only because T02 §6.2 re-projected the seeded split. A30 is retired and replaced by T05b B08–B09.

## 6. `syn001.conversion_reactor`

### 6.1 The reference convention (ADR 0011 D2; derivation)

The enthalpy of reaction `Δh_r = Σ ν_i h_i` is meaningful only if the `h_i` share one datum. ADR 0001 D5.1 fixes `h_i^L(T_r, P_r) = 0` for each component and says nothing about a common datum, because v0.0 had no reaction. ADR 0011 D2 **declares** `SYN-001-ref-v1` a formation datum: the pure liquids A, B, C have equal (zero) formation enthalpy and formation Gibbs energy at `(T_r, P_r)`. This is a declaration, not a derivation — the pseudo-components have no elemental composition (ADR 0001, `elemental_composition: null`) — and it changes no number. Consequences, each generator-checked:

- **Mass conservation forces `Σ ν_i = 0`.** `M_A = M_B = M_C`, so `Σ ν_i M_i = 0 ⇔ Σ ν_i = 0`.
- **Every liquid reaction is exactly thermoneutral:** `Δh_r^L = Σ ν_i [c_p (T − T_r) + v_i (P − P_r)] = (Σ ν_i)(…) = 0` at every `(T, P)` (equal `c_p`, equal `v_i`).
- **The vapour reaction enthalpy is `Δh_r^V = Σ ν_i L_i`**, constant. For the registered `ν = (−2, −1, 3)` (`2A + B → 3C`): `+25 000 J/mol` (endothermic in the vapour).
- For the record (equilibrium reactors are out of scope): `Δg_r^L = 0` at every `(T, P)`, so an ideal-liquid equilibrium reactor on SYN-001 would have `K_eq = 1`; `Δg_r^V = Σ ν_i L_i (1 − T/T_b,i)`.

The reference tools represent exactly this datum: IDAES with `h_form,liq = 0`, `h_form,ig = L_i`; DWSIM with ideal-gas formation enthalpies entered as `L_i` (`docs/reference-environments.md` on `wp/T06-refs`, §5.0 and §5.3).

**The energy balance is written in total-enthalpy form**, `Q + Ḣ_in − Ḣ_out = 0`: the heat of reaction is carried by the outlet's changed composition and is **never** added as a separate `ξ Δh_r` term, which would count it twice. A reactor is constructed only over a provider whose `describe().reference_convention` is in ADR 0011 D2's registered set `{SYN-001-ref-v1}`; otherwise `SpecificationError`, `reference_convention_not_reaction_consistent(<convention>)`.

### 6.3 Causal evaluator

(1) Dormant inlet → `ξ = 0`, dormant outlet (label `T_spec` or `T_in`), `Q = 0`; duty mode with `Q_spec ≠ 0` → `error`, `duty_into_dormant_stream`. (2) `ξ = X n_in,k/(−ν_k)`; the key's outlet `n_out,k = n_in,k − X n_in,k`, every other `n_out,i = n_in,i + ν_i ξ`; the first component (in component order) with `n_out,i < 0` → `out_of_domain`, `reactant_exhausted(<c>)`; no clipping (ADR 0001 D2.3). *Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R7 (review S2, measured; register R-051).*

- **What changed.** The key's outlet was `n_in,k + ν_k ξ`. At `X = 1` that is exactly `0.0` only when `|ν_k|` is a power of two; otherwise it comes out `−2.2e-16` for a fixed fraction of feeds. The review measured `6.6 %` at `ν_k = −3`, `5.9 %` at `−5` and `4.2 %` at `−7`, so the reactor refused a legitimate full conversion as `reactant_exhausted(<key>)`.
- **Why the new form is safe.** `n − X·n` is the same function, written so that rounding cannot push it below zero. For `0 ≤ X ≤ 1` and `n ≥ 0`, `fl(X n) ≤ n`, because rounding is monotone. So the key's outlet is never negative, and it is exactly `0.0` at `X = 1` for every `ν_k`. Only a non-key component can trigger the exhaustion test.
- **What did not change.**
  - A non-key reactant exhausted *exactly* by the data (RX-6) keeps its `< 0` test. A one-ulp refusal there is a boundary coincidence, and it is typed.
  - The rows keep `RX-mole` as written (`n_in,i + ν_i ξ − n_out,i`). They describe the same function; at the causal answer the key's row is a few ulps of `n_in,k`, far inside `τ`.
  - For `|ν_k| ∈ {1, 2}` the two forms are bitwise identical: `ν_k = −1` gives `n − fl(X n)`, and `ν_k = −2` gives `n − 2·(fl(X n)/2) = n − fl(X n)`. Every registered reactor case has `|ν_k| ∈ {1, 2}`, so RX-1…RX-8, RX-Z, RX-F1 and RX-F5 and every number of `ref` are unchanged.
  - RX-F1 still exhausts A (non-key): `1 − 2·0.6 = −0.2`.

(3) `P_out` in the domain, else `pressure_outside_domain(outlet)`. (4) Inlet enthalpy as §5.2 (3). (5) outlet_temperature: the TP split of `n_out` at `(T_spec, P_out)`, `Q = Ḣ_TP − Ḣ_in`; duty: `ph_state(n_out, P_out, Ḣ_in + Q_spec)`. Report `ξ` and `Q`.

### 9.3 Why no PS flash (derivation)

SYN-001's liquid entropy is `s_i^L = c_p ln(T/T_r)` (SYN-001 §2) plus ideal mixing, independent of `P` — the incompressible liquid has `(∂v/∂T)_P = 0`, so `(∂s/∂P)_T = −(∂v/∂T)_P = 0`. An isentropic compression is therefore **isothermal**, and its work is `∫ v dP = Σ n_i v_i (P_out − P_in) = Ḣ^L(T_in, P_out) − Ḣ^L(T_in, P_in)`, which is what `PUMP-work` writes using only `h`. The excess `(1/η − 1) W_s` heats the liquid by `(1/η − 1) v ΔP / c_p` (`0.0267 K` at `ΔP = 8e4 Pa`, `η = 0.75`). Both reference tools use the same work (`W = ΔP V̇/η`, equal to this for an incompressible liquid). A provider whose liquid entropy depends on `P` needs a PS route, and the manifest says so; blueprint §6.2 ("only when equipment needs it") is satisfied because this equipment does not.

### 10.2 Causal evaluator and its typed failures, in order

(1) A dormant side forces `Q = 0`; the specification must then be satisfiable — `duty` with `Q_spec = 0`, or an outlet temperature on the dormant side (it becomes that side's label), or the flowing side's outlet temperature equal to its inlet's — else `error`, `specification_unsatisfiable_with_dormant_side`. (2) Inlet enthalpies in the declared phases (R-007; `inadmissible_phase(hot_inlet|cold_inlet, <PHASE>)`). (3) `Q` from the specification; the other outlet temperature by inverting its declared-phase enthalpy. (4) Outlet temperatures in the domain, else `out_of_domain`, `outlet_outside_domain`. (5) R-007 on `hot_outlet` then `cold_outlet` → `unsupported`, `inadmissible_phase(<port>, <PHASE>)`. (6) `Q < 0` → `out_of_domain`, `heat_flow_reversed`. (7) `T_hi − T_co < 0` → `temperature_cross(hot_end)`; (8) `T_ho − T_ci < 0` → `temperature_cross(cold_end)`. A pinch (a terminal difference exactly 0) is allowed.

### 10.3 Why duty-coupled and not UA–LMTD

UA–LMTD is singular at equal terminal differences (removable, but a 0/0 in a residual), undefined at a cross, and wrong with a phase change on a side (the T–Q profile is not linear); it would still need the cross refusal. The duty-coupled form has none of these problems, carries the same energy content, and one specification closes it. UA is a design quantity v0.1 does not need (R-042).

### 10.4 Why the terminal check is exact here (derivation)

For a side in one phase at constant pressure with SYN-001's constant `c_p`, `Ḣ` is affine in `T` with slope `n_tot c_p` (§4.2). Parameterize the countercurrent exchanger by the duty `q ∈ [0, Q]` transferred between the hot inlet end and a cross-section: `T_h(q) = T_hi − q/C_h`, `T_c(q) = T_co − q/C_c`, so `ΔT(q) = (T_hi − T_co) − q (1/C_h − 1/C_c)` is **affine** in `q` and its minimum over `[0, Q]` is at a terminal: `ΔT(0) = T_hi − T_co`, `ΔT(Q) = T_ho − T_ci`. Heat flows from hot to cold everywhere iff `Q ≥ 0` and both terminals are `≥ 0`. The interior stays single-phase if both ends of each side are: at fixed `P` every `K_i` increases with `T`, so a liquid's `Σ x K` is largest at its hottest end and a vapour's `Σ y/K` largest at its coldest end, and those ends are ports the evaluator checks. Hence (6)–(8) are the second law, not an approximation of it. A side that changes phase makes the profile piecewise and needs a zoned composite-curve check; v0.1 refuses it (§19 Q2).

### 11.3 C3 — a thermally coupled recycle (exchanger, PH flash; the NET-07 candidate)

`S1` feed `(1,1,1)`, 300 K → `U-HX` cold side (LIQUID) → `S2` → `U-MIX` (K02) with recycle `S6` → `S3` → `U-PHF` (`Q_spec = 48 000 W`, inlet LIQUID) → `S4` vapour product, `S5` liquid → `U-SPLIT` (K02, `r = 0.6`) → `S6` recycle, `S7` purge → `U-HX` hot side (LIQUID; specification `hot_outlet_temperature = 310 K`) → `S8` → sink. `r = 0.6`, not 0.5, because at `r = 1/2` recycle and purge carry identical flows at one state, and a splitter wired backwards would pass.

**Reduction (derivation).** SYN-001 §5's recycle invariance holds at any flash temperature: at steady state the vapour and the purge are a single TP flash of the fresh feed at `T_f`, and the liquid loop is `L_s/(1 − r)`. The envelope around everything but the exchanger gives `Ḣ_TP(F, T_f) = Ḣ(F, 300 K) + Q_phf + Q_hx`, and the exchanger gives `Q_hx = L_s(T_f) c_p (T_f − 310 K)`. So `T_f` is the root of one scalar equation, increasing on `[350, 370] K` (generator-checked): `T_f = 359.961 523 534 679 78 K`, `Q_hx = 8 209.354 111 660 09 W`, `T_2 = 327.364 513 705 5 K`, `T_3 = 342.066 492 208 4 K`. The twin then runs the PH flash on the mixer outlet and recovers `T_f` to `1e-30 K` — the reduction is checked against the unit, not assumed.

**Why it is a thermally coupled recycle.** The tear map from the mixer outlet `(n, T)` round the loop back to itself has, at the fixed point, `∂T'/∂n = (−3.120, 4.666, 9.737) K per mol/s` and `∂n'/∂T` up to `−4.10e-3 mol/s per K` (`ref.coupled_cases.SYN-001-UL-C3.tear_map_jacobian`, spectral radius `0.527`): composition drives temperature and temperature drives composition. In SYN-001 every recycle temperature is pinned by a specification, so those entries are zero. T06 decides whether this is NET-07; T05 registers it as a candidate and states the honest limit — its fixed point reduces to one scalar equation, which is what makes it independently referenceable.

### 11.4 C3X — an infeasible exchanger with solvable equations (adversarial; outside the denominator)

C3 with `hot_outlet_temperature = 290 K`, below the cold inlet. The rows have a solution — `T_f = 360.799 581 574 126 6 K`, `Q_hx = 11 024.371 385 241 2 W`, every stream liquid with margin — but the exchanger's cold end is `−10 K`: heat would flow from 290 K liquid into 300 K liquid. Expected (A20): **never `VERIFIED`**. Either the causal evaluator is reached and the solve fails typed at `U-HX` with `temperature_cross(cold_end)`, or the EO path converges and the certificate is `FAILED` with exactly one failing check, `bounds_and_domain.U-HX.cold_end` (value `+10 K` against `1e-6 K`); anything else fails the assertion.

### 11.5 Allowances for converged states

Coupled final states are compared with the twin at T02 §6.4's per-kind allowances — flows `3.1e-7 mol/s`, `T` `1e-5 K`, `P` `0.1 Pa`, duties and work `1e-2 W` — ten times each residual tolerance. The argument is T02's: a Newton meeting a residual tolerance `τ` leaves a state error at most `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖ τ̂`, and quadratic convergence overshoots `τ` by decades. Unlike SYN-001, `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖` has not been measured on these systems, so **W0.6** measures K04's recorded solution-error bound `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ ‖F̂‖∞` (scaled, K04 §7.4) on each case and requires it `≤ 1e-8`, one tenth of the smallest scaled allowance (`3.1e-7/3`, `1e-5/100` and `1e-2/1e5` are each `≈ 1e-7`; `0.1/1e5 = 1e-6`); if it is not, the allowance is not widened — the case is reported against this section. The smallest defect a wrong unit makes is decades larger (a dropped exchanger duty moves `T_f` by about 3 K; a swapped splitter by `≥ 0.3 mol/s`).

*Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R6 (W0.6 measured by the build lane; the factors measured in the ruling round, and re-measured by A21).*

- **The bound is limited by where Newton stopped.** It is evaluated at the solve's final iterate, so whenever the last step lands above the roundoff floor it reflects the stopping point, not roundoff.
  - C1 (`8.08e-14`) and C2 (`3.88e-15`) are at the roundoff floor.
  - C3's `3.18e-9` is `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ ≈ 16.63` (K04's `onenormest`) times `‖F̂‖∞ = 1.91e-10`. The worst row, `U-PHF:PHF-duty`, is at `1.9e-2` of its tolerance.
  - At the twin's own C3 state the bound is `2.6e-15`.
- **"Overshoots `τ` by decades" is an expectation, not a guarantee.** At C3 the overshoot is 1.7 decades, and the requirement holds by a factor of 3.1.
- **The recorded bound is a lower estimate.** `onenormest` returns a lower estimate of the norm, as K04 §7.1 says. At C3 it is 6 % low: the exact `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ = 17.76` gives `3.40e-9`, which is inside `1e-8` by a factor of 2.9. At C1 and C2 the estimate equals the exact norm (58.07, 26.21).
  - A21 therefore judges the requirement on the **exact** `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁`, computed from the dense inverse (`n ≤ 64` on C1–C3), times the recorded `‖F̂‖∞`.
  - The certificate keeps recording K04's estimate unchanged.
- **The requirement stands as written.** It is what makes a deviation above the allowance diagnostic of a wrong equation, rather than of where Newton stopped.
  - It is an evidence condition on the registered cases, not a certificate check. K04 §7.4 keeps the bound out of the check set, because for a tolerance-converged state it is `O(‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ τ̂)`.
  - A future solve of these cases whose exact bound exceeds `1e-8` fails A21 and is reported against this section.
  - Neither the allowance nor the requirement is widened. The remedy, a stopping criterion or a policy value, is a design-lane question.

## 12. What the K04 verifier must add

### 12.1 Principle

## 18. What this specification does not establish

- **That any T05 model is right for any real substance.** Everything is SYN-001: invented constants, ideal VLE, equal `c_p` and `v_i`. Agreement with the twin verifies algebra and bookkeeping (A09), not thermodynamics.
- **Component discrimination in liquid enthalpy.** SYN-001's `h_i^L` is the same function for every component, so a permuted component inside a liquid enthalpy, or in a lifted liquid read only through its enthalpy, is invisible at every state (§13.5). Liquid reactions are thermoneutral and liquid energy balances composition-blind for the same reason; the vapour and two-phase cases carry the composition tests. The pump's work relation's `T_in` dependence is untested (it cancels exactly).
- **Heat of reaction in a liquid.** Every registered liquid reaction has `Δh_r = 0` by ADR 0011 D2; a non-thermoneutral liquid reaction needs different data (§19 Q8).
- **Exchanger feasibility with a phase change**, or with temperature-dependent `c_p`: the terminal check is exact only for §10.4's class; v0.1 refuses the rest.
- **EO solves with a dormant PH-type outlet** (§4.7 (a)). They are never certified. From the traversal start the solve is `CONVERGED` with an `UNVERIFIED` certificate (rank limitation, A28); from any other start the outcome is not registered. `branch_found` names the form that ran (`TWO_PHASE`), not a phase found. *(Amended 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R4; the earlier text said "refused, typed; not solved", which W0.8 contradicted.)* *(Superseded 2026-09-25 by T05b: a dormant lifted outlet is `VERIFIED` and `branch_found` is `ZERO_FLOW`; only non-lifted energy-determined outlets remain here, `docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §17.)*
- **EO solves with a single flowing component in the latent jump** (§4.7 (b)). The saturation route is solved causally only; on the EO path the outcome is a typed failure, never `VERIFIED` (A30). *(Added 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R8, review S3.)* *(Superseded 2026-09-25 by T05b: solved and certified under `T05b-phase-contract-v2`, `docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §6, §9.)*
- **Accuracy of the PH kernel beyond `τ_E` for near-pure feeds** (§4.4). An `ok` answer there is accurate to the energy tolerance's resolution. Whether a target inside the jump is answered or refused (`ph_ill_conditioned`) depends on where it lies. *(Added 2026-09-25, ruling round Q-R1.)* *(Superseded 2026-09-25 by T05b: every target is answered; what remains unestablished is EO certification in T04 F9's band, `docs/derivations/T05b-limitations-spec.md` §17.)*
- **Solution accuracy of the coupled cases** beyond what K04's recorded bound shows (W0.6), and **certificate robustness to F9** on the EO path (§12.6).
- **External validation.** T06's comparisons with DWSIM and IDAES are separate; the representability notes cited in §6.1 and §8 show the tools can express these models, not that they agree.
- **That C3 is NET-07.** T06's decision; its fixed point reduces to one scalar equation.
- **Human numerical or process-modelling review.** Not claimable by an agent.

## 20. Findings about the brief, the plan, the ADRs and earlier work

1. **F1 — The brief's premise holds, and generalizes.** "Any A → B reaction has zero liquid-phase heat of reaction" is true of *every* mass-conserving reaction on SYN-001 (equal `M_i` force `Σ ν = 0`), and only once the reference is declared a formation datum; the vapour reaction enthalpy is `Σ ν_i L_i ≠ 0`. ADR 0011 D2 makes the declaration.
2. **F2 — The pump's "work follows from `h`" needs one more fact**: that the liquid entropy is `P`-independent, which SYN-001's is (§9.3). Stated in the pump's manifest.
3. **F3 — `manifest_document` hard-codes `introduced_by_package` and `planned_package` as `K02`.** T05 manifests need `T05`; parameterizing it is inert for K02's documents.
4. **F4 — ADR 0008's M1 and M3 count every manifest in `tests/fixtures/schemas/model_manifest/valid/`.** T05 manifests must stay out of that directory (or the tests must name the six P01 files); ADR 0008 itself does not change.
5. **F5 — The orchestrator (`syn001_lifted_splits`, `tear.py`, `executor.py`) and the verifier (`checks.py`) are SYN-001-specific.** T05's coupled cases need a revision-driven flowsheet, per-unit lifted-split declarations and a per-model check table (§12). This is the largest build-lane item and a design choice of its own (W1).
6. **F6 — `UnitEvaluation` has no place for shaft work, a reaction extent or an internal transfer duty.** An additive field is needed; K02's behaviour must not change.
7. **F7 — K02's `TPFlash.evaluate` does not apply R-007 to a declared inlet phase** while its rows write that phase's enthalpy, so for an inadmissible declared inlet the evaluator's duty and the rows' would describe different functions. Harmless in SYN-001 (the flash's inlet is lifted); T05's units check it (§5.2). Handed to the build lane as a K02 finding, not fixed here.
8. **F8 — Every T05 temperature fixed by an energy balance is singular at zero flow** (§4.7); no K02 unit had one. The limitation is new with T05 and is registered rather than left to be discovered.
9. **F9 — T04 F9 reaches T05** (§12.6).
10. **F10 — K04's recorded solution-error bound is a lower estimate, and at C3 it is not tight** (ruling round Q-R6, *measured*). `onenormest` gives `‖Ĵ⁻¹‖₁ = 16.63` against an exact `17.76`. K04 §7.1 says the estimate is a lower one, and measured the ratio as 1.000 on SYN-001's states. T05's A21 judges its requirement on the exact norm (§11.5); the certificate's recorded value is K04's and is not changed here. A K04 amendment, if one is wanted, would say that the recorded `b` can under-report. That is for the design lane to raise against K04, not for T05 to change.
11. **F11 — The K04 legacy check set had a check that §12.2 omitted** (ruling round Q-R3): `energy_balance.splitter`. W1.d's cross-validation compared only the pairs it listed, so the gap was found by reading, not by a test. The cross-validation now also asserts coverage: every legacy check id is either generic (the same id in both) or paired.
