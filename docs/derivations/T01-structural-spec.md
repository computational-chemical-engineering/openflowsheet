## 1. Scope, authority, and what is out of scope

**What T01 delivers.** A structural analysis of a compiled problem's *declaration* — its process multigraph and its equation–variable incidence graph — that reports the counts blueprint §7.2 names, decides whether the declared system is structurally closed after fixed-variable and certified alias elimination, names the objects of a structural excess or deficit, computes the block-triangular form with its block metrics, selects the tear candidates of every recycle loop by a documented rule, derives the general attempt-signature rule K03 §9.1 left to it, and does all of this deterministically, without evaluating anything, so that its output is bit-identical on the registered platform pair.

**The six questions of the brief, and where each is answered.** (1) The discriminator between consistent redundancy and specification conflict: §8. (2) Canonical tie-breaks and why they are total and order-independent: §6.2, §6.4, §7.2, §9.4. (3) What a structural finding may and may not claim: §11. (4) The `structural_counts` field set and the `StructuralReport`: §12. (5) The tear-candidate rule and why it yields three and not five: §9. (6) The registered assertions: §14.

**The four recommendations of brief §4, decided by name.**

**Design invariants.**

1. **The analysis evaluates nothing.** It reads a `ProblemSpec` — ids, row builders, block sparsity declarations, parameters — and never calls `residual`, `jacobian` or a property provider. There is no state at which it runs, so there is no bad state that can poison it, and every output is a function of the declaration and the parameters alone (A03, A17).
2. **Expectations are a second derivation.** Every registered value is produced by `t01_reference.py` from a hand transcription of the equations and algorithms written from their definitions, and the implementation is compared against it. Where an object has no closed form of its own — the canonical matching, the block order — it is registered as the unique minimum of a stated finite set, and `--check` verifies the minimality.
3. **Order-invariant and order-canonical are distinguished.** The sets (DM parts, structural rank, excess and deficit, cycle count, block-size multiset, tear dimension, chosen tear stream) are invariant under any permutation of rows and columns and `--check` proves it on twenty seeded permutations. The sequences (the matching, which row of a cycle is certified, the block order) are canonical *given* the declaration order, and a permutation of the declaration changes `model_version` (ADR 0002 D2.3), so nothing compares them across differently-ordered declarations (§6.4).
4. **Unsupported is a result.** A row the structural algebra cannot trace, a loop the tear search cannot break within its budget, and a backend cross-check that cannot run are each a typed result with the object named; no path returns silence as a pass (§12.4, A20).
5. **Every claim this document makes about its own numbers is executable** in `t01_reference.py --check` (§15.2).

**Out of scope** (brief §10, restated): T02's execution strategy (acyclic order, Anderson, EO blocks, cross-unit promotion); T03's phase-attempt contract and ADR 0005 (this document *derives* the signature rule's structural half and hands it over, §9.6); T06's rank diagnostics beyond D05's separation; generalizing K03's eliminator to ratios or scaled drops (this document says what the graph shows about such rows, §8.2, and stops); a new flowsheet or benchmark; a third platform; production code. Assembling a compiled problem from `ModelManifest` documents rather than from Python is *not* delivered here and the obligation ADR 0002 D2.7 attaches to whichever package first does it is recorded as a finding (§19 finding 3).

## 2. The verdict, in one paragraph

A structural analysis of SYN-001 finds 49 declared rows over 47 free variables with 170 declared incidences and structural rank 47; the over-determined Dulmage–Mendelsohn part is seven pressure rows over five pressure columns and the under-determined part is empty. Two of the seven rows close cycles in the ±1 pressure-copy graph and their consistency mismatches, computed from the specifications alone, are exactly `0.0 Pa`: they are certified consistent-redundant, and with them removed the remaining 47 × 47 system is perfectly matched — **structurally closed**. The conflicting revision adds one row and is over-determined by one *after* the same two certificates; the excess could be discharged by relaxing any one of nine specifications (a structural fact the report states without choosing), and the unit-local count singles out the heater as the only unit whose specifications exceed its degrees of freedom (2 > 1), which is the registered `STRUCTURAL_OVER_SPECIFICATION` finding naming `SPEC-heater-outlet-T`, `SPEC-heater-duty` and `heater`. The closed 47 × 47 system has 24 blocks — one 17-block recycle SCC, the heater's 8-block lifted split downstream of it, 22 singletons; the process graph has one loop with four cycle streams of equal tear dimension 3, and the boundary-distance rule selects S6, whose T and P lie outside the recycle SCC — three variables, not five, rediscovered. The inner 44 × 44 system is closed, its flash block is upstream of the tear rows and the heater's lifted block is not, which is K03 §9.1's signature `(U-FLASH)` derived rather than declared. None of this is a statement about numerical rank or feasibility; §11 fixes the sentences that say so.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Process graph

The **process multigraph** has one node per model instance of the revision and one directed edge per material connection, from producer to consumer, in the revision's connection order (blueprint §7.2's "material/energy/signal multigraph"; SYN-001 has only material edges). For SYN-001: `S1: U-FEED → U-MIX`, `S2: U-MIX → U-HEAT`, `S3: U-HEAT → U-FLASH`, `S4: U-FLASH → U-PRODUCT`, `S5: U-FLASH → U-SPLIT`, `S6: U-SPLIT → U-MIX`, `S7: U-SPLIT → U-PURGE`. A **boundary unit** is one with no material inlet (`U-FEED`). A **process loop** is a strongly connected component of the process graph with more than one node; SYN-001 has exactly one, `{U-MIX, U-HEAT, U-FLASH, U-SPLIT}`, with cycle edges `S2, S3, S5, S6`.

### 3.2 Equation graph

The **free variables** are `CompiledProblemMetadata.variable_ids`, in order: for SYN-001 the `nTP-v1` state of each of the seven streams (ADR 0001 D2.1: `S#.n.{A,B,C}`, `S#.T`, `S#.P`, 35 columns) and twelve unit-owned variables (`U-HEAT.Q`, the heater's lifted split `S3.vap.*`, `S3.liq.*`, `S3.V`, `S3.L`, `U-FLASH.Q`, `S4.N`, `S5.N`). Specification *values* are not variables: ADR 0008 D1.3 makes them pinned parameters, so **fixed-variable elimination in this architecture is the assembly's parameterization** and has already happened when a `ProblemSpec` exists. What the blueprint calls a fixed variable is here a free column pinned by a single-column **specification row** (`S1.n.A − n_spec`), which the analysis sees as a 1 × 1 block. The **rows** are `equation_ids` in assembly order (49 for SYN-001: feed 5, mixer 6, heater 14, flash 14, splitter 10).

The **incidence** of a row is the set of columns it references. A column is **owned** by the unit that produces its stream, or by the unit its id names (`U-HEAT.Q`; the lifted `S3.*` belong to the heater through S3). A **specification row** is a single-column affine row whose constant is a revision specification's value; it records the specification's id.

### 3.3 The structural source: declaration-traced incidence, not the backend pattern

The incidence is obtained by running every row builder under a **structural algebra** whose values are incidence sets: a variable symbol is `{its id}`; a block output `"<block>.<out>"` is the set of the block's input variables that its declared `jacobian_pattern` names for that output (the same declaration the backend consumes); `+ − × ÷` and every unary function are set union; a parameter is an opaque token that neither folds nor is a variable. The same trace records, for each row, whether it is **affine with literal coefficients** — a sum of `±1 · variable` and parameters — and if so the coefficients and the signed parameters of its constant. A builder that branches in Python on a parameter *value* defeats this trace; no SYN-001 builder does, and A02 would catch one.

Why not the backend's `indptr`/`indices` at an admissible state, as brief §4.1 proposed: the compiler substitutes parameter values before building the expression (`equation.build(symbols, outputs, spec.parameters, algebra)`), so the CasADi sparsity is that of the expression *after constant folding at the pinned values*. *Measured* on `main` at `f603e9b`: nnz 170 at r ∈ {0.5, 0.95} and at 310 K and 420 K, but **167 at r = 0**, the three `U-SPLIT:SPLIT-recycle:i × S5.n.i` entries having folded away with `r = 0.0`. The registered once-through case would then have no recycle edge in its graph, an empty tear, and a "structural" result that the same flowsheet does not share at r = 0.5. The brief's §3.4 argument against numerical nonzeros — a conclusion that moves with the enthalpy datum is not structural — applies verbatim to a conclusion that moves with a parameter value. The declaration does not move. `pattern_provenance = "backend-declared"` remains exactly what K01 says it is (the compiled function's sparsity) and is used here only as a *cross-check* (A02): the backend pattern must be a subset of the traced incidence at every registered variant, equal except for the entries constant folding removes, and a `jacobian` result with `status != "ok"` makes the cross-check `unsupported`, never a pass. The analysis never reads `data`.

### 3.4 What fixed and alias elimination remove, and on what evidence

Fixed elimination: nothing beyond the parameterization of §3.2. Alias elimination: the **certified** rows of §8.2 — rows shown from the declaration to be identically a signed sum of retained rows, with a constant mismatch inside the registered tolerance of their kind. The evidence is the affine trace (literal ±1 coefficients over columns of one kind, two columns at most) and the parameters; no state, no Jacobian, no singular value. The counts of §12.1 are taken on the full declaration; the closure verdict of §8.1 is taken after the certified rows are removed; blueprint §7.2's "fixed/free decisions and alias elimination occur before the relevant counts" is honoured by reporting both and saying which is which.

## 5. Specification-promotion rows and the conflicting revision

### 5.1 The rule

A revision specification with `role: fixed` whose target is a free column that no unit row already pins becomes an **assembler-authored specification row** `SPEC:<specification id>` over that column, `accumulation: "algebraic"` (`EquationSpec`'s own rule), origin `revision#<specification id>`, attributed to the unit that owns the column. The unit is not told; `TPHeater.__post_init__`'s refusal of `specified_duty` is not the path by which the conflicting case is rejected and must not be reached by the binding.

### 5.2 The conflicting revision

`SYN-001-conflicting-heater-spec` is the nominal declaration plus `SPEC:SPEC-heater-duty` over `U-HEAT.Q` with constant `−50 000 W`: 50 rows, nnz 171. `ref.declaration.conflicting_row` registers it. This is the only registered revision that needs §5.1 in v0.1; the binding for it is Opus's, and the rule above is what it must produce.

## 6. Matching and Dulmage–Mendelsohn

### 6.1 Maximum matching

A matching pairs rows with columns along incidences, each object at most once. The **structural rank** is the size of a maximum matching (47 for SYN-001, 47 for the conflicting revision). Any correct maximum matching algorithm may be used to *compute* the sets of §6.3; only the canonical matching of §6.2 may be *recorded*.

### 6.2 The canonical matching, defined as an object

Among all maximum matchings, the **canonical matching** is the lexicographically least one under this order: columns in declaration order; each column's value is the declaration index of its matched row, or +∞ if unmatched; matchings are compared as the resulting sequence. Equivalently, constructively: for each column in order, assign it the least row (declaration order) not yet taken such that a maximum matching extending the assignments so far still exists; leave it unmatched if none does. `--check` computes it exactly this way (a matching oracle per trial), so the registered value is the minimum by construction and not the accident of an algorithm.

**Totality.** The set of maximum matchings is finite and non-empty and the order is a total order on sequences, so the minimum exists and is unique. No tie is left. **Registered** (`ref.cases.SYN-001-nominal.canonical_matching`): 47 pairs; the unmatched rows are `U-FLASH:FLASH-P:inlet` and `U-SPLIT:SPLIT-P:recycle`. That this coincides with K03's eliminated set is an observation about SYN-001's declaration order, asserted (A11) so that a change is seen, and not a rule: the certificate of §8.2 is a cycle condition and the matching is a Hall condition, and they can disagree elsewhere. `TIE-1` (§13) registers a two-row case where a greedy row-first assignment gives a different answer, so an implementation cannot pass A11 with a greedy matcher that happens to agree on SYN-001.

### 6.3 The coarse Dulmage–Mendelsohn partition

From any maximum matching: the **over-determined part** `(R⁺, C⁺)` is the unmatched rows together with everything reachable from them by alternating paths (row → any of its columns → that column's matched row); the **under-determined part** `(R⁻, C⁻)` is the unmatched columns together with everything reachable (column → any of its rows → that row's matched column); the **square part** is the rest. `R⁺` is exactly the set of rows that are unmatched in *some* maximum matching, which is what makes it the honest extent of an excess: any row in it could be the one left out. The partition is matching-independent (a theorem, and `--check` verifies it on twenty seeded permutations with their own matchings).

**Registered:** nominal `R⁺` = `{U-FEED:FEED-P, U-MIX:MIX-pressure:0, U-MIX:MIX-pressure:1, U-HEAT:HEAT-pressure, U-FLASH:FLASH-P:liquid, U-FLASH:FLASH-P:inlet, U-SPLIT:SPLIT-P:recycle}`, `C⁺` = `{S1.P, S2.P, S3.P, S5.P, S6.P}`, `R⁻ = C⁻ = ∅` — the brief's measurement, reproduced from the transcription. Conflicting: `|R⁺| = 44`, `|C⁺| = 41`, the square part being `{FLASH-duty, SPLIT-purge:*, SPLIT-T:purge, SPLIT-P:purge}` × `{S7.*, U-FLASH.Q}` — again the brief's numbers.

### 6.4 Order-independence, stated so it can be falsified

| Object | Class | Under a permutation of rows/columns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| structural rank; the four counts; `R⁺, C⁺, R⁻, C⁻` as sets; excess and deficit; number of certified rows and the column set their cycles span; the closure finding; block-size multiset; largest-block fraction; per-stream tear dimension; the chosen tear stream when §9.4's chain ends before declaration order; the signature unit set | **invariant** | unchanged (`--check`: 20 seeded permutations) |

## 7. Strongly connected components and the block-triangular form

### 7.1 Definition

On a square, perfectly matched incidence, row `r` **depends on** row `r′` when the column matched to `r′` appears in `r`. The SCCs of this digraph are the diagonal blocks; the condensation is acyclic. The blocks, their sizes and the dependency DAG are matching-independent (the fine DM decomposition is unique); a block's *order position* is not, and §7.2 fixes it.

### 7.2 Canonical block order

A topological order of the condensation in which, whenever several blocks have all their predecessors placed, the one whose first row has the least declaration index is placed next. Total: at every step the candidate set is finite and non-empty (the condensation is acyclic) and the key is a distinct integer per block. `BTF-1` (§13) registers a four-block case where a plain FIFO queue would place `d` before `c`; the rule places `a, b, c, d`.

### 7.3 Registered block metrics (blueprint §7.2 "block-size distribution, largest-block fraction")

On the nominal 47 × 47 after certificates: **24 blocks**; sizes sorted `[17, 8, 1 × 22]`; **largest-block fraction `17/47 = 0.3617021276595745`**. The 17-block is the recycle: rows `MIX-mole:*`, `HEAT-mole:*`, `FLASH-mole:*`, `FLASH-equilibrium:*`, `Ndef:*`, `SPLIT-recycle:*` over columns `S2.n.*, S3.n.*, S4.n.*, S5.n.*, S6.n.*, S4.N, S5.N`. The 8-block is the heater's lifted split (`HEAT-equilibrium:*`, `split:*`, `Vdef`, `Ldef` over `S3.vap.*, S3.liq.*, S3.V, S3.L`) and it **depends on** the recycle block, not the other way round: S3's split is a consequence of the loop, never a cause. `MIX-energy`, `HEAT-duty` and `FLASH-duty` are 1 × 1 blocks downstream of everything they read (they determine `S2.T`, `U-HEAT.Q`, `U-FLASH.Q`). The full ordered list with each block's `depends_on` is `ref.block_triangular_form.blocks`.

## 8. The discriminator: certified redundancy versus specification conflict

### 8.1 The rule

Let `E` be the certified rows (§8.2). The declaration is **structurally closed** iff the coarse DM partition of the incidence with the rows of `E` removed has `R⁺ = ∅` and `C⁻ = ∅`. Otherwise:

- any certificate whose mismatch exceeds its kind's tolerance → **`SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`**, naming the row, its signed path and the mismatch (the same outcome K03 issues at solve time, now at validation);
- `C⁻ ≠ ∅` → **`STRUCTURAL_UNDER_SPECIFICATION`**, deficit `|C⁻| − |R⁻|`, naming `C⁻` (every column that could be the free one);
- `R⁺ ≠ ∅` → **`STRUCTURAL_OVER_SPECIFICATION`**, excess `|R⁺| − |C⁺|`, naming `R⁺` and, within it, the **candidate specification rows** (relaxing any one of them lowers the excess by one — a structural fact stated without choosing among them) and the **localization** of §8.3.

Precedence: conflict, then under, then over; a declaration can be both under- and over-determined (`SQ-1`) and the report says both.

### 8.2 The affine-copy certificate, from the declaration, at validation

K03 §7.2's algorithm on the trace instead of on a Jacobian: rows of a certifiable kind (`pressure`, tolerance `1e-2 Pa`; `temperature`, `1e-6 K`; ADR 0001 D6) that are affine with literal coefficients, all `±1`, over at most two columns of that kind and — when there are two — with the coefficients **cancelling**, form a graph on the columns plus one node `CONST`; visited in declaration order, a row whose two nodes are already connected closes a cycle and is **certified** with the identity `F_e − Σ σ_k F_k = m_e` along the tree path, where — because every row on the path has exactly two `±1` columns — the variable parts telescope identically and `m_e` is the signed sum of the rows' constants, a function of parameters alone. `|m_e| ≤ τ_kind` → consistent, the row joins `E`; otherwise `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`. A row of a certifiable kind that is affine but has a coefficient other than `±1`, more than two columns, or two columns whose coefficients do not cancel (a *sum* rather than a copy: `P_a + P_b − P_spec`) is **`uncertified_affine`**: retained, listed, and never silently certified or dropped (K03 raises `UNSUPPORTED_RANK_STRUCTURE` there; T01 reports the row and lets §8.1 decide from the graph).

**Registered (nominal):** `E = [U-FLASH:FLASH-P:inlet, U-SPLIT:SPLIT-P:recycle]`, in that order; `FLASH-P:inlet = +FEED-P − MIX-pressure:0 + HEAT-pressure`, `SPLIT-P:recycle = +FEED-P − MIX-pressure:0 + MIX-pressure:1 − FLASH-P:liquid` as signed-row *sets* (the traversal order of a path is not part of the identity — §19 finding 5); `m_e = P_spec − P_f = 0.0` exactly for both. **Flash at 150 kPa:** both `m_e = −50 000.0 Pa` exactly, `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`. Consistency with R-011: this is the same certificate issued earlier, from the same identity, on evidence that is structural (the literal coefficients) plus the parameters; K03's two-state check at solve time remains and A06 requires the two to agree.

### 8.3 Localization: unit-local degrees of freedom

For each unit `u`, with every inlet treated as known and the certified rows removed: `C_u` = the columns `u` owns; the **model rows** = `u`'s non-specification rows touching `C_u`; `dof_u = |C_u| − structural rank of the model rows over C_u`; `specs_u` = the specification rows (any author) targeting a column of `C_u`; `u` is **over-specified** iff `specs_u > dof_u`. The local over-determined rows of the model rows over `C_u` are reported as **local inlet constraints** (a mixer's second pressure equality, ADR 0001 D4.5).

| Unit | `|C_u|` | model rank | `dof_u` | `specs_u` nominal | `specs_u` conflicting | local excess rows |

Bookkeeping identity, executable: `Σ specs_u + Σ (local excess) − Σ dof_u = excess − deficit` after certificates: `10 + 1 − 11 = 0` nominal, `11 + 1 − 11 = 1` conflicting. The localization is a declaration count with no numerics and no search; it names `{SPEC-heater-outlet-T, SPEC-heater-duty, U-HEAT}` in the conflicting case and nothing in the nominal one, which is what the registry expects and what ADR 0001 D4.4 says in words. When the global excess is positive and no unit is over-specified (a cross-unit design specification of blueprint [A02] seen from the other side), the report carries the candidate specification rows and `localization: none`.

### 8.4 Why confinement, not a threshold

`|R⁺| − |C⁺|` is exactly the number of rows that must be discharged for a square system, and blueprint §7.7 allows a row to be discharged only with a certificate. A threshold on `|R⁺|` accepts `OVR-1` — a duplicated single-column specification, a 2 × 1 block, a genuine over-specification — and rejects a large but fully certified pressure network. The rule "excess after certificates is zero" does neither, and `--check` verifies on the nominal case that `|R⁺| − |C⁺| = |E ∩ R⁺|` (2 = 2).

### 8.5 What the conflicting case's report contains

`finding: STRUCTURAL_OVER_SPECIFICATION`; `excess: 1`; `R⁺` after certificates: 41 rows over 40 columns (`ref.cases.SYN-001-conflicting-heater-spec.dm_after_certificates`); `candidate_specification_rows`: 10 rows over 9 specification ids (`SPEC-feed-n-{A,B,C}`, `SPEC-feed-T`, `SPEC-feed-P`, `SPEC-heater-outlet-T`, `SPEC-flash-T`, `SPEC-flash-P`, `SPEC-heater-duty`); `over_specified_units: [U-HEAT]`; `implicated_objects: [SPEC-heater-duty, SPEC-heater-outlet-T, U-HEAT]` (revision ids: `heater`). Not in the report anywhere: `UNSUPPORTED_RANK_STRUCTURE`, a compile, a solve.

## 9. The tear-candidate rule

### 9.1 Where "three, not five" comes from

The recycle stream S6 has five `nTP` variables. In the 47 × 47 BTF, `S6.n.A/B/C` lie in the 17-block; `S6.T` and `S6.P` are singleton blocks (`SPLIT-T:recycle` copies `S5.T`, which `FLASH-T:liquid` pins; `S6.P` is fixed by `MIX-pressure:1` through the certified chain) that nothing in the loop feeds back into. The loop's SCC membership is matching-independent, so "the recycle's T and P are known from the flash specification" is a *structural* fact about the declaration, read from the equation graph without knowing what a flash is. That is the plan §3.2 obligation discharged.

### 9.2 Candidates

For each process loop (§3.1), every cycle edge whose removal breaks the loop (single-edge feedback sets; SYN-001's loop is a simple cycle so all four qualify). The **dimension** of a candidate stream is the number of its `nTP` variables that lie in a non-singleton block of the square BTF; lifted variables (`S3.vap.*` and the like) are never tear variables (ADR 0001 D2.5: reconstructable results). **Registered:** S2, S3, S5, S6 all have dimension 3 and all leave their `T` and `P` untorn.

### 9.3 The score and the tie chain

Least dimension first (blueprint §7.2 "tear dimension"); then least **consumer boundary distance** — the BFS distance along material connections from a boundary unit to the edge's consumer (the unit that the traversal can evaluate first with the torn stream guessed and everything else fixed: `U-MIX` is one edge from `U-FEED`); then the connection's declaration order, with `tie_break_used: declaration_order` recorded. **Registered:** distances `S2 → 2, S3 → 3, S5 → 4, S6 → 1`; `tie_break_used: boundary_distance`; chosen `S6`. `LOOP-3` (§13) registers a loop where the rule chooses the edge that is neither first nor last in declaration order, so "first" and "last" rules are distinguishable from this one.

### 9.4 Totality

Each step is a minimum over a finite non-empty set with an integer key, and the last key (declaration order) is injective; the chain always terminates in one candidate. Multi-edge feedback sets (nested loops) are outside the v0.1 rule: a loop no single edge breaks is reported `tear: unsupported(multi_edge_feedback_set)` with the loop named (A20); T02 decides what to do with it.

### 9.5 Tear rows, the inner system

The **tear rows** of a chosen stream are the rows of its producing unit in which the tear variables appear: `SPLIT-recycle:A/B/C`. The **inner system** is the closed square system with the tear rows and tear columns removed: 44 × 44, perfectly matched, 30 blocks with sizes `[8, 8, 1 × 28]` — the flash's equilibrium block and the heater's lifted split. This is K03 §3.1's partition (`SolvePlan.tear_variable_ids`, `tear_row_ids`, the 44 × 44 inner block) reproduced from the graph; A14 requires equality by id.

### 9.6 The signature rule, derived (K03 §9.1's general rule)

A phase-selecting unit (one that lifts a split: `U-HEAT`, `U-FLASH`) is in the attempt signature iff its lifted block is an ancestor of, or contains, a block the tear rows read, in the inner BTF's dependency DAG. **Registered:** the flash block (rows `FLASH-mole:*`, `FLASH-equilibrium:*`, `Ndef:*`) is upstream of `SPLIT-recycle:*`; the heater's lifted block is not (its only consumers are `HEAT-duty` and `FLASH-duty`, which determine duties nothing reads). `signature_units = (U-FLASH)`, equal to K03's declared instance. K03 §9.1's measured consequence of freezing the heater regime (a spurious phase wall at nominal) is the reason the rule is stated as *ancestry* and not as *membership in the loop's unit set*.

## 10. Zero-flow cases and state independence

The analysis takes no state, so the five registered variants — including the two with dormant streams (`S4` at 310 K; `S5`, `S6`, `S7` at 420 K; ADR 0001 D3) and the once-through case with a dormant recycle — have **identical** structural results: `--check` compares the complete result dictionaries. The registered fact the brief asked for: *the declared pattern does not change at zero flow*, and neither does anything derived from it. What does change, *measured* at each variant's registered initializer state, is the number of declared entries whose Jacobian value happens to be zero: 4 (nominal), 12 (r = 0), 16 (r = 0.95), 17 (310 K), 26 (420 K). The four at nominal are registered by cause: `MIX-energy × S1.n.{A,B,C}` are `h_i^L(300 K, P_r) = 0` exactly by the `SYN-001-ref-v1` reference convention (ADR 0001 D5.1) and would reappear under another datum; `FLASH-duty × S4.P` is the pressure-independence of the ideal-vapor enthalpy. A matching on numerical nonzeros would see 166 entries at nominal and fewer at zero flow, which is the brief's §3.4 in numbers; the assertion that protects against it is not "the counts differ" but A03: the analysis makes no evaluation call at all.

## 11. What a structural finding may claim — the sentences

Every `StructuralReport`, and every `ValidationReport` check T01 produces, carries these verbatim (`ref` does not carry them; they are frozen here, as K04 §8.2's are):

- **S1 (scope).** "This finding is structural: it is a statement about the declared equations and variables and about which of them reference which. It was made without evaluating any equation."
- **S2 (closure).** "Structurally closed means that after the certified redundant rows are removed, every remaining equation can be assigned a distinct variable it references. It does not establish that a solution exists, that it is unique, or that any Jacobian is nonsingular."
- **S3 (numerical rank is elsewhere).** "Numerical rank and conditioning are decided by the certificate's regularity screen on the unregularized target Jacobian at a converged state (blueprint [A08]); this report makes no such claim, in either direction."
- **S4 (excess).** "Over-specified by k means that k more equations are declared than the variables can absorb after certified redundancy. Every listed candidate is an equation that could be the one in excess; relaxing any one of them lowers k by one. The list is complete and is not a minimal conflict set."
- **S5 (localization).** "A unit is named as over-specified because the specifications targeting its outlet and internal variables outnumber the degrees of freedom its own equations leave, with its inlets treated as known. This is a count of declarations, not a proof that the specifications contradict each other numerically."
- **S6 (certificates).** "A certified redundant equation is one shown from the declaration to equal a signed sum of retained equations up to a constant computed from the specifications; it is retained in the residual, evaluated by the solver and the verifier, and never discarded."

`INVALID` on a `ValidationReport` produced from a T01 finding means, and only means: *as declared, this is not a closed simulation task* (blueprint §4.3: `READY_FOR_SIMULATION` requires an appropriately closed system). It is not a claim of infeasibility. The evidence class of every T01 check is `structural`; T01 never emits `local_numerical` or `verified_minimal_subset` (A23).

## 12. Field sets

### 12.1 `ValidationReport.structural_counts` — the frozen four, defined

The schema (P01, frozen) allows exactly `free_variables`, `equations`, `matched`, `unmatched`, all integers. Definitions, taken on the **full declaration** (before certificates, after the parameterization of §3.2):

| Field | Definition | Nominal | Conflicting | `SQ-1` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `free_variables` | `|variable_ids|` | 47 | 47 | 3 |
| `equations` | `|equation_ids|` (including specification-promotion rows) | 49 | 50 | 3 |
| `matched` | structural rank | 47 | 47 | 2 |
| `unmatched` | `(equations − matched) + (free_variables − matched)`: unmatched rows plus unmatched columns | 2 | 3 | 2 |

What the four are **not** evidence of: `unmatched = 0` does not mean closed (a certified excess is still unmatched: nominal has 2), `unmatched > 0` does not mean invalid (same), `equations == free_variables` does not mean square in any useful sense (`SQ-1`), and none of them says anything about numerical rank. The verdict is the finding of §8.1, carried by the checks; the counts describe. `structural_counts_absent_reason` is used only when the analysis itself is `unsupported` (§12.4), and then names the row.

### 12.2 `StructuralReport` — a new type (plan §2.1: T01 may add types)

Returned by the graph layer, attached to the run bundle as an artifact, and the source of every `structural_analysis` check. Fields: `model_version`, `constants_sha256` (the declaration and parameters it describes); `structural_counts` (§12.1); `nnz`; `finding` (`STRUCTURALLY_CLOSED | STRUCTURAL_UNDER_SPECIFICATION | STRUCTURAL_OVER_SPECIFICATION | SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT | UNSUPPORTED`); `excess`, `deficit`; `dm_full` and `dm_after_certificates` (each: `over_rows`, `over_cols`, `under_rows`, `under_cols`, `square_rows`, `square_cols`, declaration order); `certificates` (`row_id`, `equals` as signed pairs, `constant_mismatch`, `tolerance`, `consistent`); `uncertified_affine_rows`; `candidate_specification_rows`, `candidate_specifications`; `unit_degrees_of_freedom` (§8.3's table per unit); `over_specified_units`; `implicated_objects` (revision ids); `canonical_matching` (column → row); `block_triangular_form` (blocks with `rows`, `cols`, `size`, `depends_on`; `block_sizes_sorted`; `largest_block_fraction`); `tear` (per loop: `units`, `cycle_edges`, `candidates` with `dimension`, `consumer_boundary_distance`, `torn_variables`, `not_torn`; `chosen_stream`; `tie_break_used`; `tear_variables`; `tear_rows`; `inner_block_sizes`; `signature_units`); `unsupported` (a list of typed entries, §12.4); `statements` (S1–S6); `provenance` (`analysis: "T01 declaration-traced incidence v1"`, `tie_break_rules: ["lexicographic-least-matching", "least-row-index-block-order", "dimension/boundary-distance/declaration-order"]`). Its **R0 projection** (ADR 0007) is the whole document minus the `constant_mismatch` floats, which are replaced by their `consistent` booleans; everything else is integers, ids and booleans.

## 19. Findings about the brief, the plan, the ADRs, K03 and K06

1. **The backend pattern is parameter-dependent (brief §3.1, §4.1).** *Measured*: at r = 0 the CasADi sparsity has 167 entries, not 170; the three `SPLIT-recycle:i × S5.n.i` entries are constant-folded away. `pattern_provenance = "backend-declared"` is accurate about the *compiled function* and unfit as a structural source; §3.3 makes the declaration the source and the backend a cross-check. K01's `pattern_provenance` needs no change. Anything else that reads structure from `indptr`/`indices` — K04's "compatible structural pattern" for sparsity reuse (ADR 0002 D2.7's last sentence) — should know that the pattern can shrink at a zero parameter.
2. **The K06 record (brief §3.3).** `application/validation.py` and the K06 manifest say the solver rejects the conflicting revision with `SPECIFICATION_CONFLICT`; the Opus session measured `UNSUPPORTED_RANK_STRUCTURE` (a 45 × 44 inner block). This document's A09 registers the correct downstream fact indirectly (no such string in the report, no solve reached); the record correction is the Opus session's, as it said.
3. **ADR 0002 D2.7's obligation.** The v2 structure document with a `sources` member is owed by "the package that first assembles a compiled problem from `ModelManifest` and `ProcessRevision` documents". T01 as specified here assembles only specification-promotion rows for a registered revision (§5); the unit equations still come from Python. The obligation therefore does not trigger here and must not be forgotten: it belongs to whichever package (K06's "compiler binding for a general revision", or T02) first assembles unit rows from manifests.
4. **K05 review S4, in `rank.py`.** `eliminate_alias_rows` qualifies a row from `value != 0.0` on a Jacobian at the initializer state; for affine `±1` rows this is harmless today, and Q2 records the route by which it stops being a numerical qualification at all.
5. **K03 A30's `equals` "exactly".** The path a union-find traversal reports is an ordered list, but the identity is a sum; the generator's traversal produces the same signed rows in a different order from `k03_reference.py`'s data block. A06 compares signed-row *sets*, and K03 A30 should be read the same way (an amendment of one word, "as a set", when K03's document is next touched).
6. **The certified set is order-canonical, not order-invariant (§6.4).** `--check` proves that another row order certifies another row of the same pressure cycle. K03 §7.2's "in assembled order" already implies this; it is stated here so that no later session treats `E` as a property of the flowsheet independent of `equation_ids`.
7. **Plan §4.3's acceptance word "zero-flow cases".** The registered answer is that structure does not depend on flow (§10); the acceptance evidence is A17, not a differing result.
8. **The brief's §3.6 table lists a sixth parked item** — "the CLI solves registered cases only" — that is not structural and is not closed here (§16).
9. **ADR 0001 D6 has no `heat_rate` certificate class.** A duty-copy row would be an affine copy of kind `heat_rate` and there is no registered tolerance for it, so §8.2 certifies only pressure and temperature copies. Not a defect today (SYN-001 has no such row); it becomes one the day a unit declares a duty alias, and the fix is a D6 line, not a T01 rule.
