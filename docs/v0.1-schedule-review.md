## 3. Findings

1. **The allowance is not converted into a date.** The 10–14 weeks were written for "the
   blueprint's capacity"; two packages in three days does not license extrapolating to six in
   nine, because T04, T06 and T07 carry risks of a different kind (a method that may not qualify,
   external software, an agent evaluation) and T08 carries decisions only Frank can make.
2. **Reference acquisition is overdue against the plan's own instruction** and is the item most
   likely to lengthen the critical path if left until T06 starts. It is build-lane work
   (`reference-environment acquisition`), needs no new architecture, and should start now in
   parallel with T03.
3. **T05 can also start now**, but it needs design-approved equations per model; starting it
   beside T03 doubles the design-lane load at once. Sequencing T03's specification first and
   T05's second keeps one design pass in flight at a time.
