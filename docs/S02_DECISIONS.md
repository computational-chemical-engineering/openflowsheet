### Specification (design lane `specifier`, 2026-10-22; `docs/derivations/S02-spec.md`, R-530 to R-535)

- DECISION: stage numbering and end units → top to bottom; N counts the condenser (stage 1) and reboiler (stage N); partial condenser and partial reboiler are S01 equilibrium stages with an absent inlet; the total condenser is a non-equilibrium saturated-liquid block. Alternative: condenser and reboiler outside the stage count (stages 0 and N+1). Reversible by: renumbering in the spec object only (no row changes).
- DECISION: condenser and reboiler kinds → condenser {none, partial, total (saturated)}, reboiler {none, partial}; subcooled total condenser and total reboiler are `unsupported`. Alternative: also subcooled (a `T_c − T_c*` slot) and total reboiler. Reversible by: adding a slot kind and an assertion.
- DECISION: column specifications → one slot row per non-`none` end: RR, D or Q_c at the condenser; BR, B or Q_r at the reboiler; the pair (D, B) is refused as structurally redundant; purity, recovery and temperature specifications are `unsupported`. Alternative: inverse specifications now. Reversible by: a later package adding slot kinds with their own admissibility rule.
- DECISION: ratio rows → division-free `L_1 − RR*·V_1` / `V_N − BR*·L_N`, held at their targets in the pre-solve and never continued; only `variable − parameter` slots are continued (ADR 0010 D2's precondition). Alternative: lift RR as a dimensionless variable (a `variable − parameter` row of a kind `KIND_TOLERANCE` lacks; needs a K04 tolerance decision). Reversible by: adding a dimensionless row kind by ADR and lifting the ratios.
- DECISION: pre-solve → a linear temperature profile between ADR 0012 band temperatures `T(0.1)` at `P_1*` and `T(0.9)` at `P_N*`, replacing S01's isothermal level (which puts a partial condenser at its dew point and a partial reboiler at its bubble point). Alternative: S01's isothermal pre-solve unchanged. Reversible by: changing §6 step 2 and re-emitting `ref`.
- DECISION: singular adjacent pairs → recorded as a derivation (§4.3) with generator null-vector claims and the block rule `V^a 2P^b L^c`; the phase contract is not changed in S02; P1 asserts either the closed form or a typed outcome. Alternative: an S02 signature screen around `solve_region` (a second decision function, against ADR 0005 D4). Reversible by: an ADR teaching the contract the block rule.
- DECISION: pressure profile → explicit `P_j*` per stage, non-decreasing downward, refused otherwise. Alternative: free profile (S01's). Reversible by: dropping S9.
- DECISION: ADR 0044 → not needed (additive row builders, no new row kind, `solve_region`, contract and policy `s01-default-v1` unchanged). Alternative: an ADR for the column contract. Reversible by: writing it if review disagrees.
- DECISION: S01's open questions → (a) T05b §6.6 vs v2 policies: S02 adds no policy, so the question stays S01's; (b) support-envelope L-rows for the cascade/column: left to the package that binds the column into revisions (`MODEL_BUILDERS`); S02 stays library-only like S01. Alternative: register L-rows now. Reversible by: the binding package adding them.

### WO-1 (build lane `opus-engineer`, 2026-10-22; `models/syn001/column.py`, `models/rows.py`, `tests/test_s02_column_wo1.py`)

- DECISION: where the column lives → a new unit module `models/syn001/column.py`, `MODEL_ID = "syn001.column"`, with its own `ColumnSpec`, `validate`, `declared_rows`, `regime_form` and `profile_initial`; S01's `cascade.py` is not edited. It reuses S01's id helpers, `thomas`, `EnergyPrecheck`, `CascadeFailure` and its private helpers (as `cascade_solve` does). Reason: `CascadeSpec` cannot express absent inlets (`top`, `bottom` required) and, with a total condenser, the equilibrium stages are 2..N under S01's ids, which S01's stage loop (1..N) cannot express additively. Alternative: widen `CascadeSpec.top/bottom` to optional and add a first-stage offset to S01's loops. Reversible by: folding `column.declared_rows` into `cascade.declared_rows` (row order and ids are S01's, so no id moves).
- DECISION: `ColumnSpec` shape → `duties` has one entry per stage, `None` exactly at a column-specification stage (anything else is misuse, `ValueError`, checked after S8); `EndSpecification(kind, value)`; `top`/`bottom` `None` when absent; kinds plain strings so S1/S2 refuse rather than a type check. Alternative: duties for intermediate stages only. Reversible by: changing the dataclass and `validate`'s misuse check.
- DECISION: total-condenser ids → columns `<U>.stage.1.{r,d}.<c>`, `<U>.stage.1.phi`, `<U>.stage.1.D`, and S01's `<U>.stage.1.{T,P,Q}` for `T_c, P_c, Q_c`; rows `<U>:stage:1:{bal,split}:<c>`, `<U>:stage:1:{Ddef,bubble,energy,P}`; slot rows `<U>:stage:<j>:spec:<kind>` (§2.1). Alternative: a `<U>.condenser.*` namespace. Reversible by: renaming in `column.py` (no other consumer yet).
- DECISION: pinned-input ids of slot rows → a `variable − parameter` slot pins `<column>*` (`<U>.stage.j.Q*`, `<U>.stage.1.V*` or `<U>.stage.1.D*`, `<U>.stage.N.L*`), S01's convention, so edge 3 can continue it by name; a ratio pins `<U>.stage.<j>.<kind>*`. Alternative: `<U>.spec.<end>.<kind>*` for all. Reversible by: renaming in `declared_rows`.
- DECISION: names of §3's "not offered" kinds → `temperature, purity, mole_fraction, recovery, reflux_rate, boilup_rate, distillate_to_feed_ratio` (`column.NOT_OFFERED`). Alternative: other spellings. Reversible by: editing the tuple.
- DECISION: row builders → `rows.ratio_row(terms, ratio, reference)` (`Σ terms − p·reference`, a tuple so the total condenser's `Σ r_i − RR*·D` is one builder), `rows.bubble_row(flows, ln_k_keys, total)`, and a third, `rows.fraction_row(part, fraction, whole)` for §2.2's `r_i − φ·v_i,2` (φ a column; §12 names only two builders, but no existing builder multiplies two columns linearly like this). Alternative: generalize `scaled_row` with a variable flag. Reversible by: replacing the call sites.
- DECISION: declarations → `column.EQUATIONS = S01's CAS-* families + COL-*`, and every column row's `origin` names `syn001.column`; `COL-tc-bal` is a `holdup_balance` (`N_i`) and `COL-tc-energy` a `holdup_balance` (`U`), as S01's stage rows; the slot, split, Ddef, bubble and P rows are `algebraic`. Alternative: origins under `syn001.cascade` for S01's rows. Reversible by: editing origins (identity-neutral metadata). See Q-S02-WO1-3.
- DECISION: §5 order → R1, R2, R3, R4, R6, R5 (S01's), S1 … S10, then R7 only when every slot is a duty (vacuously with no ends); an all-dormant column without ends is S01 §4.6's (R7 skipped). A03's "before any evaluation" is read as before any row, initializer, band or energy-precheck evaluation (the test stubs all four to raise); R5's admissibility call precedes S3 by §5's stated order. Alternative: S3 before R5. Reversible by: moving the S3 block.
- DECISION: §6 with N = 1 (possible only with no ends) → the profile is the single point `T_a` (the formula divides by N − 1). Alternative: refuse. Reversible by: a branch in `profile_initial`.
- DECISION: §2.3's constant label → `column.label_source` returns `ProfileLabel(1)` for stage 1 without a `top` inlet, carried in `ColumnRegimeForm.label`; WO-2 must map it (Q-S02-WO1-2). Alternative: none within the frozen `ZeroFlowForm`. Reversible by: the ruling on Q-S02-WO1-2.
- DECISION: `phi`'s quantity kind → left undeclared (`variable_kinds` lacks it), pinned by the strict xfail `test_a01_phi_is_declared_dimensionless` (Q-S02-WO1-1). Alternative: declare it `molar_flow` (wrong nominal, and a 0 lower bound by kind). Reversible by: the ruling on Q-S02-WO1-1.

### Design-lane questions (WO-1)

- QUESTION: Q-S02-WO1-1 — §0 says `φ` is "of an existing kind (`dimensionless`)", but `compile.spec.QuantityKind` is `molar_flow | molar_flow_squared | temperature | pressure | heat_rate` and `numerics.scaling.REGISTERED_NOMINALS` (SYN-001 §9, `benchmarks/registry.yaml` `scales`) has no `dimensionless`. Measured: M2's declared problem has 99 of 100 columns typed; `Scaling.from_kinds` will raise `ScaleUnavailableError` for `<U>.stage.1.phi`, so **WO-2 cannot scale M2 or V-M2** until ruled. Default I would take: add `dimensionless` to `QuantityKind` and `REGISTERED_NOMINALS` with nominal 1 (matching §10 A10's `D_x(φ) = 1`), as a column kind only (no row kind, so `KIND_TOLERANCE` is unchanged) — this edits a registered nominal table, hence the question. Needs: the ruling, and whether `registry.yaml` must carry it.
- QUESTION: Q-S02-WO1-2 — §2.3 makes stage 1's `ZERO_FLOW` label the constant `T_1⁰` when there is no `top` inlet (M1 and M4; M2's stage 2 is labelled by the column `T_c`). The orchestrator's frozen `ZeroFlowForm.label = (row, T_out, T_label)` names a column (`region._labels`, `splits.check_agreement` refuses a non-column at `splits.py:560-566`), so a constant cannot be passed. Reached only by a `ZERO_FLOW` attempt at a partial condenser (partial dormancy, unclaimed), but an attempt that proposes it would fail on a non-column label rather than end typed. Options: (a) a pinned column `<U>.stage.1.T0` with row `T0 − T_1⁰*` (additive; §3's count and `ref.dof` gain one column and row per such column); (b) a constant label in `ZeroFlowForm` (frozen interface, ADR). Default I would take: (a). WO-2 needs this before it builds `zero_flow_forms` for M1/M4.
- QUESTION: Q-S02-WO1-3 — accumulation of the total condenser's rows (ADR 0008 D3.5 territory): implemented as `COL-tc-bal` `holdup_balance N_i` and `COL-tc-energy` `holdup_balance U` (the drum holds liquid), all other S02 rows `algebraic`. Default: keep. Needs: confirmation in the package's reviewer pass.
